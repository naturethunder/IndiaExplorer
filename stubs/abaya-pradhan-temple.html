State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Thirumittakode Anchumoorthi Temple (also called Thiruviththuvakkōdu) is a Hindu temple equally associated with the deities Vishnu and Shiva. Temple is situated on the banks of the Bharathappuzha at Thirumittacode of Palakkad District in Kerala state in India. The five statues - one for Shiva and four Vishnu - are known here as Thirumattikodu five (Anchu) Moorthy Temple. As a Shiva temple, it is regarded as 108 Shivalayam of Shaivas.
