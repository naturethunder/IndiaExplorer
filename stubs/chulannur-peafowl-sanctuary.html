Chulannur Peafowl Sanctuary

State: Kerala | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Choolannur Pea Fowl Sanctuary is a bird sanctuary located in Chulanur village in Palakkad district of Kerala state, south India. The place where sanctuary is located is locally known as Mayiladumpara, which literally means "the rock where peacocks dance". As of 2022, it is the only peafowl sanctuary in Kerala.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chulanur — Chulanur is a small village in Palakkad district of Kerala state, south India. Chulanur is famous for peacocks.
  • Peringottukurissi-I — Peringottukurissi-I is a village in the Palakkad district, state of Kerala, India. It is among the villages administered by the Peringottukurissi gram panchayat.
  • Tarur-I — Tarur-I is a village in Palakkad district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Mangottu Bhagavathi Temple — Mangottu Bhagavathi Temple is in Athipotta, 24 km from Palakkad via Alathur. The Hindu goddess of the temple, Mangottu Bhagavathi, is considered the younger sister of Parakkattu Bhagwathi who has a temple in Kavasseri near by.
  • Thiruvilwamala — Thiruvilwamala is a hilly village in the northern region of Thrissur district, in the state of Kerala, southern India. It is located 47 kilometres northeast of district headquarters Thrissur.
  • Vilwadrinatha Temple — The Vilwadrinatha Temple is a Hindu temple in Thiruvilwamala, a town in the city of Thrissur, Kerala, India. The principal deities are Rama, the seventh incarnation of the god Vishnu, and his brother, Lakshmana.
