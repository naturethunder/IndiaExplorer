Jwala Ji Kangra

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

The Jwalamukhi (IAST: Jvālāmukhī) temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the goddess Jwala Devi, located in the Jawalamukhi town of Himachal Pradesh, India. It is known for its eternal flame emanating from a rock fissure on top of which the main shrine has been built. It is considered one of the major Shakti pitha shrines.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jawalamukhi — Jawalamukhi, also known as Jwalamukhi or Jawala-ji, is a temple town and a nagar parishad in Kangra district in the Indian state of Himachal Pradesh. It is the location of the Jwalamukhi temple, which is known for a naturally burning flame emanating from a rock fissure, and recognized as one of the Shakta pithas.
  • Naleti — Naleti is a small hamlet along the left bank of the Beas River. This village forms a part of Dehra subdivision of Kangra district of Himachal Pradesh, the hill state of India.
