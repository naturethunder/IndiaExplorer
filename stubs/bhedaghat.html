Bhedaghat

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: nature | Best Time: Nov – May

Bhedaghat is famous for its 100-foot towering marble rock gorge carved by the Narmada river near Jabalpur. Boating between the glowing white marble rocks — especially on full moon nights — is a magical experience.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Marble Rocks Gorge Boating — Boating between 100ft high white, pink, and blue-veined marble cliffs on Narmada river.
  • Dhuandhar Waterfalls & Cable Car — Thunderous Narmada waterfall creating a permanent cloud of mist spray with ropeway sky ride.
  • Chausath Yogini Temple Hilltop — 10th-century circular hilltop temple featuring 64 carved stone Yogini statues.
  • Balancing Rock Jabalpur — Geological marvel of a massive granite boulder balanced on a tiny rock point.
  • Madan Mahal Fort Jabalpur — 11th-century Gond ruler fort perched atop a giant granite hill outcrop.
  • Tilwara Ghat Narmada River Walk — Historic river ghat where Mahatma Gandhi's ashes were immersed in Narmada river.
