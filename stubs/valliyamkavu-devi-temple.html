Valliyamkavu Devi Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Valliyamkavu Devi Temple is a historic Durga shrine located near Travancore Rubber and Tea Company, 15 km (9.3 mi) east to 35th mile near Mundakkayam. The temple and its surroundings fall under the Peerumedu tehsil of Idukki district.
