Sreenarayanapuram Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sreenarayanapuram Mahavishnu Temple is an ancient Vishnu temple in Kerala, India. It is at Manakala (in Pathanamthitta) about 4 km (2.5 mi) from Adoor.
