Bijasan mata mandir indore

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bijasan Mata Temple is one of the temples of the Hindu goddess Durga, situated in Indore District of Madhya Pradesh, India. This temple is built in the year 1760 by Shivaji Rao Holkar. This temple is visited by thousands of devotees during Navaratri.

It operates bus services between Indore (Madhya Pradesh) and 2 other states, Rajasthan, Gujarat.

  • Lalbagh Palace — Lalbagh Palace was built by the Holkar Dynasty, and is situated in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. The Holkar family resided in the palace up to 1978.
