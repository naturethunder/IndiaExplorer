Rajgad Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct

Rajgad (literal meaning ruling fort) is a Hill region fort situated in the Pune district of Maharashtra, India. Formerly known as Murumbdev, the fort was the first capital of the Maratha Empire under the rule of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj for almost 26 years, after which the capital was moved to the Raigad Fort. Treasures discovered from an adjacent fort called Torna were used to completely build and fortify the Rajgad Fort. The Rajgad Fort is located around 60 km (37 mi) to the south-west of Pune and about 15 km (9.3 mi) west of Nasrapur in the Sahyadris range.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Torna Fort — Torna Fort, also known as Prachandagad, is a large fort located in Pune district, in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is historically significant because it was the first fort captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1646, at the age of 16.
  • Gunjwani Dam — Gunjwane Dam, is an earthfill and gravity dam on Kanandi river near Velhe, Pune district in state of Maharashtra in India.
