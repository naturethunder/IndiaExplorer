Kaila devi

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kaila Devi Temple is a Hindu temple situated in Kailadevi village of Karauli district, in the Indian state of Rajasthan.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kailadevi — Kailadevi is a village in Karauli Tehsil, in the Karauli District of the Indian state of Rajasthan. It falls under the Bharatpur Division.
  • Kailadevi Wildlife Sanctuary — Kailadevi Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected wildlife area in Karauli district of Rajasthan, India. It was notified in 1983 and covers approximately 676.38 km² area.
