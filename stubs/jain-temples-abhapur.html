Jain temples, Abhapur

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Jain temples, Abhapur is a group of twelve Śvetāmbara Jain temples located in Abhapur village near Polo Forest in Sabarkantha district of Gujarat, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Polo Forest — Polo Forest, also known as Vijaynagar Forest, is a dry mixed deciduous forest near Abhapur village in Vijaynagar Taluka, Sabarkantha district, Gujarat, India. It is located at the foothills of the Aravalli Range and on the banks of the perennial Harnav River, spread over the area of 400 square kilometres (99,000 acres).
