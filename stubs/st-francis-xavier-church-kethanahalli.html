St. Francis Xavier Church, Kethanahalli

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Saint Francis Xavier Church is a Catholic parish church around Kethanahalli village in Dharmapuri district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. The church is under the administration of Dharmapuri Diocese.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kethanahalli, Dharmapuri — Kethanahalli is a small village in Karimangalam block in Dharmapuri District of Tamil Nadu State, India. It comes under Kethanahalli Panchayath.
  • Savadiyur, Dharmapuri — Savadiyur is a village 5 km (3.1 mi) east of Palacode, Dharmapuri district, Tamil Nadu, India. This village comes under the Erraseegalahalli Gram panchayat.
  • Kariamangalam — Karimangalam is a town and taluk headquarters in Dharmapuri district in the Kongunadu region of Tamil Nadu. It is located at 20 km from Dharmapuri and 70 km from Salem.
  • Karimangalam taluk — Karimangalam taluk is a taluk in the Dharmapuri district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. On 10 February 2016, Karimangalam taluk was formed by merging parts of Palacode and Harur taluks of Dharmapuri district.
  • Christ the King Church, Palacode — Jesus the King Church in Palacode is a Roman Catholic place of worship located in the Dharmapuri district of Tamil Nadu, India. It is a Catholic church parish under the administration of the Dharmapuri Diocese.
  • Palakkodu — Palacode (also known as Palakodu or Palacode) is a panchayat town in Dharmapuri district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
