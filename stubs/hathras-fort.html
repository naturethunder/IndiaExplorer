Hathras Fort

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Hathras Fort, (also known as Raja Dayaram Fort and Shri Dauji Mandi) was constructed by Raja Dayaram Singh in the 18th century. It is located in the city of Hathras in Uttar Pradesh. Commonly known as Kila Gate, Hathras Fort is a popular tourist site, although it is not registered as a tourist place in Uttar Pradesh. The Siege of Hathras also known as the Hathras Rebellion, took place at the fort between Raja Dayaram, ruler of Hathras, and the British East India Company in February 1817.
