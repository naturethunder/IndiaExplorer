Qila Mubarak, Faridkot

State: Punjab | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Qila Mubarak, or Faridkot Fort, is a historic qila fortress located in Faridkot, Punjab, India that is spread over ten acres. It was associated with the princely-state of Faridkot. It is one of the few forts in familial possession that still survives in the region. The fortress was closed to public access for decades while its ownership was legally disputed.
