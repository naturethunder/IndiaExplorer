Kumarakom

State: Kerala | Category: beach | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Kumarakom, in Backwaters, Kerala, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its bird sanctuary, backwaters, houseboats, with the best months being Sep – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary — World-famous avian haven spanning 14 acres along Vembanad Lake, home to cormorants, egrets, herons, darter, and Siberian storks during winter months.
  • Vembanad Lake Houseboat Cruise — Cruising on traditional wooden Kettuvallam houseboats through tranquil waterways, watching rural village life and shimmering waters.
  • Pathiramanal Island — Enchanting 10-acre uninhabited island resting in the middle of Vembanad Lake, blanketed by dense tropical foliage and rare medicinal plants.
