Mandrem Beach

State: Goa | Category: beach | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Mandrem is a coastal village famous as a tourist spot in Pernem taluka in North Goa district of Goa state in India. It is 21 km from the capital Panaji.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Morjim — Morjim (pronounced Morji; the second m is silent) is a census town in Pernem, Goa, India. It is situated on the northern bank of the Chapora River estuary.
  • Querim Beach — Querim Beach is the sandy coastline of the Querim village of Pernem, Goa. It is the largest and northernmost beach in Goa.
  • Parcem — Parcem is a census town in North Goa district in the Indian state of Goa.
  • Chapora Fort — Chapora Fort (Konkani pronunciation: [ʃɑːpoːɾɑː], Devanagari: शापोरा), located in Bardez, Goa, rises high above the Chapora River.
