Songadh Fort

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Songadh fort is a 16th-century fort in Songadh town of Tapi district, Gujarat, India. The fort gots its name from the Gujarati language terms son (gold) and gadh (fort). It is located near the Tapi River Ukai Dam at an elevation of 112 meters above sea level.Thingle Sardar of Raigan Subhya of Nawapur captured this fort during the Uprising of 1857 with the Bhil army while marching towards Surat but was stopped here by the British forces.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Songadh — Songadh is a taluka in Tapi district in the Indian state of Gujarat.
  • Vekur — Vekur is a village in Tapi district of Gujarat state of India.
  • Ukai Thermal Power Station — Ukai Thermal Power Station of the Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited, India, is a power station with an installed capacity of 1,110 MW. It is one of Gujarat's major coal-fired power plants, located on the bank of the Tapi river.
  • Ukai — Ukai is a census town in Tapi district in the Indian state of Gujarat.
  • Ukai Dam — The Ukai Dam, constructed across the Tapti River, is the second largest reservoir in Gujarat, India, after the Sardar Sarovar. It is also known as Vallabh Sagar.
