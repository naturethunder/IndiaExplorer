Thalagireeshwarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Talagirisvara Temple is a Hindu temple located in the village of Panamalai in the Viluppuram district of Tamil Nadu, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mandagapattu Temple — Mandagapattu Tirumurti Temple is a Hindu temple situated in the village of Mandagapattu in the Viluppuram district of Tamil Nadu, India.   • Gingee block — The Gingee block is a revenue block in the Viluppuram district of Tamil Nadu, India. It has a total of 60 panchayat villages.
  • Gingee taluk — Gingee taluk is a taluk of Viluppuram district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. The headquarters of the taluk is the town of Gingee.
