Pandavgad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Pandavgad Fort) (also called Pandugad) (Wai T; 17° 55' N, 73° 50' E; RS. Wathar, 20 m. SE;), 4177 feet above sea level, lies four miles north-west of Wai. The fort is conspicuous over a low spur branching east from its southern angle. From a distance it appears a square fortification with natural escarpment of cut black stone.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Wai Caves — Wai Caves are 9 Buddhist Caves, situated at Lonara, 7 km north of Wai. The Chaitya hall containing a Stupa has since been converted into a Shiva temple.
  • Pasarni — Pasarni is a village in the taluka of Wai in the Satara district of Maharashtra.
  • Wai, Maharashtra — Wai (ISO: Vāī; Pronunciation: [ʋaːi] ) is a town in Satara district of Maharashtra state in India. Located on the Krishna River, Wai was a prominent town during the Peshwa era.
  • Dhom Dam — Dhom Dam is an earthfill and gravity dam on Krishna River near Wai in state of Maharashtra in India.
