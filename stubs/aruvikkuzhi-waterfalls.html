Aruvikkuzhi Waterfalls

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Aruvikuzhy Waterfalls (not to be confused with Aruvikkuzhy Falls in pathanamthitta, Kerala) is a waterfall in Kottayam district in the Kerala state of India. It is situated 2 km (1.2 mi) from Pallickathode and 7.5 km (4.7 mi) from Pampady. The waterfall measures about 30 ft in height and active only during monsoon. In summer the river almost dries up.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kooroppada — Kooroppada is a village in Kottayam district, Kerala, India, situated 16 kilometres east to Kottayam town. Kooroppada village was formed in 1953 by merging Kooroppada with a certain portion of Pampady and Anikkad villages.
  • Anicad Sree Bhagavathi Temple — Anicad Sree Bhagavathi Temple is a well-known Hindu temple of the goddess Bhadrakali located at Anicad, Kottayam, Kerala, India. The temple is located on top of a hill.
  • Anicad (Kottayam) — Anickadu (a.k.a. Anicad or Anickad) is a village in Kottayam district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Moozhayil Sankaranarayana Temple — Moozhayil Sankaranarayana Temple is a Hindu temple located at Anicad, in Kottayam, Kerala, India. This is one of the 7 Sankaranarayana temples in Kerala.
  • Pallickathodu — Pallickathodu, also spelled Pallikkathodu which includes Anickadu village, is a town in eastern Kottayam district, Kerala, India. It falls under the Kanjirappally assembly constituency.
  • Lakkattoor — Lakkattoor is a small village in Kooroppada panchayat, about 17 km east of Kottayam, Kerala located between Kottayam, Ettumanoor, Pala and Ponkunnam. Surrounded by small hills, valleys, streams and rubber trees.
