Dadra and Nagar Haveli Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Daman & Diu | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Dadra and Nagar Haveli Wildlife Sanctuary encompasses protected forested terrain across the Western Ghats foothills. Famed for its lush moist deciduous canopies, Satmaliya Deer Park, and the scenic Madhuban reservoir waters, it is a tranquil haven for birdwatching, nature trails, and wildlife exploration.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Satmaliya Deer Park & Nature Reserve — A dedicated protected enclosure within the sanctuary harboring flourishing herds of spotted deer (chital), sambar, blackbucks, and nilgai with watchtowers overlooking the scenic Madhuban reservoir.
  • Madhuban Dam & Dudhni Lake — A massive dam built across the Daman Ganga River creating a vast picturesque reservoir offering panoramic vistas of foothill forests, speedboating, and serene shoreline picnic spots.
  • Vanganga Lake Garden, Dadra — A beautifully manicured 7.5-hectare Japanese-style lake garden situated around a serene central lake with rustic wooden bridges, thatched huts, and lush jogging trails.
  • Tribal Cultural Museum, Silvassa — A distinguished cultural museum preserving the rich heritage and lifestyle of the indigenous Warli, Kokna, and Dhodia tribes, exhibiting traditional musical instruments, ornaments, Warli paintings, and hunting tools.
