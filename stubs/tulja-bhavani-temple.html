Tulja Bhavani Temple

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shree Tulaja Bhavani Temple (Marathi: श्री तुळजाभवानी मंदिर), is a 12th century CE Hindu temple dedicated to goddess Bhavani. It was built in 12th century CE by Mahamandaleshwara Māradadeva of the Kadamb dynasty. Considered as one of the 51 Shakti Pithas, it is located on the banks of Mandakini River and Bori Dam in Yamunachala Hill of Balaghat Range of Tuljapur, which is 45 km northeast of Solapur, in Dharashiv district of Maharashtra in India.
