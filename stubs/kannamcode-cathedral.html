Kannamcode Cathedral

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kannamcode Cathedral or Kannamcode St. Thomas Orthodox Cathedral is one of the oldest churches in Central Travancore. The church belongs to the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church under the Adoor Kadampanad Orthodox Diocese. Kannamcode Cathedral is situated in Adoor, Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India.
