Udaipur

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Udaipur is arguably India's most romantic city. Built around a series of artificial lakes by the Mewar Rajput dynasty, its intricate palaces, ghats, and havelis reflect beautifully in the water. The City Palace is one of the largest in Rajasthan.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • City Palace — Vast palace complex on the banks of Lake Pichola. Museum with royal artefacts.
  • Lake Pichola Boat Ride — Sunset boat ride to Jag Mandir island palace. Most romantic activity in Udaipur.
  • Jaisamand Lake — Second largest artificial lake in Asia. Hunting lodge and marble chhatris.
  • Jag Mandir Island — Island palace on Lake Pichola. Garden, café and stunning views of City Palace.
  • Saheliyon ki Bari — Garden of maidens with ornate fountains, marble elephants and lotus pools.
  • Bagore ki Haveli Museum — 18th century haveli with 100 rooms. Evening dance show with folk performances.
