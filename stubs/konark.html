Konark

State: Odisha | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Konark, in Coromandel Coast, Odisha, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its sun temple, chariot, unesco, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Konark Sun Temple — 13th-century UNESCO temple shaped as the Sun God’s chariot.
  • Chandrabhaga Beach — Tranquil beach with a sunrise viewpoint near the temple.
  • ASI Konark Museum — Sculpture fragments recovered from the Sun Temple.
