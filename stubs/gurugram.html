Gurugram

State: Haryana | Category: city | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Gurugram is modern India's corporate and entertainment hub. Beyond its soaring glass skyscrapers, Cyber Hub nightlife, and Michelin-worthy dining, Gurugram offers nature retreats like Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary, Aravali Biodiversity Park, and luxury golf resorts.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • DLF CyberHub — Premier food and nightlife destination featuring global cuisine and open plazas.
  • Sultanpur National Park — UNESCO-listed wetland sanctuary hosting over 250 species of migratory birds.
  • Kingdom of Dreams — Vibrant cultural complex showcasing live musical theater and Indian cuisine.
  • Heritage Transport Museum — India's first comprehensive museum dedicated to vintage cars, locomotives, and aviation.
  • Sheetla Mata Mandir — Revered 18th-century temple dedicated to Goddess Sheetla Devi.
  • Aravali Biodiversity Park — 380-acre restored forest park featuring native flora and walking trails.
