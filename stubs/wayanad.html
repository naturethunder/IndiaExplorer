Wayanad

State: Kerala | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – May

Wayanad, in Western Ghats, Kerala, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its caves, waterfalls, wildlife, plantations, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Edakkal Caves — Prehistoric petroglyph caves from 6,000 BCE.
  • Chembra Peak — Trek to a heart-shaped lake and Wayanad’s highest point.
  • Banasura Sagar Dam — India’s largest earthen dam with island-dotted boating.
