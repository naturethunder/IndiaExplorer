Chitkul

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct

Chitkul, in Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its last indian village, baspa river, apple orchards, with the best months being Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Last Village of India — India’s last inhabited village before the Tibet border.
  • Baspa Riverside — Pristine river meadows framed by snow peaks.
  • Mathi Temple — Ancient wooden village temple of local goddess Mathi.
