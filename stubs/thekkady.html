Thekkady

State: Kerala | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Thekkady, in Idukki, Kerala, is one of India’s favourite wildlife reserve. Visitors come for its periyar tiger reserve, spice gardens, boating, with the best months being Sep – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Periyar Lake Boat Safari — Boat cruise to spot elephants, bison and birds at the waterline.
  • Spice Plantation Tour — Guided walk through cardamom, pepper and clove gardens.
  • Periyar Nature Walk — Guided forest trek with armed naturalist in the reserve.
