Kalamassery Mahaganapathy Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kalamassery Mahaganapathy Temple (Malayalam: കളമശ്ശേരി മഹാഗണപതി ക്ഷേത്രം) is a Hindu temple in North Kalamassery dedicated to Ganapathy (Ganesha).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kalamassery metro station — Kalamassery is a station of Kochi Metro. The station is located between Muttom and Cochin University, in the municipality of Kalamassery.
  • St. Thomas Church, Kalamassery — St. Thomas Marthoma Church Kalamassery, is a prominent Christian parish of the Kottayam – Kochi diocese under the Malankara Mar Thoma Syrian Church of Malabar.
  • Rajagiri — Rajagiri is a part of the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI), an indigenous Syrian Catholic religious congregation founded in 1831. It is located in Rajagiri Valley, Kakkanad, Kochi.
  • Kalamassery — Kalamassery ([kɐɭɐmɐʃːeːɾi]) is a major industrial region in the city of Kochi in the state of Kerala, India. It is located around 9 km (5.6 mi) northeast of the city centre.
  • Muttom metro station — Muttom is a station of Kochi Metro. The station is located between Ambattukavu and Kalamassery.
  • St. Joseph's of the Woods EMHS — St. Joseph's of the Woods English-Medium Higher Secondary School is a Catholic Co-Educational higher secondary school in Thrikkakkara (Kalamassery), Kochi in the Indian state of Kerala.
