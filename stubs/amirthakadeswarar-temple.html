Amirthakadeswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Mela Kadambur Amirthakadeswarar Temple (மேலக்கடம்பூர் அமிர்தகடேஸ்வரர் கோயில்) (Sanskrit: Amruta: nectar Ghata: pot Iswara: God) is a Hindu temple located at Melakadambur in Cuddalore district of Tamil Nadu, India. The historical name of the place is Tirukadambur. The presiding deity is Shiva. He is called as Amirtha Kadeswarar.
