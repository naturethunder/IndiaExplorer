St. Mary's Cathedral, Ranchi

State: Jharkhand | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. Mary's Cathedral, situated along Dr. Camil Bulcke Path (Purulia Road) in the heart of Ranchi, Jharkhand, is the seat of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Ranchi. Consecrated in 1909, the cathedral is acclaimed for its Gothic Revival architecture, twin soaring bell spires, high rib-vaulted nave, and luminous stained glass windows. As the spiritual heart of the Catholic faith in the Chota Nagpur plateau, it stands as an enduring architectural and historical monument amidst peaceful tree-lined grounds.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Archdiocese Heritage Campus & Presbytery — The surrounding Archbishop's House and colonial presbytery campus features early 20th-century stone buildings, educational institutions, and tranquil tree-lined walkways on Purulia Road.
  • Ranchi Lake & Swami Vivekananda Memorial — Excavated in 1842 by British Colonel Onslow at the foot of Ranchi Hill, Ranchi Lake features an immense 33-foot bronze statue of Swami Vivekananda situated on a central island accessible by bridge.
  • Tagore Hill (Morabadi) — Rising 300 feet above the Morabadi plains, Tagore Hill was the tranquil retreat of Jyotirindranath Tagore (elder brother of Rabindranath Tagore). The hilltop pavilion offers sweeping views of Ranchi city.
  • Jagannath Temple, Dhurwa — Built in 1691 by King Ani Nath Shahdeo atop a hillock in Dhurwa, this 17th-century Jagannath Temple mirrors the architectural design of the famous Puri temple and hosts the annual Rath Yatra festival.
  • Pahari Mandir (Ranchi Hill) — Perched 2,140 feet above sea level atop Phansi Tongri (Ranchi Hill), Pahari Mandir is dedicated to Lord Shiva. Climbing its 468 stone steps rewards pilgrims with 360-degree vistas across Ranchi.
  • GEL Church (Established 1845) — The Gossner Evangelical Lutheran (GEL) Church on Main Road is the oldest church in eastern India's Chota Nagpur region, founded in 1845. It features historic stone arches and a prominent bell tower.
