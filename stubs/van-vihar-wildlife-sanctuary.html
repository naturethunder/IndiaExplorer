Van Vihar Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Rajasthan | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Van Vihar Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located in the Dholpur district of Rajasthan, India. Established in 1955, it spans approximately 26.6 square kilometers and is one of the oldest wildlife reserves in the region. The sanctuary is located about 16 kilometers from Dholpur city on the Vindhya Plateau.
