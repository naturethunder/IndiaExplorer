Neemrana

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Sept – Mar

Neemrana is an ancient historical town in Rajasthan's Alwar district, located just 120 km from New Delhi. Built in 1464 AD, the 14-tiered Neemrana Fort Palace carved into the Aravalli hills is one of India's premier heritage luxury resorts.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Neemrana Fort Palace — 15th-century 14-tiered hill fort featuring hanging gardens, pools, and ziplining.
  • Neemrana Baori — 9-storey deep subterranean stepwell built in the 18th century for royal caravans.
  • Siliserh Lake Palace — Picturesque 19th-century royal hunting lodge located on Siliserh Lake.
  • Hill Fort Kesroli — One of the oldest heritage forts in Rajasthan with panoramic countryside views.
