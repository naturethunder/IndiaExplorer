Nakoda

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shri Nakoda Ji, formally known as Nakoda Parshvanatha Tirth, is a world-renowned Jain pilgrimage shrine located near Mewanagar and Balotra in Barmer district, Rajasthan. Dating back to the 3rd century with major historical consecrations, the sacred complex is dedicated to the 23rd Tirthankara, Lord Parshvanatha, and Nakoda Bhairav. Famed for its pristine white marble architecture, intricately sculpted toranas, gilded ceilings, and peaceful desert setting amidst the surrounding hills, Nakoda draws pilgrims from across India seeking spiritual blessings.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Balotra Heritage & Textile Town — Balotra is a vibrant historical city on the right bank of the Luni River, famed for its centuries-old hand-block printing, vibrant textile craft markets, and authentic Marwari hospitality.
  • Khed Brahmadham & Ranchhodraiji Temple — Khed was the historic first capital of the Rathore dynasty in Marwar. It houses the ancient Ranchhodraiji Vishnu Temple and the revered Brahma Temple along the sacred Luni riverbanks.
  • Mewanagar Hill Shrines — Mewanagar, the historic town adjoining Nakoda, is cradled by rugged Aravalli ridges featuring panoramic viewpoints, ancient meditation caves, and historic step-paths climbed by generations of ascetics.
  • Bithuja Dham & Luni River Basin — Bithuja Dham is a venerated pilgrimage sanctuary dedicated to Baba Ramdevji on the banks of the Luni River. Devotees visit for the holy darshan, vast open courtyards, and peaceful riverside atmosphere.
  • Jasol Majisa Mata Temple — Jasol is the seat of the revered Mata Rani Bhatiyaniji Temple (Majisa), one of Western Rajasthan's most sacred folk shrines. The grand temple features ornate domes, vibrant prayer halls, and spiritual energy.
  • Siwana Fort & Aravalli Ridges — Perched atop a rugged hill, the historic 10th-century Siwana Fort was built by the Parmara dynasty and served as a legendary stronghold for Rathore heroes during imperial sieges. It offers breathtaking panoramic views.
