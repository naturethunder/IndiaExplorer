Rajrappa

State: Jharkhand | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Rajrappa is a waterfall and a pilgrimage centre in the Chitarpur CD block in the Ramgarh subdivision of the Ramgarh district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chhinnamasta Temple, Rajrappa — Chhinnamastika Temple is a Hindu temple, dedicated to Goddess Chinnamasta is a Hindu pilgrimage centre located in Rajrappa, in Ramgarh district of Jharkhand, India. The place attracts devotees from all parts of Jharkhand, and also from the neighbouring states of West Bengal and Bihar.
  • Sewai — Sewai is a census town in the Chitarpur (community development block) CD block in the Ramgarh subdivision of the Ramgarh district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
  • Ramgarh Coalfield — Ramgarh Coalfield are located in Ramgarh district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
  • Rajrappa Area — Rajrappa Area is one of the operational areas of the Central Coalfields Limited located in the Ramgarh district in the state of Jharkhand, India. The projects of the Rajrappa Area are: Rajrappa OCP and Rajrappa Washery.
  • Chitarpur — Chitarpur (also written as Chitar Pur) is a census town in the Chitarpur CD block in the Ramgarh subdivision of the Ramgarh district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
  • Chitarpur (community development block) — Chitarpur (community development block) is an administrative division in the Ramgarh subdivision of the Ramgarh district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
