Capela de Nossa Senhora da Saúde

State: Goa | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Capela de Nossa Senhora da Saúde or the Chapel of Our Lady of Health, is a Roman Catholic chapel on the island of Chorão in Goa, India. It was originally built in 1705.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chapel of Sacra Familia (Chorão Island) — The Capela da Sagrada Família (Chapel of the Holy Family) is a Roman Catholic chapel built in 1769 on the island of Chorão, Goa.
  • Epidemics in Chorão Island — The Chorão Epidemic killed over a thousand people in Chorão, an island along the Mandovi River near Ilhas, Goa, India. It also caused thousands to flee the island, and led to the closure of Real Colégio de Educação de Chorão in 1859.
  • Chorão (island) — Chorão (formerly known as Choddnnem) is an island along the Mandovi River near Tiswadi, Goa, India. It is the largest among other 17 islands of Goa.
