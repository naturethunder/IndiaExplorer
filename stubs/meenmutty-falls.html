Meenmutty Falls

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Meenmutty WaterFall is situated in Padinjarathara in Wayanad District, Kerala. It is the largest waterfall in Wayanad. It is a three-tiered waterfall with a height of 1000 feet and it is on Banasura hills. It can be accessed from Padinjarathara ;.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Banasura Hill — Banasura Hill is situated in Padinjarathara and it is one of the tallest mountains in the Western Ghats of the Wayanad district, Kerala, India. The hill is named after Banasura, a mythical character of Indian legends.
  • Valat — Valat is a village in the Wayanad district of the state of Kerala, India. The village is situated near to Kannur district.
  • Thondernad — Thondernad is a village in Wayanad district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Padinjarathara — Padinjarathara is a major town in Wayanad district in the state of Kerala, India. The largest earth dam in India, Banasura Sagar and other main tourist attractions like Meenmutti Waterfalls, Bappanam Mala, Banasura Hill and Kurumbalakotta are situated in Padinjarathara.
  • Poozhithodu Weir — Poozhithodu Weir is a small diversion dam in Kavilumpara panchayath in Maruthomkara village of Kozhikode district in Kerala, India. This project is a run – off the river scheme utilizing water from Illiani puzha and Kadanthrapuzha .
  • St. George Forane Church Kallody, Wayanad — St. George Forane Church Kallody is situated in Mananthavady taluk of Wayanad district.
