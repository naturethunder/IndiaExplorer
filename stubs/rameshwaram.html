Rameshwaram

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Apr

Rameshwaram, in Pamban Island, Tamil Nadu, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its ramanathaswamy, pamban bridge, dhanushkodi, with the best months being Oct – Apr.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ramanathaswamy Temple — Char Dham temple with India’s longest pillared corridor.
  • Dhanushkodi — Ghost town at land’s end where two seas meet.
  • Pamban Bridge — India’s first sea bridge, with a scenic train crossing.
