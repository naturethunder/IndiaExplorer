Subramaniya Swamy Temple, Tiruchendur

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Subramanya Swamy Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Hindu god Murugan, located in Thoothukudi district, Tamil Nadu. It is one of the Six Abodes of Murugan (Arupadai Veedu), a set of foremost and sacred Hindu temples, dedicated to Murugan. It is also one of the Vaippu Sthalams. The temple complex is located in the eastern end of the town on the shores of the Bay of Bengal.
