Chilkur Balaji Temple

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Chilkur Balaji Temple is an ancient Hindu temple of Lord Balaji on the banks of Osman Sagar in Rangareddy District in Telangana. It is one of the oldest temples in the Hyderabad Dist earlier now in Rangareddy Dist, built during the time of Madanna and Akkanna, the uncles of Bhakta Ramadas. C.S. Rangarajan is the current head priest of the Chilkur Balaji Devasthanam. The temple has no hundi and does not accept any money from devotees.
