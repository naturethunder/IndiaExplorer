Dhanushkodi

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Apr

Dhanushkodi is a legendary abandoned coastal town situated on the narrow eastern tip of Pamban Island in Tamil Nadu. Flanked by the serene waters of the Bay of Bengal (Mahodadhi) on one side and the turbulent Indian Ocean (Ratnakara) on the other, Dhanushkodi is celebrated for Arichal Munai—the closest point to Sri Lanka—and the hauntingly picturesque ruins of its 1964 cyclone-destroyed church and railway terminus.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Arichal Munai (Land's End Tip) — The southernmost point of Rameswaram island crowned with the Ashoka Pillar emblem, where roads end and endless ocean horizons begin.
  • Dhanushkodi Ghost Town Ruins — Haunting brick ruins of the historic town submerged and abandoned during the catastrophic 1964 cyclone, including the ruined church, school, and railway station.
  • Pamban Sea Bridge — Historic 1914 railway cantilever bridge and twin road bridge spanning 2.06 km across the turquoise sea channel connecting Mandapam to Rameswaram island.
