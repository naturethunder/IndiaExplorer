Langshiang Falls

State: Meghalaya | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Langshiang Falls is located near the village of Sangriang, 24 kilometres (15 mi) from Nongstoin, in West Khasi Hills district in the Indian state of Meghalaya. It can also be seen from Mawpon village. The total height of the falls is generally quoted at around 337 metres (1,106 ft). However, that needs to be confirmed.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nongkhnum River Island — The second largest river island in Asia, featuring sandy beaches, wooden footbridges, and roaring river rapids.
  • Nongstoin Pine Hills — District headquarters surrounded by lush Khasi pine forests, rolling highland hills, and scenic mountain trails.
  • Kynshi River Rapids — Mighty river rapids cascading through steep granite canyons above the majestic Langshiang Falls.
