Nuranang Falls

State: Arunachal Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Formed by the pristine waters of the Nuranang River cascading into the roaring Jung River, Nuranang Falls is one of the most stunning waterfalls in Northeast India. Fed by glacial streams from Sela Pass, the falls feature a thunderous drop amidst lush alpine forests, creating iridescent mists and rainbows against the dramatic Himalayan mountain backdrop.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jang Town — Jang is a tourist destination, town and subdivision in Jang Valley in Tawang district of Arunachal Pradesh state of India. It lies on NH-13 Trans-Arunachal Highway 40 km southwest of Tawang and north of Sela Tunnel/Sela Pass.
  • Lhau — Lhau is an idyllic highland Monpa village near Jang in the Tawang valley, known for its traditional stone-and-timber houses, fluttering prayer flag trails, and panoramic views of the forested river canyon.
  • Tsona Chu — The pristine Tsona Chu River is a fast-flowing glacial stream carving through rugged mountain gorges near the Tawang border, feeding into the roaring cascades of the Nuranang River system.
