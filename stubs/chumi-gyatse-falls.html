Chumi Gyatse Falls

State: Arunachal Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Revered in Tibetan Buddhist tradition as the blessings of Guru Padmasambhava, Chumi Gyatse Falls comprises 108 pristine natural waterfalls that cascade down sheer rock cliffs into a scenic mountain gorge in the Yangtse sector of Tawang. Surrounded by vibrant prayer flags and alpine wilderness, this sacred site offers dramatic highland scenery, misty mountain vistas, and serene nature trails along the high-altitude frontier.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Yangtse Mountain Ridge — The dramatic high-altitude Yangtse Mountain Ridge rises majestically above the Tawang frontier, offering breathtaking panoramic vistas of Eastern Himalayan snow peaks, glacial canyons, and pristine mountain wilderness.
