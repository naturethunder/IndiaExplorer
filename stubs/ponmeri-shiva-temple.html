Ponmeri Shiva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Ponmeri Shiva Temple is a famous Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva in Vatakara, Kozhikode in Kerala, India. The temple is famous as it has a unique sub-temple dedicated to Lord Brahma, who is rarely worshipped at temples.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Villiappally — Villiappally is a census town in Kozhikode district in the Indian state of Kerala. Villiappally is the headquarters of Villiappally Panchayath, which contains two villages, Memunda and Villiappally.
  • Karthikappalli — Karthikappalli is a small village in Kozhikode district in the state of Kerala, India. The population was about 19,021 people according to the 2011 Census of India.
  • Memunda — Memunda is a village near Vatakara town in Kozhikode district of Kerala, south India. The Lokanarkavu Temple is located at Memunda.
  • Lokanarkavu Temple — Lokanarkavu Temple is an ancient Hindu temple dedicated to goddess Durga devi situated in Memunda, 4 km from Vatakara, in Kozhikode District, North Malabar region of Kerala state of south India. Lokanarkavu is a short form of Lokamalayarkavu which means lokam (world) made of mala (mountain), aaru (river) and kavu (grove).
  • Ayancheri — Ayancheri is a village in the Vadakara Taluk of Kozhikode district in the state of Kerala, India. It has now become a small business hub among the nearest villages.
  • Katameri — Katameri (also spelled Kadameri) is a small village near Vatakara, Kozhikode district in Kerala, India. It is around 14 km east from Vatakara.
