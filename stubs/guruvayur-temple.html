Guruvayur Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Guruvayur Sri Krishna Temple is situated in the town of Guruvayur, Thrissur district, Kerala, India. Located approximately 26 kilometers (16 miles) northwest of Thrissur city, the temple stands as one of the most revered and actively visited Hindu pilgrimage sites globally. Dedicated to the deity Guruvayurappan, a beloved form of Vishnu, the temple is affectionately hailed by devotees as Bhuloka Vaikunta—the Holy Abode of Vishnu on Earth. In terms of foot traffic, it is one of the busiest Vaishnava shrines in the country.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mammiyur Mahadeva Temple — Ancient Shiva temple with exquisite mural paintings and separate sanctums for Mahavishnu, Ganapathi, and Subramanya, traditionally visited alongside Guruvayur.
  • Punnathur Kotta (Anakkotta Elephant Sanctuary) — Historic 10-acre palace estate of Punnathur Raja converted into a sanctuary housing over 50 elephants dedicated to temple service.
  • Chavakkad Beach & Estuary — Serene coastal beach where the Chettuva backwater estuary meets the Arabian Sea, featuring coconut groves and calm golden sands.
  • Palayur Historic Syrian Church — Historic church founded by St. Thomas the Apostle in 52 AD, displaying centuries-old granite stonework, copper bell towers, and sacred Thaliyakulam pond.
