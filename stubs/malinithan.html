Malinithan

State: Arunachal Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Malinithan is an archaeological site containing the ruins of an early medieval period temple on the northern bank of the Brahmaputra River in the Indian state of Arunachal Pradesh. It is generally attributed to the Chutia kings in the 13th-14th century. Art-historical study of the iconography and development of Indian temple art suggests that the cultural period represented by the Malinithan remains extended from about the 10th to the 14th century. The scale of its stone architecture has also been taken to indicate construction under a prosperous polity before the Ahoms, with its latest phase
