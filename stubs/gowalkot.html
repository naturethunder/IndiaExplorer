Gowalkot

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Gowalkot is a small fort located on the southern bank of Vashishti River, about 10.0 km (6.2 miles) from Chiplun in Maharashtra, India. This fort is guarded by the river on three sides and a trench on the fourth side. Its population which includes Hindus, Muslims, and Buddhists. According to old natives of Gowalkot, the history of Gowalkot goes back to a King, who was Hindu and finally was converted in Muslim, his surname was "Chougle", and most of the land in Gowalkot is owned by the Chougle family.
