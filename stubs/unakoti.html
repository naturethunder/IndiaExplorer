Unakoti

State: Tripura | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Apr

Unakoti, meaning 'one less than a crore', is an ancient pilgrimage site hidden in the green hills of Tripura. Featuring colossal 30-foot rock carvings of Lord Shiva, Ganesha, and Durga carved into vertical rock walls.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Unakotiswara Kal Bhairav Head Relief — Colossal 30ft rock-cut bas-relief Shiva head carved into limestone cliff face.
  • Ganesha Kingdom Rock Reliefs — Triple Ganesha rock reliefs and Chaturmukha Linga carved into rock walls.
  • Unakoti Sacred Spring (Sitaram Kunda) — Natural jungle stream flowing past ancient stone rock sculptures.
  • Jampui Hills Orange Orchards — Misty hill station famed for orange orchards and Mizo tribal villages.
  • Manu Valley Tea Gardens — Rolling green tea gardens near Kailashahar town.
  • Kailashahar Royal Palace Ruins — Former royal capital of Tripura kings featuring colonial rajbari architecture.
