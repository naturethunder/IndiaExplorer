Tirupati

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Tirupati, in Tirumala Hills, Andhra Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its balaji temple, pilgrimage, hills, with the best months being Sep – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sri Venkateswara Temple — World’s most-visited temple atop the seven Tirumala hills.
  • Sri Kalahasti Temple — Ancient Vayu (wind) element Shiva temple.
  • Chandragiri Fort — 11th-century fort and palace of the Vijayanagara kings.
