Basilica of Our Lady of the Holy Rosary, Karumathampatti

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Basilica of Our Lady of the Holy Rosary, located at Karumathampatti in Tamil Nadu, is a prominent Catholic shrine in the Roman Diocese of Coimbatore. The church is dedicated to Our Lady of the Holy Rosary, and its annual feast is celebrated on the first Sunday of October every year, attracting pilgrims from across the region.
