Tranquebar (Tharangambadi)

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Tharangambadi (Tranquebar) served as a Danish trading colony from 1620 to 1845. Dominating the shoreline is Fort Dansborg, a yellow-painted sea fort. The quiet cobblestone streets feature Danish colonial bungalows.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Fort Dansborg — Iconic 1620 Danish fort overlooking the Bay of Bengal with two-tier barracks, governor's chambers, and archeological museum.
  • New Jerusalem Church & Zion Church — India's first Protestant church consecrated in 1718 by royal Danish missionary Bartholomaeus Ziegenbalg, featuring cross-shaped architecture.
  • Town Gate (Landporten) — Arched colonial entrance gate built in 1792 bearing the royal Danish monogram of King Christian VII, marking the boundary of the fortified town.
  • Masilamani Nathar Temple — Built in 1306 AD by Pandyan King Maravarman Kulasekara Pandyan I, this coastal Shiva shrine showcases unique monolithic Dravidian stonework.
