Madhukesvara temple

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Banavasi Madhukeshwara Temple is an ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva. It is located in Banavasi, which is in Karnataka State of India, 24 km (15 mi) away from the nearest city, Sirsi.
