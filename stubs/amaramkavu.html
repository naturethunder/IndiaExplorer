Amaramkavu

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Amaramkavu is a sacred grove and Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess VanaDurga, situated in Kolani, Thodupuzha Municipality, Idukki district, Kerala. Amaramkavu is the largest sacred grove in the Idukki district. Amaramkavu has an area near to 3 acres and is rich in biodiversity.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Thodupuzha — Thodupuzha ([kaʈ:ap:ɐna]) is a municipality and taluk in the Idukki district of Kerala, India. It is located 55 km (34.2 mi) west of the district headquarters in Painavu and about 195 km (121.2 mi) north of the state capital Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Kalayanthani — Kalayanthani is a small town in Thodupuzha Taluk. It lies in the Idukki district of Kerala, India.
  • Perumpillichira — Perumpillichira is a small village that comes under the Panchayat of Kumaramangalam in Idukki district in the Indian state of Kerala. The name ‘Perumpillichira’ comes from the name of a large water reservoir adjacent to a Hindu temple at the heart of the village.
  • Ilaveezha Poonchira — Ilaveezhapoonchira is a tourist destination located in Melukavu village in Kottayam district near Kanjar. Ilaveezha Poonchira is surrounded by three enchanting hillocks - Mankunnu, Kodayathoormala and Thonippara.
  • Karikkode Bhagavathy Temple — Karikkode Bhagavathy Temple (Malayalam: കാരിക്കോട് ഭഗവതി ക്ഷേത്രം) is a Hindu temple located in the village of Karikkode near Thodupuzha in Idukki district in the Indian state of Kerala. The structure is believed to be 460 years old.
  • Vandamattom — Vandamattom is a village in Kodikulam Panchayath, Thodupuzha Taluk, Idukki district, Kerala, India.
