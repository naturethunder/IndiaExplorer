Vasudeva Doul

State: Assam | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Vasudev Doul (also spelled Vasudeva Doul or Basudev Dol) is a historic Vaishnavite temple located at Barachuk in the Kalabari area of Gohpur subdivision in Biswanath district of Assam, India. The temple is dedicated to Vāsudeva, a form of Vishnu, and represents an important example of late medieval Ahom architecture. The monument is listed among the protected archaeological sites of Assam under the Directorate of Archaeology, Government of Assam. It is also recorded as a protected monument under the Archaeological Survey of India.
