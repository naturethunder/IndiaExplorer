Rajbari National Park

State: Tripura | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Rajbari (Bison) National Park is a national park in the Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary, in Tripura, India. It covers an area of about 31.63 square kilometres (12.21 sq mi).
