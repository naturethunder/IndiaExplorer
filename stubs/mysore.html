Mysore

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mysore, in Deccan Plateau, Karnataka, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its palace, dasara, chamundi hill, silk, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mysore Palace — Indo-Saracenic royal palace, dazzling when lit by 100,000 bulbs.
  • Chamundi Hill Temple — Hilltop temple of the city’s patron goddess, reached by 1,000 steps.
  • Brindavan Gardens — Terraced gardens with a musical fountain below the KRS dam.
