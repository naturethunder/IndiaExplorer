Satkosia Tiger Reserve

State: Odisha | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Satkosia Tiger Reserve is a tiger reserve located in the Angul and Nayagarh district of Odisha, India covering an area of 963.87 km2. It was declared a wildlife sanctuary in 1976 and a tiger reserve in 2007.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Satkosia Gorge — Satkosia Gorge is a gorge in eastern Odisha, India, carved by the Mahanadi River. The gorge is located within the Satkosia Tiger Reserve which is a United nations Protected area.
