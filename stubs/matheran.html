Matheran

State: Maharashtra | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – May

Matheran, in Sahyadri, Maharashtra, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its vehicle-free, toy train, viewpoints, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Panorama Point — 360° viewpoint famed for sunrise over the Sahyadris.
  • Matheran Toy Train — Narrow-gauge heritage train winding up from Neral.
  • Charlotte Lake — Quiet lake supplying the town, with a lakeside Shiva temple.
