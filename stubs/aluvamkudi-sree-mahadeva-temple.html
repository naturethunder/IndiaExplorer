Aluvamkudi Sree Mahadeva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Aluvamkudi Sree Mahadeva Temple is an old Hindu temple situated in the mountainous forests of Pathanamthitta district, Kerala, India. The temple dedicated to Shiva is said to have been created by Parashurama. It was found during a Devaprashnam (astrological ritual) that Rama had conducted poojas in this temple. The archaeological department has stated that the temple is approximately more than 2000 years old.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Veluthodu Dam — Veluthodu dam (Malayalam: വെളുത്തോട് അണക്കെട്ട് ) is a part of Kakkad Hydro Electric Project and is located in Seethathode panchayath of Ranni Taluk in Pathanamthitta District of Kerala, India. It's a Concrete-Gravity dam built across the Veluthodu river, a tributary of Kakkad River which is again a tributary of Pamba River.
  • Vayyattupuzha — Vayyattupuzha is a village situated near to Chittar in Pathanamthitta district, Kerala. It is predominantly a rural hilly area but is very famous for spice trade.
  • Seethathodu — Seethathodu is a village in the Konni taluk, Pathanamthitta district, state of Kerala, India, near Chittar Town. Predominantly, it is a rural region with agriculture being the most important sector.
  • Adavi, Kerala — Adavi is a major tourist destination in Konni, Kerala, situated in the banks of Kallar river. Eco-tourism project in Adavi is jointly launched by Kerala Tourism Development Corporation and Department of Forests and Wildlife, Kerala.
  • Moozhiyar Dam — Moozhiyar Dam is a gravity dam located close to Kakki reservoir in Seethathodu panchayath of Pathanamthitta district in Kerala, India. The Kakkad Hydroelectric Project is powered by water from the dam.
