Ashtamangalam Siva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Ashtamangalam Siva Temple is located in the city of Thrissur at Ashtamangalam in Thrissur district. The presiding deity of the temple is Shiva, located in separate sanctum sanatoriums, facing east. It is believed that this temple is one of the 108 Shiva temples of Kerala and is installed by sage Parasurama dedicated to Shiva. The temple is located near Karyattukara on the Laloor road.

  • Kanattukara — Kanattukara is a ward and residential area in Thrissur, Kerala, India. It is home to the Sankarankulangara Bhagavathy Temple.
  • Ayyanthole Karthyayani Temple — Ayyanthole Karthyayani Bhagavathy Temple is a Hindu temple situated in Ayyanthole, Thrissur city of Kerala, India. Cochin Devaswom Board controls the temple.
  • Olarikara — Olarikkara, usually called Olari, is an important gateway to Thrissur city of Kerala state, South India. It is a major residential area in the city.
