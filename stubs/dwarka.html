Dwarka

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Dwarka, in Saurashtra, Gujarat, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its char dham, krishna, coast, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Dwarkadhish Temple — 5-storey Char Dham temple to Lord Krishna on the Gomti.
  • Bet Dwarka — Island shrine reached by ferry, Krishna’s legendary residence.
  • Nageshwar Jyotirlinga — One of the twelve sacred Jyotirlinga Shiva shrines.
