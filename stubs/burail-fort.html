Burail Fort

State: Chandigarh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Burail Fort is a fort situated in Sector 45 of Chandigarh, India (presently).It Is firstly Constructed by Raghuvanshi Rajputs in 12th Century. It was built during the Mughal period.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Terraced Garden, Chandigarh — The Terraced Garden is a public garden in the city of Chandigarh, India. Established in 1979, it spans an area of approximately 10 acres (4.0 hectares) and is known for its distinctive terraced layout.
  • Model Jail, Chandigarh — Model Jail is a prison located in the Union Territory of Chandigarh, India. It is a Central Jail established in 1972.
  • Japanese Garden, Chandigarh — The Japanese Garden is a park located in Sector 31 in the Union Territory of Chandigarh. Built in 2014 on 13 acres of land by the Indian Government, it was inaugurated by Shivraj Patil on 7 November 2014.
  • Parrot Bird Sanctuary, Chandigarh — City Bird Sanctuary Chandigarh or Parrot Bird Sanctuary Chandigarh is a bird sanctuary situated in sector 21 of the city of Chandigarh, India. It is primarily habitat of parrots.
  • Sanitary Garden — The Sanitary Garden or Kala Sagar (lit. 'ocean of art') is an art exhibition in Chandigarh, India consisting of sculptures made from toilets and other sanitary items. The garden is a unique government museum where the world's largest collection of art created purely from damaged or discarded sanitary items is exhibited.
  • Chandigarh Hockey Stadium — The Chandigarh Hockey Stadium is a field hockey stadium at Chandigarh, India. It was the home of the Chandigarh Dynamos of the Premier Hockey League.
