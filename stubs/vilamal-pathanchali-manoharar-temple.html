Vilamal Pathanchali Manoharar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Vilamal Pathanchali Manoharar Temple ( Tamil: விளமல் பதஞ்சலி மனோகரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located at Vilamal in Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, India. The temple is dedicated to Shiva, as the moolavar presiding deity, in his manifestation as Pathanchali Manoharar. His consort, Parvati, is known as Yazhinum Menmozhiyaal.
