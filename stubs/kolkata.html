Kolkata

State: West Bengal | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kolkata, nestled on the eastern bank of the sacred Hooghly River in West Bengal, is India's historic capital and artistic nerve centre. From the regal British-era white marble splendour of the Victoria Memorial to the engineering marvel of Howrah Bridge, spiritual solace at Dakshineswar Kali Temple, and iconic yellow Ambassador taxis bustling down wide tree-lined boulevards, Kolkata charms travellers with its unmatched intellectual and festive soul.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Victoria Memorial — Grand Indo-Saracenic white Makrana marble palace commemorating Queen Victoria, surrounded by 64 acres of royal landscaped gardens, reflection pools, and 25 galleries of rare art and colonial artifacts.
  • Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu) — World-famous suspended balance cantilever steel bridge engineered without nuts or bolts over the Hooghly River, carrying over 100,000 vehicles and 150,000 pedestrians daily.
  • Dakshineswar Kali Temple — Venerated 19th-century Navaratna nine-spire temple on the Hooghly riverbank founded by Rani Rashmoni, sacred for its association with mystic saint Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa and 12 riverside Shiva shrines.
