Burhi Gosani Devalaya Jorhat

State: Assam | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Burhi Gosani Devalaya is a Hindu shakta temple which was originally used to worship the primordial deity Burhi and was later sanskritised to goddess Durga. It is situated at Dewal Road in Jorhat, Assam, India.
