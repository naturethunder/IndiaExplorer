Ninra Narayana Perumal temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Ninra Narayana Perumal Temple (also called Nindra Narayana Perumal Temple) in Thiruthangal, a neighborhood of Sivakasi City Municipal Corporation in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu, is dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu. Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, the temple is glorified in the Divya Prabandha, the early medieval Tamil canon of the Alvar saints from the 6th–9th centuries AD.
