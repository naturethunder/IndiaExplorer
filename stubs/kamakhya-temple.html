Kamakhya Temple

State: Assam | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kamakhya Temple (Assamese: kamakhya mondir) is a Hindu temple at Nilachal hills in Guwahati, Assam is one of the oldest and most revered centres of Tantric practices, dedicated to the goddess Kamakhya. The temple is the center of the Kulachara Tantra Marga and the site of the Ambubachi Mela, an annual festival that celebrates the menstruation of the goddess. Structurally, the temple is dated to the 8th-9th century with many subsequent rebuildings—and the final hybrid architecture defines a local style called Nilachal. It is also one among the oldest 4 of the 51 pithas in the Shakta tradition.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kamakhya, Guwahati — Kamakhya is the sacred temple precinct crowning Nilachal Hill, revered as one of the oldest of the 51 Shakti Peethas with breathtaking panoramic vistas over Guwahati and the Brahmaputra River.
  • Maligaon — Located at the northern foothills of Nilachal Hill, Maligaon is a historic railway township serving as the headquarters of the Northeast Frontier Railway, nestled among lush subtropical hills.
  • Northeast Frontier Railway Stadium — The Northeast Frontier Railway Stadium in Maligaon is a premier sporting venue hosting regional cricket and athletics, framed by the picturesque green ridges of Kamrup.
  • Kendriya Vidyalaya Maligaon — A prominent educational institution situated in the serene railway colony of Maligaon, bordered by the lush evergreen canopies and gentle hillslopes of Nilachal.
  • Silagrant — Silagrant is a tranquil riparian locality situated along the mighty Brahmaputra river corridor near Guwahati, renowned for sweeping riverbank vistas, sandbars, and dramatic sunsets.
  • Atal Udyan — Atal Udyan is an expansive riverside public park and promenade in Guwahati, offering manicured gardens, open green lawns, and panoramic viewpoints overlooking the Brahmaputra.
