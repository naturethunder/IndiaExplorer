Vedanarayana Temple, Nagalapuram

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Vedanarayana Temple or Matsya Narayana Temple is a Hindu temple in Nagalapuram, Andhra Pradesh, India. It is a Vaishnava temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu in the form of Matsya, the fish avatar, who is referred to as Matsya Narayana or Veda Narayana. This is one of the few temples in India where Lord Vishnu is depicted in Matsya avatar, the first incarnation of the Dashavatara, Lord Vishnu's 10 main incarnations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nagalapuram — Nagalapuram is a village and headquarters of Nagalapuram mandal in Tirupati district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. This town is home to Vedanarayana Temple, where the presiding deity Vishnu is in the form of Matsya, the first incarnation of Dasavatara.
  • Pichatur — Pichatur is a village in Tirupati district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is the mandal headquarters of Pitchatur mandal.
  • Beerakuppam — Beerakuppam is a small village in Nagalapuram Mandal in Tirupati district in the state of Andhra Pradesh in India. Beerakuppam is in Satyavedu Assembly Constituency.
