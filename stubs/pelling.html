Pelling

State: Sikkim | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Pelling, in West Sikkim, Sikkim, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its kanchenjunga views, skywalk, monasteries, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pemayangtse Monastery — One of Sikkim’s oldest monasteries with a 7-tier wooden sculpture.
  • Skywalk & Chenrezig Statue — Glass skywalk leading to a 137-ft Buddha statue.
  • Khecheopalri Lake — Sacred wish-fulfilling lake where leaves never settle on water.
