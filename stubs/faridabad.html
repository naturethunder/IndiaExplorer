Faridabad & Surajkund

State: Haryana | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar (Feb Crafts Mela)

Faridabad is Haryana's largest city, rich in Tomar Dynasty history. Best known for Surajkund — an 10th-century sun reservoir lake — and the annual Surajkund Crafts Mela, as well as the pristine Mangar Bani sacred grove valley.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Surajkund Lake — 10th-century amphitheater-shaped sun lake built by King Surajpal of Tomar dynasty.
  • Raja Nahar Singh Palace — 18th-century fort palace in Ballabhgarh showcasing Jat architecture.
  • Mangar Bani Sacred Forest — Ancient virgin sacred grove in the Aravalli hills with rich biodiversity.
