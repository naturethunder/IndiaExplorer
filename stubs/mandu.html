Mandu (Mandav)

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Jul – Mar

Mandu is a ruined fortress city in Madhya Pradesh set on a plateau perched over the Narmada valley. Famed for its Afghan architecture, Jahaz Mahal appears to float between two lakes.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jahaz Mahal (Ship Palace) — 15th-century Afghan palace built between Kapur and Munj lakes appearing like a ship afloat.
  • Rani Roopmati Pavilion & Sunset Point — Hilltop fort pavilion offering breathtaking sunset vistas over the Narmada river plains.
  • Hindola Mahal (Swinging Palace) — Tilted wall Afghan royal audience hall with massive sloping stone buttresses.
  • Baz Bahadur Palace — 16th-century palace combining Afghan and Rajasthani court architecture with courtyards.
  • Hoshang Shah's Marble Tomb — India's first marble structure built in 1440 which inspired Shah Jahan's Taj Mahal.
  • Jami Masjid Mandu — Colossal Pashtun architecture mosque modeled after Damascus Great Mosque.
