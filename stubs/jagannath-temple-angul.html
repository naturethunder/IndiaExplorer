Jagannath Temple, Angul

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Saila Srikhetra is a local Hindu temple site located in the district of Angul in the state of Odisha. It is a regional spiritual center where people worship Jagannath, similar to how people worship at the Jagannath Temple in Puri.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Black Diamond Museum, Angul — Black Diamond Museum and Library (Odia: ବ୍ଲାକ୍ ଡାୟମଣ୍ଡ ମ୍ୟୁଜିୟମ୍ ଏବଂ ଲାଇବ୍ରେରି; Oriya: Blāk ḍāyamaṇḍ myujiẏam ebaṁ lāibreri, pronounced [Blāk ḍāyamaṇḍ myujiẏam ebaṁ lāibreri] ), popularly known as Black Diamond Museum or Black Diamond Complex, is a digital combined cultural museum and library located in Angul, Odisha. Established in 2024, it focuses on preservation of the history, technology, and heritage of coal mining in the Talcher coalfields and the Angul region.
  • Nalconagar, Angul — Nalconagar is a census town in Angul district in the Indian state of Odisha. Nalconagar, Angul is a suburb of Angul, Odisha.
  • Angul Aluminum Park — Angul Aluminium Park (also referred to as Angul Aluminium Park Private Limited) is an industrial park located in Angul, in the state of Odisha. It is developed by National Aluminium Company Limited and the Odisha Industrial Infrastructure Development Corporation.
  • Angul Stadium — Angul Stadium is a cricket ground in Angul, Odisha. The ground was established in 1999 and is owned by Angul District Sports Association.
  • Electric Loco Shed, Angul — Electric Loco Shed, Angul is a motive power depot performing locomotive maintenance and repair facility for electric locomotives of the Indian Railways, located at Angul of the East Coast Railway zone in Odisha, India. As of 1 June 2026, there are 301 locomotives in the shed.
  • Angul — Angul (also known as Anugul) is a city, municipality and the headquarters of Angul district in the state of Odisha, India. Angul has an average elevation of 195 m (640 ft) above sea level and total area of 6232 km2.
