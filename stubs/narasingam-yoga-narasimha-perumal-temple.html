Narasingam Yoga Narasimha Perumal Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Narasingam is a village located 8 km from Madurai on the road to Melur, in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is named for the nearby cave temple dedicated to Yoga Narasimha Perumal, at the foot of the Yanaimalai hills. This temple was constructed in 770 CE by Madurakavi alias Marankaari who was the minister of the Madurai King Parantaka Nedunjadaiyan. There is a front mandapam (court).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Yanaimalai — Yanaimalai (Elephant Hill) is a protected monument and tourist attraction in Tamil Nadu, India. It has Jain sculptures, a Shaivite temple, and a Vaishnavite temple, namely the Narasingam Yoga Narasimha Perumal Temple.
  • Othakadai — Othakadai is a panchayat town in Madurai district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
  • Thirumohoor Kalamegaperumal temple — The Thirumohoor Kalamegaperumal Temple (also known as Thirumohoor or Tirumogoor temple) is a Hindu temple near Melur, Madurai district in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu, is dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu. Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, the temple is glorified in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, the early medieval Tamil canon of the Alvar saints from the 6th–9th centuries CE. It is one of the 108 Divya Desams dedicated to Vishnu, who is worshipped as Kalamega Perumal and his consort Lakshmi as Mohanavalli Thayar.
  • Thirumohur — Thirumohur is a neighbourhood in Madurai district of Tamil Nadu state in the peninsular India. Thirumohur is located at an altitude of about 162 m above the mean sea level with the geographical coordinates of 9.949700°N 78.207000°E / 9.949700; 78.207000.
  • Ilandaikulam — Ilandaikulam is a village in Madurai district, India. It is about 2 km (1.2 mi) from Maatuthavani bus stand.
  • Kannadendal — Kannadendal is a census town in Madurai district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
