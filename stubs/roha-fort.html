Roha Fort

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Roha Fort is one of the many forts of Kutch, Gujarat. The fort is located on the periphery of Roha village, in Nakhatrana Taluka of Kutch. It was the seat of Roha Jagir. One hundred twenty Soomra Rajput princesses sought asylum with Abda (Jagirdar of Abdasa) who died in the battle with Allaudin Khilji.
