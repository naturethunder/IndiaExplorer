Teru Malleshvara Temple, Hiriyur

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Teru Malleshvara Hindu temple (also spelt Teru Malleshwara) is a Vijayanagara era temple in Hiriyur town near the historically important Chitradurga city in the Indian state of Karnataka. Hiriyur is situated on the right bank of the Vedavati river. The "Teru Malleshvara" fair (Jatra, a religious gathering with festivities) is celebrated annually for a week starting from the full moon in January or February (called magha).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Hiriyur — Hiriyur or Hiriyuru is a town and a taluk headquarter located in Chitradurga district in the Indian state of Karnataka. Hiriyur is situated along National Highway 48.
  • Maskal, Chitradurga — Maskal is a village in Hiriyur taluk in the Chitradurga district of Karnataka state, India. It belongs to the Bangalore Division.
