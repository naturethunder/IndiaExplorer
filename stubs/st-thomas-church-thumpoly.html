St. Thomas Church, Thumpoly

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Thomas Church, Thumpoly (also known as Known as the 'Sanctuary of Fatima' of South India and the 'Velankanni' of South Kerala,Thumpolypally) in Kerala, India, is a Catholic pilgrimage church named after Saint Thomas the Apostle, and dedicated to the Immaculate Conception of the mother of Jesus. It is located in the Ambalappuzha taluk of Alappuzha district within the Roman Catholic Diocese of Alleppey. Its parish is one of the largest in the diocese and in the state of Kerala.
