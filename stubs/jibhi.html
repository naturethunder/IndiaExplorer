Jibhi

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Jibhi is a serene village in Tirthan valley surrounded by dense pine, deodar, and apple orchards. Famous for wooden bridges crossing gushing mountain streams, Jibhi is the base for trekking to Jalori Pass (3,125m).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jibhi Waterfall & Wooden Walkways — Hidden forest waterfall accessed via wooden bridges crossing mountain trout streams.
  • Jalori Pass Mountain Ridge (3,125m) — High mountain pass connecting Kullu and Shimla valleys with panoramic Great Himalayan views.
  • Serolsar Sacred Lake Trek — Sacred mountain ridge lake surrounded by dense oak forests and Budhi Nagin temple.
  • Chehni Kothi Fort Tower — 1500-year-old 102ft high wooden-stone tower fort built without cement.
  • Shringa Rishi Temple Banjar — Three-tiered wooden Pagoda temple dedicated to local deity Shringa Rishi.
  • Choie Waterfall Tirthan — Forest waterfall trek starting from Sai Ropa through pine woods.
