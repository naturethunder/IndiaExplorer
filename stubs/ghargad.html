Ghargad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ghargad Fort / Gadgada Fort (Marathi: घरगड किल्ला) is a fort located 145 km from Mumbai, in Nashik district, of Maharashtra. This fort is less visited fort in Nashik district. This fort is quite difficult to climb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jategaon — Jategaon is a village in Nashik taluka in Nashik district of state of Maharashtra, India. Jategaon belongs to Madhe Maharashtra and Northern Maharashtra region and Nashik Division.
  • Waldevi Dam — Waldevi Dam, is an earthfill dam on the Waldevi River near Nashik in the state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Bahula Fort — Bahula fort is located in the Igatpuri taluka of Nashik district. It is located on the ancient trade route passing through Thal ghat.
  • Anjneri Dam — Anjneri Dam, is an earthfill dam on Panzara River near Sakri in Nashik district of state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Mukane Dam — Mukane Dam, is an earthfill dam on the Aaundha river near Igatpuri, Nashik district in the state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Anjaneri — Anjaneri, one of the forts in the mountain range of Nasik-Trimbakeshwa. Anjaneri is located 20 km away from Nasik by Trimbak Road.
