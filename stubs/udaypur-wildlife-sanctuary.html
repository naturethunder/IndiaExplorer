Udaypur Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Bihar | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Udaypur Wildlife Sanctuary (also spelled Udaipur), containing Udaypur Lake (also spelled Udaipur Lake, not to be confused with Udaipur City's Five lakes) which is a Ramsar site, is a wildlife sanctuary located in West Champaran district of Bihar state, India. It was established in 1978, and covers an area of 8.74 km2. The wildlife sanctuary is predominantly 319 ha wetland, located on an oxbow lake in the floodplain of the Gandaki River.
