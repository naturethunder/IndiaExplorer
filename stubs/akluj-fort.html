Akluj Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

This fort was built by Yadav king Raja Singhan in the 13th century. In that period, this fort was completely destroyed in the war. The history of this fort is not very long and its middle history almost vanished in that period. In history that mentioned (In Moghal period it was also known as Asadnagar) Sambhaji along with 26 others was captured by Mukarrab Khan from Sangameshwar and brought to this fort The fort was ruled by Bahmani Kingdom, Sultanate of Bijapur, Mughal Empire, and Maratha Empire.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Akluj — Akluj is a town on the banks of Nira in Solapur district, Maharashtra, India. Akluj was earlier known for its large trade in cotton, which has almost disappeared at present.
  • Malshiras — Malshiras is a town and the headquarters of Malshiras taluka in Solapur district of Maharashtra state in western India. Its governing body was promoted to Nagar Panchayat from a Gram Panchayat in 2017.
