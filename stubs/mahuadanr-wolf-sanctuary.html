Mahuadanr Wolf Sanctuary

State: Jharkhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mahuadanr Wolf Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary located in Latehar district of Jharkhand. It is part of Palamau Tiger Reserve. It was declared to be a wildlife sanctuary in 1976 for the protection of the Indian wolf. Its the only wildlife sanctuary in India dedicated exclusively to the Indian grey wolf.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Lodh Falls (Burha Ghad) — Jharkhand's tallest waterfall dropping 143 meters in multiple cascades, echoing through dense sal forest canopies.
  • Betla National Park — One of India's earliest tiger reserves, renowned for deciduous sal forests, herds of wild elephants, gaurs, and 16th-century Chero dynasty forts.
  • Netarhat Plateau & Sunrise Point — Affectionately called the 'Queen of Chotanagpur', perched at 3,600 feet with pine forest groves, pear orchards, and spectacular sunrise views at Magnolia Point.
