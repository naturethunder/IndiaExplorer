Chakrata

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Perched at an elevation of 2,118 meters in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand, Chakrata is an idyllic retreat enveloped in whispering deodar, pine, and oak woodlands. Founded as a British Indian Army cantonment in 1869, the town retains its peaceful, unhurried colonial charm free from commercial crowds. From the roaring 312-foot plunge of Tiger Falls to the 300-km Himalayan panoramas from Deoban and golden hour sunsets at Chilmiri Neck, Chakrata offers an authentic mountain sanctuary for nature enthusiasts, trekkers, and peace seekers.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tiger Falls — One of the highest direct waterfalls in Uttarakhand, plummeting 312 feet (95 meters) into a crystal-clear natural plunge pool surrounded by dense oak and deodar canopy.
  • Deoban (Devban) — Situated at an altitude of 2,865 meters amidst ancient deodar forests, Deoban offers sweeping 300-km panoramic vistas of the Greater Himalayas, including Chaukhamba and Nanda Devi peaks.
  • Chilmiri Neck — The highest peak in Chakrata cantonment, famed for mesmerizing 360-degree sunset panoramas, rolling mountain ridges, and views of the Yamuna valley and Rohru hills.
  • Budher Caves & Moila Danda — An emerald undulating alpine bugyal (meadow) trek leading to the fascinating Miola Tibba and unexplored ancient limestone stalactite caves and historic Pari Tibba temple.
  • Kanasar Deodar Forest — Home to Asia’s oldest and largest deodar cedar trees with trunk circumferences exceeding 6 meters, surrounded by rolling lush meadows and historic wooden forest rest houses.
  • Ram Tal Horticultural Garden — Peaceful terraced mountain garden featuring a natural alpine pond, diverse birdlife, blossoming apple and plum orchards, and tranquil green picnic lawns.
