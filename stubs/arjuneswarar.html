Arjuneswarar

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Arjuneswarar Koil is situated on the banks of Amaravathi River in Kadathur village, in Tiruppur district. The main deity, Moolavar in this temple is Arjuneswarar. The alternate names for the Moolavar are Marudheesar, Marudhudaiyar and Marundheesar. The consort of the main deity is worshiped in the name of Gomathi Amman.
