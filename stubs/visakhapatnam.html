Visakhapatnam

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Visakhapatnam, in Eastern Ghats, Andhra Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its beaches, submarine, araku nearby, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • RK Beach — Lively city beachfront with the INS Kursura submarine museum.
  • Kailasagiri — Hilltop park with ropeway and giant Shiva-Parvati statues.
  • Borra Caves — Million-year-old limestone caves with dramatic stalactites.
