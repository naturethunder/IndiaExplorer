Valparai

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Sep – May

Valparai is a pristine hill station in the Anaimalai Hills surrounded by tea plantations and rainforest reserves. Drive up via 40 thrilling hairpin bends starting from Aliyar dam.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sholayar Dam & Reservoir Viewpoint — Second deepest dam in Asia offering vast blue water reservoir views.
  • Aliyar Dam & 40 Hairpin Bends Drive — Thrilling 40-hairpin climb starting from Aliyar dam up to Valparai plateau.
  • Niramai & Briar Tea Estates — Rolling green tea plantations with tea estate walks and processing tours.
  • Chinnakallar Waterfalls — Highest rainfall zone in South India featuring cascading rainforest falls.
  • Loam's Viewpoint (Hairpin 9) — High altitude viewpoint overlooking Aliyar reservoir basin.
  • Balaji Temple Tea Estate Walk — Private estate temple set inside serene tea garden surroundings.
