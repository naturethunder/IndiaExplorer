Sukreeswarar temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sukreesvarar Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the deity Shiva, located at Sarkkar Periyapalayam in Tiruppur district in Tamil Nadu, India.
