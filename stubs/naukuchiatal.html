Naukuchiatal

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun & Sep – Nov

Naukuchiatal, literally translated as the "lake of nine corners", is an idyllic hill hamlet situated at an altitude of 1,220 meters in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Fed by a natural underground perennial spring, it is the deepest lake in the Nainital district, reaching depths of over 40 meters. Surrounded by rolling hills covered in oak and pine forests, Naukuchiatal holds an aura of mystique: traditional folklore asserts that anyone capable of glimpsing all nine corners simultaneously from a single vantage point achieves moksha (spiritual liberation). Beyond its mythological charm, Naukuchiatal is renowned as Uttarakhand's premier hub for adventure sports including tandem paragliding, kayaking, and angling, as well as an internationally acclaimed sanctuary for birdwatchers.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Naukuchiatal Lake (Lake of Nine Corners) — The deepest lake in the Nainital lake district (plunging over 40 meters), Naukuchiatal is fed by an underground perennial spring. Its distinctive nine corners are steeped in folklore: seeing all nine corners at once from land is believed to bestow spiritual liberation.
  • Hanuman Temple & 52-Foot Colossus — Located on the main approach to Naukuchiatal, this remarkable temple features an imposing 52-foot standing statue of Lord Hanuman, visible from across the valley. It includes an artificial cave network representing the holy Vaishno Devi shrine.
  • Jungliagaon Village & Birding Trails — A serene Himalayan hamlet situated 8 km from Naukuchiatal, renowned among international ornithologists. Over 150 species of resident and migratory birds—including blue whistling thrushes, Himalayan bulbuls, and flycatchers—thrive in these unspoiled oak groves.
  • Pandegaon Paragliding Launch — Naukuchiatal is the premier paragliding hub of Kumaon. Tandem flights launch from the scenic Pandegaon heights, gliding over rolling pine valleys and giving thrill-seekers an eagle-eye view of the nine-cornered lake before landing near the valley floor.
  • Kamal Tal (Lotus Pond) — A picturesque adjoining waterbody carpeted with vibrant water lilies and lotus blooms. In summer and monsoon months, the entire pond turns pink and white with blooming lotuses, attracting colorful butterflies and rare dragonflies.
