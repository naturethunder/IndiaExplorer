Balaji Mandir Ketkawla

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Balaji Temple of Ketkawle, is copy of the original Tirumala Venkateswara Temple located 60 km from Pune, Maharashtra. It is on Pune-Bangalore highway.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nasrapur — Nasrapur is a village located 13 kilometres (8.1 mi) from Bhor Taluka in the Pune District of Maharashtra, India. It is located 36 kilometres (22 mi) south of Pune, the district headquarters, and 160 kilometres (99 mi) from the state capital, Mumbai.
  • Purandar Fort — Purandar Fort is a mountain fort in Pune district in the Western Indian state of Maharashtra, India. The fort stands at 1,374 metres (4,508 ft) above sea level in the Western Ghats, 50 kilometres (31 mi) to the southeast of Pune.
