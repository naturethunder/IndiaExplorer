Chikmagalur

State: Karnataka | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Chikmagalur, in Western Ghats, Karnataka, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its coffee, mullayanagiri, waterfalls, with the best months being Sep – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mullayanagiri Peak — Highest peak in Karnataka at 1,930m.
  • Baba Budangiri — Sacred cave-shrine range famed for the first Indian coffee beans.
  • Hebbe Falls — Two-tier waterfall reached by a jeep ride through coffee estates.
