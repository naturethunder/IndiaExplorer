Vairatgad Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Vairatgad fort is a fort situated near to the town of Wai vyajwadi in Maharashtra, India. The top of the fort is very small. There are remnants of old buildings. The bastion at the entrance is dilapidated.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kudal (Satara) — Kudal is a village in the Satara district, Maharashtra, India. It is surrounded by a river called Niranjana or Kudali.
  • Wai, Maharashtra — Wai (ISO: Vāī; Pronunciation: [ʋaːi] ) is a town in Satara district of Maharashtra state in India. Located on the Krishna River, Wai was a prominent town during the Peshwa era.
