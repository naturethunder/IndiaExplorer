Mehandipur Balaji Temple

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mehandipur Balaji Temple is a Hindu temple located in Mehandipur, Dausa district, in the Indian state of Rajasthan. The temple is dedicated to the deity Hanuman. The name Balaji refers to the childhood form of Hanuman, revered in several parts of the country. Unlike similar religious sites, it is located in a town rather than the countryside.
