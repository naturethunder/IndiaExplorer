Digha

State: West Bengal | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Digha, in Bay of Bengal, West Bengal, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its beach, marine aquarium, weekend getaway, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • New Digha Beach — Broad sandy beach situated 2 km from Old Digha, flanked by casuarina groves and offering calm swimming waters.
  • Marine Aquarium & Regional Centre (MARC) — India's largest marine biodiversity aquarium equipped with 32 viewing tanks showcasing exotic marine species of the Bay of Bengal.
  • Udaipur Beach — Unspoiled beach at the West Bengal - Odisha border featuring red ghost crabs, tranquil waters, and serene coastal tranquility.
