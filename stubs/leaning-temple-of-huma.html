Leaning Temple of Huma

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Leaning Temple of Huma in India is one of only a very few leaning temples in the world. It is located in Huma, a village situated on the bank of the Mahanadi, 23 km (14 mi) south of Sambalpur in the Indian state of Odisha. The temple is dedicated to the Hindu god Lord Bimaleshwar. It is unknown if this structure is leaning by design or for another reason.
