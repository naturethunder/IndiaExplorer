Betla National Park

State: Jharkhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Betla National Park is a national park located in the Latehar and Palamu district of Jharkhand, India. Its the only national park in the state. It is spread over an area of 226.32 km2 (87.38 sq mi) and is a part of the Palamu Tiger Reserve. It was declared a national park in 1986.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Palamu fort — The Palamu Fort are two ruined forts located 3 k.m from Betla National Park, Latehar district on the bank of Auranga River, in the Indian state of Jharkhand. The original fort in the plains and the other on an adjoining hill are attributed to the kings of the Chero dynasty.
  • Satbarwa block — Satbarwa block is one of the administrative blocks of Palamu district, Jharkhand state, India. It is one of the important block/Taliuka in NH-75 (old numbering).
  • Polpol — Polpol is a village of Palamau district, situated 16 km from the nearest district town, Medininagar. National Highway 39 crosses this village.
  • Barwadih — Barwadih is a town and Community Development Block in Latehar district of Jharkhand state in India.
