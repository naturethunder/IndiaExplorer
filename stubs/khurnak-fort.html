Khurnak Fort

State: Ladakh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Khurnak Fort (Tibetan: མཁར་ནག, Wylie: mkhar nag) is a historic ruined fort on the northern shore of Pangong Lake at 4,250 m elevation. Denoting the geographic middle boundary of the Pangong basin in eastern Ladakh, the fort stands on the alluvial fan of the Changlung Lungpa river overlooking crystalline turquoise waters and the barren, majestic ridges of the Changchenmo mountain range.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pangong Tso (North Shore Waters) — The famed endorheic high-altitude salt lake situated at 4,225m. The crystal waters shift dramatically between cobalt blue and turquoise, bordered by the jagged arid peaks of the Changchenmo range.
  • Changlung Lungpa Plain & Ruined Ramparts — The broad alluvial mountain plain where glacial meltwaters flow toward Pangong Lake. Features historical dry-stone fortification walls, ancient trader campsites, and panoramic Changthang desert expanses.
  • Spangmik Shoreline & High Himalayan Viewpoint — The premier vantage point across Pangong Lake towards the northern mountains, offering iconic perspectives of snow-crested peaks reflected across the mirrored alpine water surface.
