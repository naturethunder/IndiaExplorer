Goa

State: Goa | Category: beach | Best Time: Nov – Mar

Goa, India's smallest state, punches well above its weight as a travel destination. With 101km of coastline divided between the busy northern beaches and the quieter south, plus a UNESCO-listed old city quarter, spice plantations and some of Asia's best beach parties, Goa is truly an all-seasons destination.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Baga Beach — North Goa's most popular beach. Water sports, shacks and lively evenings.
  • Old Goa Churches — UNESCO World Heritage. Basilica of Bom Jesus holds St. Francis Xavier's remains.
  • Palolem Beach — South Goa's crescent-shaped gem. Calm waters, kayaking and silent disco.
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls — India's 5th highest waterfall at 310m. Jeep safari through forest essential.
  • Fontainhas Latin Quarter — Goa's heritage quarter with colourful Portuguese-era houses and art galleries.
  • Spice Plantation Tour — Guided tour through cardamom, pepper and cashew plantations with Goan lunch.
