Ghosalegad Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ghosalegad Fort is located 8 km south of the Roha town on Roha-Bhalgaon-Murud road. This fort is in form of a narrow strip 20mt in width. This fort has a small hill with a narrow spur guarded by fortification.
