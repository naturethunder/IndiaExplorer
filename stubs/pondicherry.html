Pondicherry

State: Puducherry | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Pondicherry, in Coromandel Coast, Puducherry, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its french quarter, beaches, auroville, cafes, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Promenade Beach — 1.5-km seafront promenade with the Gandhi statue and old pier.
  • Auroville Matrimandir — Golden meditation sphere at the heart of the experimental township.
  • French Quarter (White Town) — Mustard-yellow colonial villas, boutiques and cafes.
