Shikari Devi Temple

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Shikari Devi Temple is a popular Hindu temple dedicated to goddess Durga in the form of Hunter's Goddess (in Hindi: Shikari Devi). The temple is located 18 kilometres (11 mi) from Sanarli in Karsog valley and at the same distance from Janjheli which is on the other side of the hill, through steep road to the temple in Mandi district of the North Indian state of Himachal Pradesh. It is believed that the temple was established by the Pandavas during their exile at the time of Mahabharata.

  • Bagsiad — Bagsiad is a small town at altitude of 2000m which is a central area for two village panchayats (i.e. Sharan and Kandha-Bagsiad) in tehsil Thunag of Seraj constituency in district Mandi, Himachal Pradesh.
