Mumbai

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mumbai, in Konkan Coast, Maharashtra, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its gateway, marine drive, bollywood, street food, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gateway of India — 1924 basalt arch on the harbour, Mumbai’s defining landmark.
  • Marine Drive — 3.6-km seafront promenade called the “Queen’s Necklace” at night.
  • Elephanta Caves — UNESCO island rock-cut Shiva caves reached by ferry.
