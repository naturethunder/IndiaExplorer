Lothian Island Wildlife Sanctuary

State: West Bengal | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Lothian Island Wildlife Sanctuary is situated in South 24 Parganas district, West Bengal, India. The wildlife in this sanctuary includes estuarine crocodiles, olive ridley sea turtles, spotted deer, jungle cats and rhesus macaques. The tropical wetland forest consists of mangrove vegetation that provides a dense cover along the habitat.

  • Gobardhanpur — Gobardhanpur is a village within the jurisdiction of the Patharpratima police station in the Patharpratima CD block in the Kakdwip subdivision of the South 24 Parganas district in the Indian state of West Bengal.
  • Henry Island (India) — Henry's Island is an island near Bakkhali in South 24 Parganas of West Bengal, India. It is around 130 km (81 mi) from Kolkata.
