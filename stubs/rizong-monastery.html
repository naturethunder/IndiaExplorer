Rizong Monastery

State: Ladakh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Rizong (or Rhizong) gompa, Gelugpa or Yellow Hat Buddhist monastery, also called the Yuma Changchubling, is in Ladakh, India. It is situated at the top of a rocky side valley on the north side of the Indus, to the west of Alchi on the way to Lamayuru. It was established in 1831 by Lama Tsultim Nima under the Gelukpa order, at Ri-rdzong. There are 40 monks in the monastery.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Giramangu — Giramangu, also known as Gaira Mangue or Mangyu, is a village in the Sham district of Ladakh, India. It is located in the Likir tehsil.
  • Saspol Caves — Saspol Caves (a.k.a. Gon-Nila-Phuk Cave Temples) are situated in the hills behind Suspol, Ladakh, India, which is about 76 km from the city centre of Leh.
  • Hemis Shukpachan — Hemis-Shukpachan or Hemishok Pachan is a village in the Sham district of Ladakh, India. It is located on the right side bank of Indus River and is in the Likir tehsil.
  • Mangyu temple complex — The Mangyu temple complex located in the village of Mangyu, Ladakh is one of the earliest in Ladakh, India. Believed to be contemporaneous to the temples at Alchi Monastery and Sumda Chun, the earliest structures are supposed to be dating to the late 12th/early 13th century but as per the oral history and local belief the temples were established by Lotsawa Rinchen Zangpo.
  • Alchi — Alchi is a village in the Sham district of Ladakh, India. It is located in the Likir tehsil, on the banks of the Indus River 70 km (43.49miles) downstream from the capital Leh.
  • Saspochey — Saspochey is a village in the Sham district of Ladakh, India. It is located in the Likir tehsil.
