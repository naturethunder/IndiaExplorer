Daringbadi

State: Odisha | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Sep – May

Daringbadi is a serene hill station at 3,000 feet in Odisha surrounded by dense pine forests and coffee plantations. Known for sub-zero winter mornings where dew drops freeze into thin ice, Daringbadi features gushing waterfalls, tribal heritage, organic pepper gardens, and cool mountain air.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Daringbadi Pine Forest Walk — Dense pine tree plantation trails surrounded by misty hills and crisp mountain air.
  • Daringbadi Coffee & Black Pepper Gardens — Organic coffee plantations managed by Odisha Forest Dept with black pepper vines.
  • Midubanda Waterfalls — Cascading waterfall hidden deep inside forest gorges with natural bathing pools.
  • Hill View Park & Butterfly Garden — Elevated hilltop park offering sunset views over Kandhamal valley.
  • Duluri River & Granite Rock Bed — Crystal mountain stream flowing over smooth pink granite rock formations.
  • Ludo Waterfalls & Nature Camp — Unexplored forest waterfall with canopy trails inside deep jungle.
