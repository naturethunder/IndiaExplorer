Talra Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Talra Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Shimla, Himachal Pradesh, India. It became a wildlife sanctuary in 1962. This wildlife sanctuary covers an area of 40 km2. It is an eco-sensitive zone, notified by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MOEFCC).
