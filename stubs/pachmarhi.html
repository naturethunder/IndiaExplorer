Pachmarhi

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – Jun

Pachmarhi, in Satpura, Madhya Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its only hill station of mp, caves, waterfalls, with the best months being Oct – Jun.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bee Falls — Popular bathing waterfall reached by a short forest descent.
  • Pandav Caves — Five ancient rock-cut caves set in a garden.
  • Dhupgarh — Highest point of the Satpura range, famed for sunsets.
