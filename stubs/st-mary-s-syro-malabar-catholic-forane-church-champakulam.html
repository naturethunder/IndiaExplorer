St. Mary's Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church, Champakulam

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Champakulam Kalloorkadu St. Mary's Syro Malabar Catholic Basilica Church (Malayalam: ചമ്പക്കുളം വലിയ പള്ളി) is an ancient Christian Church in Kerala. It belongs to the Syro-Malabar Church under the Archeparchy of Changanacherry. It is a Forane church, with several parishes under it.
