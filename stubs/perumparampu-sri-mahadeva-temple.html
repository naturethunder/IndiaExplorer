Perumparampu Sri Mahadeva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Perumparampu Sri Mahadeva Temple is located at Edappal in Malappuram district. The main deity of the temple is Shiva in the sanctum sanctorum facing west. It is believed that this temple is one of the 108 Shiva temples of Kerala and is installed by sage Parasurama dedicated to Shiva. The temple is located around 3km away from Edappal on the route of Edappal - Parappuram - Ayankalam Road.

  • Chamravattam — Chamravattam is a village located in Malappuram district, Kerala, India. This village is on the shores of the Bharathapuzha.
