Varkala

State: Kerala | Category: beach | Best Time: Nov – Mar

Varkala is Kerala's most dramatic beach destination. The 15-metre red laterite cliffs running parallel to the beach are unique on India's coastline. With a laid-back vibe, excellent seafood restaurants perched on the clifftop, yoga centres, and Ayurvedic retreats, it attracts both spiritual seekers and beach lovers.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Papanasam Beach — Sacred Hindu beach with red cliffs. Sunset from the clifftop path is magnificent.
  • Janardhana Swamy Temple — 2,000-year-old Vishnu temple on the cliff. Sacred spring (Sree Padateertha Kund).
  • Kappil Beach — Secluded beach where a backwater meets the sea. Less crowded, ideal for sunrise walks.
  • Varkala Cliff Promenade — Clifftop path lined with cafes, yoga studios, and shops. Best at golden hour.
  • Anjengo Fort — Dutch-era coastal fort with a working lighthouse and ancient cemetery. Rarely visited.
  • Backwater Kayaking Tour — Mangrove kayaking through the Edava backwaters. Spot kingfishers and egrets.
