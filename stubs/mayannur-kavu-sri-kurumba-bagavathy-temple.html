Mayannur Kavu Sri Kurumba Bagavathy Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Mayannur Kavu Sri Kurumba Bagavathy Temple is a Hindu temple located in Mayannur of Thrissur District of Kerala. The temple is dedicated to Goddess Sri Kurumba Bhagavathi.
