Khajuraho

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Khajuraho, in Bundelkhand, Madhya Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its temples, sculptures, unesco, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Western Group of Temples — UNESCO Chandela temples famed for intricate erotic sculpture.
  • Kandariya Mahadev — The largest and most ornate of the Khajuraho temples.
  • Light & Sound Show — Evening show on the temples’ history, narrated by Amitabh Bachchan.
