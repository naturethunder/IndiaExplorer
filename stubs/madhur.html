Madhur

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Madhur is a sacred temple located in Kerala, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Political divisions of Kasaragod, Kasaragod and Thalangara. The best time to visit is Nov – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Political divisions of Kasaragod — Kasaragod district has four types of administrative hierarchies:
  • Kasaragod — Kasaragod (Malayalam: [kaːsɐrɐɡoːɖɨ̆] ) is a municipal town and the administrative headquarters of the Kasaragod district in the state of Kerala, India. Established in 1966, Kasaragod was the first municipal town in the district.
  • Thalangara — Thalangara is a part of Kasaragod Town, the district headquarters of the Kasaragod district in the South Indian state of Kerala. Malik Denar Juma Masjid and Dargah is located here.
  • Malik Dinar Mosque — The Malik Dinar Mosque, also known locally as the Malik Deenar Masjid and Malik Deenar Juma Masjid, and officially known as the Hazrath Malik Deenar Grand Juma Masjid, is a Sufi Sunni mosque and dargah, located in Thalangara, in the town of Kasaragod, in the state of Kerala, India. Founded by Malik Dinar and believed to been completed in c. 22 AH (642/643 CE), it is one of the oldest mosques in India.
  • Chemnad — Chemnad is a town in Kasaragod district in the Indian state of Kerala. It is located east of Kasaragod town, across the Chandragiri River, and covers 8.3 square kilometres (3.2 sq mi) of land.
  • Nellikunnu Muhyaddin Juma Masjid — Muhyaddin Juma Masjid (Malayalam: മുഹ്യദ്ദീന്ജുമ മസ്ജിദ്), is a Sunni Friday mosque and dargah located at Nellikunnu, Kasaragod district, in the state of Kerala, India.
