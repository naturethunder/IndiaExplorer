Ancient Temple at Ladhoo

State: Jammu & Kashmir | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Ancient Temple, Ladhoo, is located in the middle of a natural fed pond or spring known as Sanz Haer Nag or Sanyasar Nag in Ladhoo, a village in the Pulwama district of the southern part of the Kashmir Valley of the Indian union territory of Jammu and Kashmir. It is four km (2.5 mi) away from NH44 via Lethapora and 18 km (11 mi) from Srinagar. The temple is listed among List of Monuments of National Importance in Jammu and Kashmir. It has also been attributed by other including, Sun Temple or Jeevanath Temple or Shiv temple, but these names found no historical proof.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ladhoo — Ladhoo or Ladhu is an ancient village in Pulwama district of Kashmir, situated on the foothills of Wasturwan, renowned for its 8th-century spring temple and saffron heritage.
  • Khrew — Khrew is a scenic town nestled beneath the Wasturwan mountain slopes in Pulwama, known for lush orchards, mineral springs, and the revered hillside shrine of Jwalamukhi.
  • Jwala Ji Temple (Kashmir) — Jwalamukhi Temple in Khrew is a celebrated Hindu shrine perched on a scenic hillock, dedicated to deity Jwala Ji, the kuldevi of many Kashmiri Pandits, with panoramic views over the saffron valley.
  • Avantiswami Temple (Avantipur) — Avantiswami Temple is an illustrious 9th-century classical stone temple complex built by King Avantivarman on the Jhelum river banks, showcasing magnificent Kashmiri stone colonnades.
  • Lethapora — Lethapora is a historic town on the NH44 highway corridor in Pulwama, globally famous as the primary saffron market hub of the Kashmir Valley, surrounded by vast karewa plateaus.
  • Lelhar — Lelhar is an idyllic agrarian village in Pulwama known for sweeping spring mustard fields, traditional walnut groves, and serene views across the Pir Panjal mountain range.
