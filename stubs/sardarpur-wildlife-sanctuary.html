Sardarpur Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sardarpur Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located in Dhar district, of Madhya Pradesh in India. It was officially notified in 1983. Originally, the sanctuary spanned approximately 348 km² (34,800 ha). The landscape is dominated by open grasslands with patches of scrub and seasonal wetlands habitats.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mohankheda — Mohan Kheda is a Śvetāmbara Jain tirtha (pilgrimage place) located in the Dhar district of Madhya Pradesh in India. The site is situated 105 kilometres (65 mi) from Indore and 47 km (29 mi) from Dhar on the Indore-Ahmedabad Highway.
  • Rajgarh, Dhar — Rajgarh is a town and a nagar panchay, near city of Dhar in Dhar district in the Indian state of Madhya Pradesh.
