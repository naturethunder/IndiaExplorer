Rajiv Gandhi National Park

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Rajiv Gandhi National Park is a national park located in Rameswaram of Kadapa District, Andhra Pradesh, India. Its area is about 2.4 square kilometers of tropical dry deciduous forest mostly growing on a sandy soil. It lies on the north bank of the Penna River.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Proddatur — Proddatur is a town in the YSR Kadapa District of Andhra Pradesh, located on the banks of the Penna River. The city is a municipality, and also the mandal headquarters of Proddatur mandal.
