Kasbeswari Kali Mata

State: Tripura | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kasbeswari Kali Mata or Kamalasagar Kali Temple was built on a hill-top by Maharaja Dhanya Manikya in the late 15th century. It is situated just beside the Bangladesh border, The lake, Kamalasagar, in front of this temple rightly enhances its beauty. Kamalasagar Temple is located about 27 km from Agartala in a small village called Kasba . It falls on Indo- Bangladesh border.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kasba Upazila — Kasba (Bengali: কসবা) is an upazila of Brahmanbaria District, Chittagong Division in east-central Bangladesh. It is located approximately 150 km north of Chittagong and was part of greater Cumilla District until 1984.
  • Kamalasagar Border Lake & Grounds — Sprawling historic 15th-century freshwater lake adjacent to the holy Kasbeswari Kali Mata temple.
  • Kamalasagar — Kamalasagar is an artificial lake in Tripura, India, constructed by King Dhanya Manikya of Tripura in the 15th century and is a popular spot for picnickers. A mela is held there every October during the Navaratri festival.
  • Mondobhag train collision — The Mondobhag train collision occurred on 12 November 2019 at Mondobhag railway station, Kasba, Bangladesh. Two trains were involved.
  • Bagra, Chittagong — Bagra (also, Bāgra) is a village in Chittagong Division, Bangladesh.
  • Purquil — Purquil is a village in the east-central part of Bangladesh. It is located at Mehari Union of Kasba Upazila in Brahmanbaria District of Chittagong Division.
