Pushkar

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Pushkar, in Ajmer, Rajasthan, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its holy lake, camel fair, brahma temple, cafes, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pushkar Lake — Sacred lake ringed by 52 bathing ghats and temples.
  • Brahma Temple — One of the very few temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma.
  • Savitri Mata Temple — Hilltop temple reached by ropeway with sweeping desert views.
