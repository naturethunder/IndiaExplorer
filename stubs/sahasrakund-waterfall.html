Sahasrakund Waterfall

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sahasrakund Waterfall (also Sahastrakund) is a waterfall on the Painganga River of Nanded district, Maharashtra, India. This town is on the boundary of the Yavatmal and Nanded districts on the Painganga River. Sahasrakund situated at a distance 172 km from Yavatmal and 100 km from Nanded. It is also 50 km from Nirmal and 100 km from Adilabad.from Himayatnagar 18 Km only.
