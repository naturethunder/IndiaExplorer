Spiti Valley

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: adventure | Best Time: Jun – Sep

Spiti Valley is a cold desert mountain valley in north-eastern Himachal Pradesh at 3,800m. Its stark, treeless landscapes, ancient Buddhist monasteries, and villages accessible only in summer months make it one of India's most remote and extraordinary destinations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Key Monastery (Ki Gompa) — 1,000-year-old monastery perched dramatically above the Spiti river at 4,166m.
  • Chandratal Lake — Crescent-shaped glacial lake at 4,300m. One of India's most beautiful campsites.
  • Hikkim Post Office — World's highest post office at 4,400m. Post a postcard!
  • Dhankar Monastery & Lake — Dramatic 1,000-year-old fort monastery on a cliff above Dhankar Lake. Short trek to the lake.
  • Pin Valley National Park — Cold desert biosphere with ibex, snow leopard and the rare Siberian ibex. Trophy trek routes.
  • Komik Village — World's highest village connected by motorable road at 4,513m. Ancient Tangyud Monastery.
