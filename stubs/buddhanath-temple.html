Buddhanath Temple

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The 12th-century Buddhanath Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the god Shiva. The temple is in the village of Garedi Panchana in the block of Balipatna about 22 km (14 mi) away from the city of Bhubaneswar, India. The temple is said to have been built by the king Chodaganga Dev of the Somavanshi dynasty. Legend has it that snakebite victims do not die if they are brought to the temple premises.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Adaspur — Adaspur is a village in the Kantapada block of Cuttack district, Odisha, India, known for landmarks like the Baraha Nrusingha Temple and Tara Pokhari. The village is connected by State Highway 60 (SH-60) and is home to educational institutions such as Udayanath College of Science and Technology and Prachi Academy.
  • Chausath Yogini Temple, Hirapur — The Chausathi Yogini Temple (64-Yogini Temple) of Hirapur, also said Mahamaya Temple, is 20 km outside Bhubaneswar, the capital of Odisha state of Eastern India. It devotes to the worship of the yoginis, auspicious goddess-like figures.
  • Pratapsasan — Pratapsasan is a census town in Khordha district in the Indian state of Odisha.
