New Delhi

State: Delhi | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Conceived in 1911 as the imperial capital of British India and designed by architects Sir Edwin Lutyens and Sir Herbert Baker, New Delhi is renowned for its grand axial vistas, neoclassical sandstone government palaces, and expansive green canopies. From the 42-metre triumphal arch at India Gate and the sprawling ceremonial lawns of Kartavya Path to the peaceful marble petals of Lotus Temple, the sacred healing waters of Gurudwara Bangla Sahib, and the historic tombs within Lodhi Garden, New Delhi represents the majestic civic and cultural heart of modern India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • India Gate & Kartavya Path — Grand 42-metre triumphal arch war memorial dedicated to 84,000 fallen soldiers, facing the National War Memorial and Kartavya Path.
  • Rashtrapati Bhavan & Amrit Udyan — Stately 340-room presidential palace designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens, featuring the world-famous Amrit Udyan (Mughal Gardens).
  • Lotus Temple (Baháʼí House of Worship) — Award-winning architectural icon shaped like a blossoming white marble lotus flower with 27 petals welcoming all faiths.
  • Gurudwara Bangla Sahib — Prominent Sikh shrine associated with Eighth Sikh Guru Har Krishan Ji, featuring a sacred golden dome, holy pond, and mega-langar.
  • Lodhi Garden — Serene 90-acre heritage park containing the 15th-century domed tombs of Sayyid and Lodi rulers amidst manicured botanical lawns.
  • Sunder Nursery Heritage Park — 90-acre UNESCO-awarded ecological heritage park restoring 15 Mughal monuments, lakeside pavilions, and botanical conservatories.
