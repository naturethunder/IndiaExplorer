Shantinatha temple, Ramtek

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shantinatha temple, Ramtek is a major Jain tirth (pilgrimage site) in the Indian state of Maharashtra, located between the villages of Ramtek in Nagpur district.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Khindsi Lake — Khindsi Lake is a lake near the city of Ramtek in the Nagpur district of India. The lake offers boating activities, watersports, restaurants, and resorts, which are operated by Rajkamal Tourism and Olive Resorts at Khindsi Lake.
  • Ramtek Dam — Ramtek Dam, is an earthfill dam on Sur River near Ramtek, Nagpur district in the state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Mansar, India — Mansar is a census town in Ramtek tehsil of Nagpur district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. This town is located 5 km west of Ramtek and 45 km northeast of Nagpur city.
  • Kachurwahi — Kachurwahi is a Gram Panchayat village in Ramtek Tehsil of Nagpur district, India. It is situated in the South East of Ramtek at the distance of 10 km from Ramtek.
  • Mahadula — Mahadula is a census town in Nagpur district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
