Dhodap Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Dhodap is one of the hill forts in Maharashtra state in India. Situated in Chandwad taluka in the Nashik district. Base village is Hatti. the fort is 4829 ft (1472 mt) above sea level.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Abhona — Abhona is a village in Kalwan tehsil of Nashik district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It belongs to the North Maharashtra region.
  • Chankapur Dam — Chankapur Dam, is an earthfill dam on the Girna River near Abhona in Kalwan tehsil of Nashik district in the state of Maharashtra in India. The dam were built in 19th century by British.
  • Nanduri Dam — Nanduri Dam, is an earthfill and gravity dam on Tapti river near Ama Local Nallaher, Nashik district in the state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Ahivant Fort — Ahivant Fort is a fort located 55km from Nashik, in Nashik district, of Maharashtra. This fort is an important fort in Nashik district.
  • Satmala Range — Satmala (pronunciation:[sɐtmaːla]) is a mountain range which runs across Nashik District, Maharashtra. They are an integral part of the Sahyadris range within Nashik.
  • Saptashrungi — Saptashrungi or Saptashringi (Marathi: सप्तशृङ्गि, Saptaśr̥ṇgī) is a site of Hindu pilgrimage in the village of Vani, situated 60 kilometres (37 mi) from Nashik in Indian state of Maharashtra. According to Hindu traditions, the goddess Saptashrungi Nivasini dwells within the seven mountain peaks.
