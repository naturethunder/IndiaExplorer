Belgaum Fort

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Belgaum Fort is in the city of Belgaum, in the Belgaum district, in Karnataka state, India. It was begun by Jaya Raya, also called Bichi Raja, an ally of the Ratta dynasty, in the year 1204. It has undergone several renovations over the centuries under dynastic rulers of the region. The fort, built with ramparts and a large moat, with historical and religious monuments dated to the Adil Shahi dynasty.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kamal Basadi (Kamal Basti) — Exquisite 10th-century Ratta dynasty Jain temple renowned for its astonishing carved dome ceiling resembling a masterfully blossoming 72-petal lotus.
  • Rajhansgad Yellur Fort — Majestic hilltop stone fortification constructed by the Ratta and Maratha rulers, commanding breathtaking 360-degree vistas across the Belagavi plateau.
  • Kapileshwar Temple — Revered as the Dakshina Kashi, this ancient stone temple dedicated to Lord Shiva is believed to date back to the 12th century with sacred stone lingam.
  • Military Mahadeva Temple — Spiritual stone sanctuary maintained with meticulous precision by the Indian Army Maratha Light Infantry, surrounded by lush landscaped gardens.
  • Belgaum Fort Lake (Kote Kere) — Serene water reservoir surrounding the fort ramparts, featuring pedestrian jogging tracks, pedal boating facilities, and evening musical fountains.
  • Gokak Falls — Often termed Karnataka’s Niagara, the Ghataprabha River dramatically plunges 52 meters down a horseshoe sandstone cliff beside an ancient suspension bridge.
