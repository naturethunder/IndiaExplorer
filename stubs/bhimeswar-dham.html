Bhimeswar Dham

State: Assam | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bhimeswar Dham, is a Hindu temple, located at Dakini hill (also known as Daini pahar) near Pamohi in Guwahati, Assam, India. It is situated at the hill just besides the Deepor Beel. It is believed that Shiva had incarnated here to destroy a demon called Bhimasura and protect his devotees. The exact location of Dakini is a debated subject.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Betkuchi — Betkuchi is a verdant locality on the southwestern outskirts of Guwahati near Pamohi, surrounded by rolling hills, tea gardens, and forested streams of Kamrup Metropolitan district.
  • Garchuk Lachit Garh — Garchuk Lachit Garh is a historical earthen fortification and rampart constructed during the medieval Ahom kingdom under General Lachit Borphukan to defend Guwahati.
  • Swahid Smarak Kshetra — Swahid Smarak Kshetra is a landscaped memorial park and garden in Paschim Boragaon, Guwahati, built to honor the martyrs of the Assam Movement amidst green manicured grounds.
  • Jyotikuchi — Jyotikuchi is a scenic residential foothill neighborhood in southwest Guwahati bordering the Pamohi river valley and green hillsides of the Southern Kamrup range.
  • Borjhar (LGBI Airport) — Borjhar is the premier aviation and transport gateway of Northeast India, home to the Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport surrounded by scenic paddy fields and hills.
  • Dhirenpara — Dhirenpara is a vibrant valley neighborhood in south Guwahati situated close to the Pamohi hills, offering quick access to the peaceful forested tracks of Bhimeswar Dham.
