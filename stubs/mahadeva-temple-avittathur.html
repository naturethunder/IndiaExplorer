Mahadeva Temple, Avittathur

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Avittathur Mahadeva Temple, an ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva is situated at Avittathur village of Thrissur District in Kerala state in India. Avittathur gramam (village) is one of the 64 original Brahmin settlements in the ancient Kerala. The Avittathur temple as well as Avittathur Gramam is equally famous like Thaliparamb gramam, Sukapuram gramam, Irinjalakuda gramam and Peruvanam gramam. Avittathur Mahadeva Temple is more than 2000 years old and four old inscriptions have been discovered.

  • Kaduppassery — Kaduppassery is a village in Mukundapuram taluk, Thrissur district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Kallamkunnu — Kallamkunnu is a town in Kerala, India. It is the second-largest town in Nadavaramba and 15th-largest in Irinjalakuda.
