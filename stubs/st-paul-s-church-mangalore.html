St. Paul's Church, Mangalore

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Paul's Church is an Anglican church in Mangalore, India. St. Paul's Church is located at the south-west corner of the Nehru Maidan (formerly the Central Maidan) in Mangalore.

  • Kadri Manjunath Temple — Kadri Manjunatha Temple is a historic temple in Mangaluru in the state of Karnataka, India. The incharge and priests in the temple are Shivalli Madhva Brahmins.
