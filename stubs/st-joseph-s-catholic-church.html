St. Joseph's Catholic Church

State: Jammu & Kashmir | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

St. Joseph's Catholic Church is a parish of the Roman Catholic Church in Baramulla, Jammu and Kashmir, India. It was established in 1891 by the Mill Hill Missionaries, making it the oldest Catholic church in Jammu and Kashmir, and currently belongs to the Jammu Srinagar Diocese. St.
