Hazara Falls

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Hajra Falls is a waterfall 191 km from Nagpur, 50 km from Gondia and close to the Gondia district in India. Hajra Falls, in Salekasa tehsil, is a tourist attraction during the rainy season. It is located 1 km from Darekasa railway station.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Salekasa — Salekasa (Marathi: सालेकसा) is a village and a tehsil in the Deori subdivision of Gondia district in Nagpur division in the Berar region in the state of Maharashtra, India. In 2020, a cache of explosives and weapons were found in a Naxal hideout in Salekasa.
  • Chandsuraj — Chandsuraj is a village located in Salekasa, within the Gondiya district of Maharashtra, India. Its population is 626 as of 2011.
