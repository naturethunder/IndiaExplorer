Kochi

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kochi, in Malabar Coast, Kerala, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its fort kochi, chinese nets, spice trade, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chinese Fishing Nets — Iconic cantilevered nets lining the Fort Kochi shore.
  • Mattancherry Palace — Portuguese-built palace with vivid Hindu mythological murals.
  • Jew Town & Synagogue — Antique-lined lane and the 1568 Paradesi Synagogue.
