Kothandaramaswami Temple, Nandambakkam

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kothandaramaswami Temple is a Hindu temple located at Nandambakkam, a suburb of Chennai, India. Dedicated to Rama, the spot is associated with the legendary sage Bhrigu.

  • Madras War Cemetery — Madras War Cemetery is located in Nandambakkam, Madras (Chennai), Tamil Nadu, India. It was created to receive Second World War graves from civil and cantonment cemeteries in the south and east of India where their permanent maintenance could not be assured.
