Nartiang Durga Temple

State: Meghalaya | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Nartiang Durga Temple is a Hindu temple located in the West Jaintia Hills district of Meghalaya in northeastern India. It is one of the 51 Shakta pithas and is one of the holiest sites for devotees of the Shaktism sect of Hinduism. It is 600 years old. The Hindus in the Jaintia Hills believe that this temple is the permanent abode of Goddess Durga.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nartiang Monoliths Park — The largest cluster of prehistoric monoliths in India, featuring towering menhirs erected by ancient Jaintia kings.
  • Thadlaskein Lake — Historic scenic lake dug with bow-ends by medieval Jaintia warriors, surrounded by peaceful pine-covered hills.
  • Jaintia Hills Heritage Valley — Lush undulating countryside featuring traditional Jaintia tribal villages, terraced fields, and sacred groves.
