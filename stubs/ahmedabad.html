Ahmedabad

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Ahmedabad, in Sabarmati, Gujarat, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its heritage city, sabarmati ashram, textiles, with the best months being Nov – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sabarmati Ashram — Gandhi’s riverside ashram and museum of the freedom struggle.
  • Adalaj Stepwell — Intricately carved five-storey 1498 stepwell.
  • Sidi Saiyyed Mosque — Famous for its delicate tree-of-life jali stone lattice.
