Chandrawati Jain temple

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Chandrawati Jain temple is a Jain tirth located in the Chandrawati village located 23 kilometres (14 mi) from Varanasi. This place is believed to be birthplace of Chandraprabha, the 8th Thirthankara, of Jainism.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gomti River — The Gomti, Gumti or Gomati River is a river flowing entirely within the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. It is a tributary of the Ganges.
  • Swarved Mahamandir — Swarved Mahamandir is a charitable meditation centre built in Varanasi and inaugurated by Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and with its seven-storey claims to be the biggest meditation centre in the World. The centre is built with an estimated cost of Rs 100 crores and has a seating capacity of 20,000 people at a time.
