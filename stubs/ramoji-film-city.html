Ramoji Film City

State: Telangana | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ramoji Film City is an integrated film studio facility located right outside of Hyderabad, India. Spread over 2,000 acres (810 ha), it was recognized as the world's largest film studio complex by the Guinness World Records in 2005. Established in 1996 by Telugu media proprietor Ramoji Rao, it has been described as a "city within a city" by The Guardian. In addition to serving as a major hub for film production, Ramoji Film City is a popular thematic holiday destination, featuring a mix of natural landscapes, artificial sets, and an amusement park.
