Ranikhet

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Ranikhet, in Kumaon, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its golf, pine forests, himalayan views, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jhula Devi Temple — Temple of thousands of bells offered for wishes fulfilled.
  • Chaubatia Gardens — Apple and apricot orchards with a Himalayan viewpoint.
  • Upat Golf Course — One of Asia’s highest 9-hole golf courses amid pines.
