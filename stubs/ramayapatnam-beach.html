Ramayapatnam Beach

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ramayapatnam Beach is located on the coast of Bay of Bengal, in Prakasam district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gollapalem — Pedda Gollapalem is a village in Nidamarru hamlet, Kruthivennu mandal, located in Krishna district on the sea coast of Bay of Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, India.
  • Mogalthur mandal — Mogalthur mandal is one of the 19 mandals in the West Godavari district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is administered under the Narasapuram revenue division.
  • Mogalthuru — Mogalthuru is a village in West Godavari district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It was a princely estate during the British rule.
  • Gollapalem Island — Chinna Gollapalem Island is a river delta island formed by the Upputeru Rivulet in Andhra Pradesh, India. The island is located in the Kruthivennu mandal of Krishna District.
  • Kalipatnam — Kalipatnam is a village in West godavari district of the Indian state Andhra Pradesh. Lankalakoderu railway Station, Narasapur railway Station are the nearest railway stations.
