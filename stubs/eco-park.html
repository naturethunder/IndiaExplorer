Eco Park

State: West Bengal | Category: nature | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Eco Park (Prakriti Tirtha) is an expansive 480-acre urban park situated in New Town, Rajarhat, Kolkata. Surrounding a majestic 104-acre waterbody with Sabuj Sathi island at its core, the park features iconic replicas of the Seven Wonders of the World (including the Eiffel Tower, Taj Mahal, and Great Pyramid of Giza), lakeside boating and water sports, themed botanical pavilions like the Rose Garden and Bamboo Forest, and sprawling cycling promenades.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Seven Wonders of the World Replica Zone — Monumental zone featuring faithful architectural replicas of the Seven Wonders of the World: the Great Pyramid of Giza, Eiffel Tower, Roman Colosseum, Christ the Redeemer, Taj Mahal, Petra, and Easter Island Moai statues.
  • Sabuj Sathi Lake & Eco Island — Expansive 104-acre freshwater lake offering pedal boats, swan boats, shikaras, and kayaking, with an elevated wooden walkway connecting the shoreline to the glass-house restaurant on central Sabuj Sathi Island.
  • Themed Botanical Gardens & Butterfly Park — Curated collection of specialized horticultural gardens including a rich Rose Garden, serene Japanese Garden with pagoda pavilions, misty Bamboo Forest boardwalks, and a tropical Butterfly Conservatory.
