Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary and Mollem National Park

State: Goa | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary and Mollem National Park is a 240 square kilometres (93 sq mi) protected area located in the Western Ghats of West India, in Dharbandora taluk, Goa State, along the eastern border with Karnataka. The area is situated near the town of Molem, 57 kilometres (35 mi) east of Panaji, the state capital of Goa. National Highway 4A divides it into two parts and the Mormugao - Londa railway line passes through the area. It is located between 15°15"30' to 15°29"30' N and 74°10"15' to 74°20"15' E. Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mollem, Goa — Mollem (pronounced mole; the alphabet m is silent) is a small village in Goa,on NH4 near the border with Karnataka in the foothills of the Sahyadri Mountain range at the beginning of Anmod Ghat. It comes under the Dharbandora block.
  • Braganza Ghats — Braganza Ghats are a stretch of hill section in the Western Ghats alias Sahyadri Range at the Karnataka – Goa border. This 26 km ghat section rail road connects coastal Goa to the hinterlands of Karnataka and other parts.
  • Mahadev Temple, Tambdi Surla — Mahadeva Temple, Tambdi Surla is a 13th-century Hindu temple in the Kadamba style dedicated to Lord Shiva, one of the main deities in Hinduism, and is an active place of Hindu worship. It is an ASI protected Monument of National Importance in Goa.
  • Castle Rock, Karnataka — Castle Rock is a village in the Uttara Kannada district of the Indian state of Karnataka. The village is located in the Western Ghats on the state's border with Goa - at an elevation of 621 m (2,040 ft).
  • Anmod — Anmod (officially recorded as Anamod) is a village in the Supa (Joida) taluk of Uttara Kannada district in the Indian state of Karnataka. Situated directly on the border between Goa and Karnataka, it serves as a primary transit point along the Belgaum-Goa route via the Anmod Ghat.
  • Dudhsagar Falls — Dudhsagar Falls (lit. 'Sea of Milk') is a four-tiered waterfall on the Mahadayi River in the Indian state of Goa. It is 60km from Panaji by road and is located on the Belagavi–Vasco Da Gama rail route about 46km east of Madgaon and 80km south of Belagavi .
