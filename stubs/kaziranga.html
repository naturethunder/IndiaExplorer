Kaziranga

State: Assam | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Apr

Kaziranga National Park is one of India's greatest wildlife success stories. It hosts two-thirds of the world's great one-horned rhinoceros population, along with tigers, wild elephants, wild water buffalo and swamp deer. UNESCO and a Tiger Reserve.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Elephant Safari — Best way to see rhinos up close. Morning elephant back safari through tall grass.
  • Jeep Safari (Central Range) — Cover more ground by jeep. Multiple ranges: Central (best), Western, Eastern.
  • Kaziranga Orchid Park — 500+ orchid species from Northeast India. Also butterfly garden and birds.
  • Western Range (Bagori) — Second best range. Excellent sightings of rhinos in tall elephant grass. Less crowded.
  • Brahmaputra River Boat Ride — Gentle Brahmaputra cruise. Spot river dolphins, migratory birds and Assam scenery.
  • Kohora Market — Main market town near Kaziranga. Best place for Assamese food, muga silk and souvenirs.
