Sultanpur National Park

State: Haryana | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sultanpur National Park is a national park in Haryana, India, covering approximately 1.43 km2 (0.55 sq mi).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Garhi Harsaru — Garhi Harsaru is a town and railway junction in northern Gurugram district, Haryana, India. An inland container depot, Garhi Harsaru ICD, is located near the Garhi Harsaru Junction railway station.
  • Patli Hajipur — Patli Hajipur is a village in Gurgaon district of the Indian state of Haryana. It is located in Faruknagar taluk.
  • Dhankot — Dhankot is a village in Gurgaon, Gurgaon District, Haryana, in India. Dhankot is 6.842 km distance from its mandal main town Gurgaon.
  • Baoli Ghaus Ali Shah — Baoli Ghaus Ali Shah, also known as Ghaus Khan ki Baoli, is an 18th-century stepwell located in Farrukhnagar, Gurugram district, Haryana, India. Constructed during the reign of Mughal Emperor Farrukhsiyar, it was commissioned by Ghaus Ali Shah, a local chief who served under the emperor.
  • Dhorka — Dhorka is a village in Gurgaon district and the eponymous tehsil in the Indian state of Haryana. It is one of 38 villages in Gurgaon Block.
  • Farrukhnagar — Farrukhnagar is a small town and municipality in Farrukhnagar tehsil of Gurugram district in the Indian state of Haryana. It is situated 21 kilometres (13 mi) from Gurgaon and shares its border with Jhajjar district.
