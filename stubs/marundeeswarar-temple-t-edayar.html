Marundeeswarar Temple, T. Edayar

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Marudeeswarar Temple (மருதீஸ்வரர் கோயில்) (also called Idayatrunathar Temple) is a Hindu temple dedicated to the deity Shiva, located in T. Edaiyar, a village in Viluppuram district in the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu. Shiva is worshipped as Marudeeswarar, and is represented by the lingam. His consort Parvati is depicted as Gnanambigai. The temple is located on the Southern banks of Thenpennai River on the Thirukoilur - Thiruvennainallur road.
