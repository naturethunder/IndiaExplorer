Kappatagudda Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Karnataka | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kappatagudda Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary located in Gadag district in the Indian state of Karnataka. It was formed in 2019 and it spans 244.15 km2 (94.27 sq mi) over dry deciduous hills across Gadag, Mundargi, and Shirahatti taluks.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tippapura — Tippapura also spelled as Thippapura, is a village in the Mundargi taluk of Gadag district in the Indian state of Karnataka. Tippapura is located south to district headquarters Gadag and Taluka headquarters Mundargi.
