Soundareswararswamy Temple, Thiruppanaiyur

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Soundareswararswamy Temple or Sountharyanathar temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to the God Shiva. It is situated in the village of Thiruppanaiyur, three and a half kilometres (2.2 mi) from Nannilam. The temple is centred on a palmyra tree which is the sthalavrksham. The palmyra tree gives its name to the village itself ("Thiru" (holy) + "Panai" (palm) + "Ur" (city) or the "city of the holy palm").

The temple is dedicated to Shiva, as the moolavar presiding deity, in his manifestation as Pasupatheeswarar.

The presiding deity is Shiva in the form of Veerateswarar and his consort is known as Elavar Kuzhali.
