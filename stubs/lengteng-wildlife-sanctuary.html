Lengteng Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Mizoram | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Lengteng Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area in Saitual district in eastern Mizoram, northeast India. It is an alpine forest and contains the second highest peak in Mizoram. It is specially a conservation interest on rare species of birds. It was declared a protected area in 1999, and a national wildlife sanctuary by the Indian Ministry of Environment and Forests on 31 May 2001.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Lamzawl — Lamzawl is a serene village in the Saitual/Champhai region of Mizoram, surrounded by lush river streams and emerald mountain terraces.
  • Ngopa — Ngopa is a prominent hillside town in northeastern Mizoram, serving as the central administrative hub near the Lengteng wildlife conservation corridor.
  • Selam, Champhai — A scenic highland settlement renowned for traditional Mizo stone architecture, high altitude bamboo groves, and unspoiled nature trails.
  • Kawlbem — An authentic Mizo village nestled on a high ridge overlooking deep forested ravines and clear mountain streams flowing toward the Tiau river.
