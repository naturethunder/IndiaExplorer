Vantawng Falls

State: Mizoram | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Vantawng Khawhthla is located 5 kilometres (3.1 mi) south of Thenzawl in Serchhip district in the Indian state of Mizoram. It is the highest uninterrupted waterfall in Mizoram. It is about 92 kilometres (57 miles) away from Aizawl.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tuirihiau Falls — A magical natural curtain waterfall near Thenzawl where visitors can stand behind the falling water inside a sheltered concave rock cavern.
  • Thenzawl Handloom Town & Deer Park — A picturesque tableland town known as Mizoram's cultural weaving center, featuring open green meadows, pine groves, and peaceful rural life.
