Palakkad Fort

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Palakkad Fort is an old fort situated in the heart of Palakkad city in Kerala state, southern India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Fort Maidan — Fort Maidan, also known as Kota Maidanam, is a multi-use stadium in Palakkad, India. It is currently used mostly for cricket matches.
  • Diocese of Sultanpet — Roman Catholic Diocese of Sultanpet (Latin: Dioecesis Sultanpetensis) is a Roman Catholic diocese consisting of Palakkad district, Kerala, India erected from the division of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Coimbatore and Roman Catholic Diocese of Calicut, and a suffragan of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Verapoly.   • Yakkara — Yakkara, is an area in the city of Palakkad, Kerala, India. It is located approximately 4 kilometers from the city centre and is predominantly segregated as West Yakkara and East Yakkara.Government Medical College, Palakkad is located in East Yakkara.
  • Manapullikavu — Manapullikavu is a Hindu temple located in Palakkad city, Kerala of India. The temple is most famous for the annual festival that takes place in the temple known as Manappullikavu Vela.
  • Stadium Bus Stand Palakkad — Stadium Bus Stand is located on the heart of the Palakkad City, near the Indira Gandhi Municipal Stadium in Kerala, India. Stadium Bus Stand is the largest Private Bus Stand in the city of Palakkad.
  • Palakkad — Palakkad (Malayalam: [pɐːlɐkːɐːɖɨ̆] ), also known as Palghat, historically known as Palakkattussery, is a City in the Indian state of Kerala. It is the administrative headquarters of Palakkad District.
