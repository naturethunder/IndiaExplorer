Gurdwara Nanaklama

State: Sikkim | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Gurdwara Nanaklama is a heritage landmark located in Sikkim, India. Visitors come here for historic architecture, cultural heritage and scenic views. Nearby places worth exploring include Chungthang, Lachen River and Chungthang Forest Block. The best time to visit is Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chungthang — Chungthang is a town in Mangan district in the Indian state of Sikkim. It is located just 28 km from Mangan town, the district headquarters.
  • Lachen River — The Lachen River is a tributary of the Teesta River in the state of Sikkim, India. It is one of the two main tributaries of the Teesta along with the Lachung River, which rise on opposite sides of the Donga Range in neighbouring Bhutan and converge in Chungthang in the North Sikkim district.
  • Chungthang Forest Block — Chungthang Forest Block is a village in Chungthang subdivision, North Sikkim district, Sikkim, India. The Ministry of Home Affairs has given it a geographical code of 260867.
  • Shipgyer — Shipgyer is a village in Chungthang subdivision, North Sikkim district, Sikkim, India. The Ministry of Home Affairs has given it a geographical code of 260862.
  • Naga-Namgor — Naga-Namgor is a village in Mangan subdivision, North Sikkim district, Sikkim, India. The Ministry of Home Affairs has given it a geographical code of 260868.
  • Tung, Sikkim — Tung is a village in the Chungthang subdivision of North Sikkim district in the north Indian state of Sikkim. The Ministry of Home Affairs has given it a geographical code of 260863.
