Therthangal Bird Sanctuary

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Therthangal Bird Sanctuary is a bird sanctuary in Ramanathapuram district in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. Established in 2010, it is spread across an area of 29.295 ha (72.39 acres). It has been designated as a protected Ramsar site since 2024.
