St. Joseph’s Parish Shrine, Pavaratty

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Joseph's Parish Shrine, Pavaratty is a Christian church located in Kerala, India. Located in a small town named Pavaratty, the church is popularly known as Pavaratty Palli. It is a parish of Syro-malabar traditions under the auspices of the Syro Malabar Catholic Archeparchy of Thrissur.
