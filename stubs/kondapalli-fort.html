Kondapalli Fort

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kondapalli Fort, also locally known as Kondapalli Kota and Kondapalli Quilla, lies to the west of Kondapalli in Ibrahimpatnam mandal of NTR district in Andhra Pradesh, India was built by Prolaya Vema Reddy of Reddi Kingdom during the 14th century CE. It was initially built as a leisure place and business center and later served as a military training base for the British rulers. Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kondapalli — Kondapalli is an industrial and residential hub situated in the western part of Vijayawada in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. Dr Narla Tata Rao Thermal Power Station also known as Vijayawada Thermal Power Station (VTPS) is one of the major Thermal Power stations of the state is located in between Ibrahimpatnam and Kondapalli.
  • Ibrahimpatnam — Ibrahimpatnam (known locally as IBM) is a part of Vijayawada in NTR district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is also the mandal headquarters of Ibrahimpatnam mandal.
  • Budameru Rivulet — Budameru is a rivulet in NTR district which originates in the hills surrounding Mylavaram and empties itself into Kolleru Lake. Budameru is also known as The Sorrow of Vijayawada.
  • Kondapalli Toy Crafts Village — Kondapalli village is celebrated worldwide for its 400-year-old GI-tagged wooden toys handcrafted from local Tella Poniki softwood by traditional artisan guilds.
  • Elaprolu — Elaprolu is a village in NTR district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is located in Ibrahimpatnam mandal of Vijayawada revenue division.
  • Kavuluru — Kavuluru is a village near Vijayawada and Kondapalli in the NTR district of Andhra Pradesh in India.
