Siddheshwar & Ratneshwar Temple

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Siddheshwar, and Ratneshwar Temple is situated about 2 kilometres (1.2 mi) from Latur City of Maharashtra, India. The temple was built by King Tamradwaj. There is a fair every year during Mahashivratri.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mamadapur, Latur — Mamadapur is a panchayat village in the Latur taluka of Latur district in the western state of Maharashtra, India. Mamadapur is the only village listed for the gram panchayat.
  • Babhalgaon — Babhalgaon is a village in Latur district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
  • Sakol — Sakol is a panchayat village in Shirur Anantpal Taluka in Latur subdivision of Latur district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. The village of Sakol is 12 km by road southeast of the village of Shirur Anantpal and 40 km by road northeast of the town of Nilanga.
