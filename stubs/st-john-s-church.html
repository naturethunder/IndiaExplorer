St. John's Church

State: West Bengal | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. John's Church, Midnapore is a church made by the Church Mission Society of England. It was built in 1851 at Sekpura in Midnapore, Paschim Medinipur district in the Indian state of West Bengal. There are several notable graves inside the church cemetery.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Midnapore — Midnapore (Bengali pronunciation: [medinipur]), is a city known for its history in the Indian state of West Bengal. It is the headquarters of the Paschim Medinipur district.
  • Medinipur division — Medinipur division is one of the 5 divisions in the Indian state of West Bengal. It is the westernmost division of West Bengal.
  • Vidyasagar Shishu Niketan — Vidyasagar Shishu Niketan is a private primary and higher secondary English-medium school in Midnapore in West Bengal, India. Established in 1975, the school is spread across two campuses in the city, and run by the Society for the Betterment of Education, a local group under the supervision of the district administration.
  • Midnapore Sadar (community development block) — Midnapore Sadar is a community development block that forms an administrative division in Medinipur Sadar subdivision of Paschim Medinipur district in the Indian state of West Bengal.
  • Vidyasagar Vidyapith — Vidyasagar Vidyapith Boys' School, popularly known as Bangla School, is one of the oldest school located in Midnapore town, West Bengal, India. This school was established with the blessings of Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar, the 19th century educationist and social reformer of Bengal, on 22 August 1845.
  • Matkatpur — Matkatpur (also called Matkadpur) is a village in Kharagpur I CD Block in Kharagpur subdivision of Paschim Medinipur district in the state of West Bengal, India.
