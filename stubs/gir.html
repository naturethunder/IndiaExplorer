Gir National Park

State: Gujarat | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Dec – Mar

Gir National Park, in Saurashtra, Gujarat, is one of India’s favourite wildlife reserve. Visitors come for its asiatic lions, safari, forest, with the best months being Dec – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gir Lion Safari — The only place on earth to see Asiatic lions in the wild.
  • Devalia Safari Park — Fenced interpretation zone with near-guaranteed sightings.
  • Kankai Mata Temple — Forest temple deep inside the Gir sanctuary.
