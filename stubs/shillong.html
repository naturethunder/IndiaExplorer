Shillong

State: Meghalaya | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Shillong, in Khasi Hills, Meghalaya, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its scotland of east, waterfalls, living roots, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Elephant Falls — Three-tiered waterfall on the city outskirts.
  • Umiam Lake — Vast reservoir with kayaking and scenic viewpoints.
  • Shillong Peak — Highest point with panoramic views of the city and hills.
