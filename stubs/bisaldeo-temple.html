Bisaldeo temple

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bisaldeo temple, also known as Bisaldev temple or Bisal Deoji's temple, is a Hindu temple in Bisalpur, India. It is located beside the Bisalpur Dam on the Banas River, in the Tonk district of Rajasthan state. The temple is dedicated to Gokarneshvara, an aspect of Shiva. A Monument of National Importance, it was commissioned by the 12th century Chahamana ruler Vigraharaja IV, who is also known as Bisal Deo.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bisalpur Dam — Bisalpur Dam is a gravity dam on the Banas River near Bisalpur in Tonk district, Rajasthan, India. The dam was completed in 1999 for the purpose of irrigation and water supply.
  • Dai River (India) — River Dai originates in the southeastern slopes of the Aravalli Range near Nasirabad Tehsil of Ajmer District. It flows southeast for about 40 km and east for about 56 km in Ajmer District, and for a short reach through Tonk District, before joining the Banas River near Bisalpur village in Tonk District.
  • Beesalpur — Beesalpur is a village in Deoli tehsil, Tonk district, Rajasthan, India, located at 31 km from main Deoli city. The population was 4,106 at the 2011 Indian census.
