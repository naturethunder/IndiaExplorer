Ladakh

State: Ladakh | Category: adventure | Best Time: Jun – Sep

Ladakh is unlike anywhere else on earth. At an average altitude of 3,500m, this high-altitude cold desert offers dramatic landscapes of barren mountains, turquoise lakes, and ancient Buddhist monasteries. Leh is the gateway to a dozen breathtaking destinations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pangong Tso Lake — The iconic 134km long lake at 4,350m. Colours shift from blue to green to red.
  • Khardung La Pass — World's highest motorable road at 5,359m. Permit required from DC office.
  • Thiksey Monastery — Tiered monastery perched on a hill. 12-storey gompa with stunning valley views.
  • Nubra Valley — Double-humped Bactrian camels on sand dunes at 3,000m. Via Khardung La.
  • Shanti Stupa — White-domed Buddhist stupa on a hilltop. Best sunrise and sunset spot in Leh.
  • Hemis Monastery — Largest and wealthiest monastery in Ladakh. Annual Hemis Festival in June/July.
