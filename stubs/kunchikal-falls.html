Kunchikal Falls

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kunchikal Falls is a waterfall in India located in the Nidagodu village near Yadur-Masthikatte in the Shimoga district of Karnataka state. The waterfall cascades down rocky boulders and the total height of the falls is 455 meters (1,493 feet), according to the World Waterfall Database. Kunchikal Falls is formed by the Varahi River. After the construction of the Mani Dam near Sulagodu and an underground power generation station near Hosangadi, Udupi district, the water flow to the falls has greatly reduced and is visible only during the rainy season (July-Sept).
