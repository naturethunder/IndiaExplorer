Kalpa

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct

Kalpa, in Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its kinnaur kailash, apple orchards, suicide point, with the best months being Apr – Jun, Sep – Oct.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kinnaur Kailash View — Front view of the sacred 6,050m Kinnaur Kailash range.
  • Suicide Point — Sheer cliff viewpoint over the Sutlej valley.
  • Roghi Village — Traditional Kinnauri village amid apple orchards.
