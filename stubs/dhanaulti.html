Dhanaulti

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Year Round

Dhanaulti is a quiet hill station at 7,500 feet located 24 km beyond Mussoorie in Uttarakhand. Offers quiet forest walks inside Eco Parks and panoramic Himalayan snow views.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Amber & Dhara Eco Parks — Protected deodar forest park with walking trails, skywalks and zip-lining rides.
  • Surkanda Devi Temple & Cable Car — High altitude Shakti Peeth temple (2,757m) offering 360-degree snow peak vistas.
  • Potato Farm & Himalayan Viewpoint — Terraced organic potato fields facing snow-capped Garhwal Himalayan peaks.
  • Tehri Dam Backwaters & Water Sports — Highest dam reservoir in India featuring jet-skiing, speedboats, and floating huts.
  • Kodia Jungle Forest Trek — Quiet natural spring forest trail home to barking deer and wild pheasants.
  • Kanatal Ridge Viewpoint — Peaceful pine-covered hill town between Dhanaulti and Chamba.
