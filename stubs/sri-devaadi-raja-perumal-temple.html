Sri Devaadi Raja Perumal Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Devaadi Raja Perumal temple (Tamil: தேவாதிராஜன் பெருமாள் கோயில்) (also called Amaruviappan temple) in Therazhundur, a village in Mayiladuthurai district of the South Indian state of Tamil Nadu, is dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu. Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, the temple is glorified in the Naalayira Divya Prabandham, the early medieval Tamil canon of the Alvar saints from the 6th–9th centuries CE.
