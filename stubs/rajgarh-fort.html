Rajgarh Fort

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Rajgarh Fort, also known as hill ‌Fort, ‌is a historic‌ fortification‌ situated‌ in ‌the ‌Aravalli Hills‌ ‌near Rajgarh‌ town‌, Alwar district, Rajasthan, India, at approximately 27°14'N 76°37'E, with an elevation of 479 meters (1,571 feet). It was constructed in the 1730s and 1740s by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh the kachwaha ruler of Jaipur. The fort, now in ruins, is maintained by the Archaeological Survey of India.
