Puri

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Puri, in Coromandel Coast, Odisha, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its jagannath, beach, rath yatra, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jagannath Temple — One of the Char Dham, home of the famous Rath Yatra chariots.
  • Puri Beach — Wide golden beach famed for sunrise and sand art.
  • Chilika Lake — Asia’s largest brackish lagoon with dolphins and migratory birds.
