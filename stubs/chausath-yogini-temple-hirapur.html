Chausath Yogini Temple, Hirapur

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Chausathi Yogini Temple (64-Yogini Temple) of Hirapur, also said Mahamaya Temple, is 20 km outside Bhubaneswar, the capital of Odisha state of Eastern India. It devotes to the worship of the yoginis, auspicious goddess-like figures.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pratapsasan — Pratapsasan is a census town in Khordha district in the Indian state of Odisha.
  • Tosali — Tosali or Toshali was an ancient city in the present day Odisha state in eastern India. It was the capital of the eastern province of the Kalinga Kingdom.
  • Sisupalgarh — Sisupalgarh or Sisupalagada (Odia: [sisupaːl̪ɔɡɔɽɔ] ) is situated in Khurda District in Odisha, India, and houses ruined fortifications. First inhabited around 7th to 6th centuries BCE, it is one of the largest and best-preserved early historic fortifications in India, and was once the capital of ancient Kalinga.
  • Mahabhoi Sasana Tank — Mahabhoi Sasana Tank is situated on the right bank of the stream Gangua. It is mentioned as a tank in the forgotten monuments of Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India.
  • Madneswar Siva Temple — Madanesvara Siva Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Siva built around 12th century CE. It is situated on the left side of the Mahavir Lane branching from Garage Chowk in Santarapur to Sisupalgarh, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. The enshrined deity is a Siva lingam within a circular yonipitha (basement).
  • Brahmeswara Temple — Brahmeswara Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India, erected at the end of the 9th century CE, is richly carved inside and out. This Hindu temple can be dated with fair accuracy by the use of inscriptions that were originally on the temple.
