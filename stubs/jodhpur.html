Jodhpur

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Jodhpur, in Marwar, Rajasthan, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its blue city, mehrangarh, forts, markets, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mehrangarh Fort — One of India’s largest forts, towering 122m over the blue city.
  • Jaswant Thada — Marble royal cenotaph beside a lake, the “Taj of Marwar”.
  • Umaid Bhawan Palace — Art-deco royal palace, part museum and part Taj hotel.
