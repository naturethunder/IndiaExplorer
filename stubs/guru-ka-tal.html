Guru ka Tal

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Guru ka Tal is a historical Sikh pilgrimage place dedicated to the memory of ninth Guru Sri Guru Tegh Bahudar Ji. Guru ka Taal is near Sikandra, in Agra. The Gurudwara was built over the place where the Guru Tegh Bahadur offered voluntary arrest to Aurangazeb, the Mughal Emperor. Several devotees gather every year to pay homage to the great Sikh guru (who was martyred along with his followers for sake of freedom to practice one's faith) in this Gurudwara.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kakraita — Kakraita, also known as Kakrahta or Kakrentha, is a village of Sikandra block of Agra District in Uttar Pradesh, India.
  • Akbar's tomb — Akbar's tomb is the mausoleum of the third and greatest Mughal emperor Akbar. The tomb was built in 1605–1613 by his son, Jahangir and is situated on 119 acres of grounds in Sikandra, a suburb of Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India.
  • Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani — The Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani or Mariam's tomb is the mausoleum of Mariam-uz-Zamani, commonly known by the misnomer Jodha Bai, the favorite wife of the Mughal Emperor Akbar. The tomb was built by her son Jahangir, in her memory between years 1623–1627 and is located in Agra's Sikandra suburb next to Akbar's tomb, in the direction of Mathura.
  • List of tombs of Mughal Empire — Mughal tombs are a set of tombs built by various ruling in Mughal Emperors. All of them have marked influence from Iranian Timurid forms.
  • Swamibagh — Soamibagh is a town and a nagar panchayat in Agra district in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh.
  • Sanjay Place — Sanjay Place, is a Business District in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is a big market of electrical, communication, electronic items.
