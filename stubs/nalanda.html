Nalanda

State: Bihar | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Nalanda, in Magadh, Bihar, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its ancient university, ruins, unesco, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nalanda University Ruins — UNESCO ruins of the world’s oldest residential university.
  • Nalanda Archaeological Museum — Buddhist bronzes and stuccos excavated from the site.
  • Xuanzang Memorial Hall — Memorial to the Chinese pilgrim-scholar who studied here.
