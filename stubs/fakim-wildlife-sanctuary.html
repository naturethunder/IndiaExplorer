Fakim Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Nagaland | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Fakim Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area in Nagaland, India. It is situated close to the India-Myanmar border in Pungro circle of Kiphire district, and covers an area of 642 ha (1,590 acres). Fakim village is the gateway to the sanctuary. It was established in 1983.
