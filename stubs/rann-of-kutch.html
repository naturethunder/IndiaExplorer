Rann of Kutch

State: Gujarat | Category: adventure | Best Time: Nov – Feb (during Rann Utsav)

The Great Rann of Kutch is the world's largest salt desert — a vast, blinding white expanse that stretches to the horizon. The annual Rann Utsav (Nov–Feb) transforms this remote region with cultural celebrations, folk music and tent city accommodation.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • White Rann Sunset Point — The great white expanse at sunset turns golden pink. Otherworldly experience.
  • Rann Utsav Tent City — Government-run luxury tent city during festival season. Folk music every night.
  • Dholavira — UNESCO Harappan city — 5,000 years old. Extraordinary water management system.
  • Kalo Dungar (Black Hills) — Highest point in Kutch at 462m. Magnificent views of the white salt desert stretching to horizon.
  • Flamingo City, Little Rann — 50,000+ flamingos roosting at a seasonal wetland. One of India's greatest wildlife spectacles.
  • Mandvi Beach — Clean, uncrowded beach with traditional wooden dhow boat-building on the shore.
