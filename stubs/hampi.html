Hampi

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Mar

Hampi is one of India's most dramatic UNESCO World Heritage Sites. The ruins of the 14th-century Vijayanagara capital spread across 4,100 hectares of surreal boulder-strewn landscape along the Tungabhadra river. The Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple and Hampi Bazaar are the highlights.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Vittala Temple Complex — The Stone Chariot and musical pillars. The most iconic image of Hampi.
  • Virupaksha Temple — Living temple continuously worshipped since 7th century. 9-storey gopura.
  • Matanga Hill Sunrise — Best sunrise spot in Hampi. 360° panorama of ruins, boulders and the Tungabhadra.
  • Lotus Mahal & Elephant Stables — Elegant Indo-Islamic Lotus Mahal beside the grand 11-domed elephant stables.
  • Queen's Bath — Royal bathing complex with a moat. Ornate balconies and arched corridors.
  • Coracle Ride on Tungabhadra — Circular boat ride across the Tungabhadra. Links main Hampi with Hippie Island.
