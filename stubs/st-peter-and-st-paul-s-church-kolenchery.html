St. Peter and St. Paul's Church, Kolenchery

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Peter and St. Paul's Church, Kolenchery is a sacred temple located in Kerala, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Kolenchery, Aikaranad South and Kottoor. The best time to visit is Nov – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kolenchery — Kolenchery is a town in the Poothrikka gram panchayat in Ernakulam district, Kerala. Located on National Highway 85, Kolenchery is situated 3 km (1.9 mi) from Poothrikka and 20 km (12 mi) from Kochi.
  • Aikaranad South — Aikaranad South is a village in Ernakulam district in the Indian state of Kerala. Aikaranad South is also known as Poothrikka.
  • Kottoor — Kottoor Church or Kottoor St. George Orthodox Syrian Church is a chapel of Kolenchery Church.
  • Aikkaranad — Aikaranad Grama panchayat is a Grama panchayat in Ernakulam district in the Indian state of Kerala. Aikaranad Grama panchayat is situated in Vadavukode Block panchayat.
  • Kadayiruppu — Kadayiruppu is a village on the outskirts of Kolenchery town in Kerala, India. Kadayiruppu located 4 km from Kolenchery on Perumbavoor Road.
  • Pancode — Pancode is a small village in Ernakulam district of the state of Kerala in India. Pancode is just 16 km away from the prestigious Smart City Project and InfoPark, Kochi.
