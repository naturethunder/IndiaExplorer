Loktak Lake

State: Manipur | Category: nature | Best Time: Oct – Apr

Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, famous for its unique circular floating biomass islands called 'phumdis'. On the largest phumdi lies Keibul Lamjao National Park.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Keibul Lamjao Floating National Park — World's only floating national park on phumdi rings home to the endangered Sangai dancing deer.
  • Sendra Island 360 Viewpoint — Elevated island inside Loktak lake offering panoramic views of circular floating biomass rings.
  • Moirang INA Memorial & Museum — Historic site where Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose's INA tricolor flag was first unfurled in 1944.
  • Karang Cashless Island Village — India's first cashless island fishing village located in the middle of Loktak lake.
  • Loukoipat Ecological Park — Tranquil natural lake pond surrounded by lotus blossoms and forested hills.
  • Tharon & Khangkhui Caves — Prehistoric limestone cave formations near Bishnupur.
