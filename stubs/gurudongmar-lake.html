Gurudongmar Lake

State: Sikkim | Category: adventure | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Oct – Nov

Gurudongmar Lake is a magnificent high-altitude sacred lake situated at 17,800ft in North Sikkim near the Tibetan border. Surrounded by perpetual snow peaks, its turquoise waters remain partly unfrozen even in freezing winter.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gurudongmar Lake Shore — Vast sacred turquoise glacial lake surrounded by snow-covered Himalayan peaks.
