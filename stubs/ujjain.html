Ujjain

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ujjain, in Malwa, Madhya Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its mahakaleshwar, kumbh, shipra, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mahakaleshwar Temple — Jyotirlinga temple famed for its dawn Bhasma Aarti.
  • Ram Ghat — Sacred Shipra-river ghat, a focal point of the Simhastha Kumbh.
  • Kal Bhairav Temple — Unusual temple where liquor is offered to the deity.
