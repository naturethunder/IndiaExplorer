Madaksira hill fort

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Madakasira Fort, also known as Simhagiri, is a hill fort located in the Madakasira town of Sri Sathya Sai district, in Andhra Pradesh, India. The Government of India has designated it as a Monument of National Importance.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Madakasira — Madakasira is a town with a population 19,432 in 2001 and also a mandal in Sri Satya Sai district in the state of Andhra Pradesh in India. It is located near the state border with Karnataka.
