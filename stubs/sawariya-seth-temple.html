Sawariya Seth Temple

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sawariya Seth Temple, popularly known as Sanwaliya Seth or Shri Sanwaliya Ji Maharaj Temple, is one of the most revered Hindu pilgrimage temples of Rajasthan, located at Mandaphia village in Chittorgarh district. The temple is dedicated to Lord Krishna in the form of a dark-complexioned merchant or Seth, believed to fulfil the worldly and spiritual wishes of devotees. The main marble temple complex features three sanctum chambers housing distinct idols of Krishna — Sanwaliya Seth Ji, Sanwaliya Bihari Ji, and Sanwaliya Lala Ji. Tens of thousands of pilgrims visit every week, particularly on Ekadashi and during the Kartik Mela fair. The temple is managed by a government trust and maintains a unique tradition where devotees deposit offerings of cash, gold, and silver as symbolic business dealings with the deity.
