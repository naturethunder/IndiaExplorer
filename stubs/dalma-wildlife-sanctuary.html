Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Jharkhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary in the Indian state of Jharkhand. It was inaugurated in 1975 and provides habitat for an Indian elephant population.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bhumij Revolt — The Bhumij Revolt (also known as Ganga Narayan's Hungama, Jungle Mahal Uprising) was revolt of Bhumij tribals, led by Ganga Narayan Singh during 1832–33 in the Manbhum and Jungle Mahal areas of the erstwhile Midnapore district, Bengal. The British have called it "Ganga Narain's Hangama" while historians have also written it as the Chuar rebellion.
  • Dimna Lake — Dimna Lake is an artificial reservoir located near Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary, 15 km from the city of Jamshedpur, in the state of Jharkhand, India. Constructed in 1944, by Tata Steel to deal with the water scarcity in the city.
  • Kopali — Kopali (also spelled as Kapali) is a census town in the Chandil CD block in the Chandil subdivision of the Seraikela Kharsawan district in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
  • Al Kabir Polytechnic — Al-Kabir Polytechnic, also known as Al Kabir Institute of Management and Technology, is a private institute in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, India. Established in 1990 by Abdul Hakim, it is one of the oldest private polytechnic institutes in eastern India.
  • Mango (Jamshedpur) — Mango is a suburb in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand. The city is situated across the Subarnarkeha River and is connected to Jamshedpur via three bridges laid side by side.
  • Boram block — Boram block is a CD block that forms an administrative division in the Dhalbhum subdivision of East Singhbhum district, in the Indian state of Jharkhand.
