Padiyanoor Devi Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Padiyanoor Sree Chamundi Devi Temple or Padiyanoor Devi Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Chamundi located in Thiruvananthapuram, India. This ancient temple, is situated at Poovachal, around 30 km from Thiruvananthapuram city.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kuttichal — Kuttichal (formerly known as Mannoorkara) is a village in Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India. Kuttichal is part of Kattakada Taluk, Vellanad block panchayath and Aruvikkara Legislative Assembly.
  • Kulathummal — Kulathummal is a village in Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Kattakada — Kattakada is a town in Thiruvananthapuram district of Kerala. It is situated 13 km north of Neyyattinkara town, 18 km south of Nedumangad town and 20 km east of the Capital City, Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Kootayanimoodu Chamundi Temple — Kootayanimoodu sri Chamundeswari temple is a Hindu temple, This ancient temple dates back to the 18th century. The temple is dedicated to Chamundeswari Devi, who is a form of Durga / Shakti.The temple is located in kootayanimoodu, Vellanadu panchayat, Thiruvananthapuram district, Kerala and is one of the oldest temples in the state.
  • Vellanad — Vellanad is a village in the Nedumangad Taluk district of Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India.
