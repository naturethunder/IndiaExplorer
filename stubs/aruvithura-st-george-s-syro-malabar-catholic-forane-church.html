Aruvithura St. George's Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. George's Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church, Aruvithura, more familiarly known as Aruvithura Church, is a Syro-Malabar Catholic church located at Aruvithura, in Kottayam district, Kerala, India. Traditionally, it is believed that the Syrian Catholic Church at Aruvithura was established in the 1st century. The first edifice of the church at Aruvithura was made of granite stones in the manner of Hindu temples.
