Jim Corbett

State: Uttarakhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Jun

Jim Corbett, in Nainital, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite wildlife reserve. Visitors come for its tigers, jeep safari, ramganga, elephants, with the best months being Nov – Jun.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Dhikala Zone Safari — India’s oldest national park core zone, prime tiger territory.
  • Corbett Falls — A 20m forest waterfall surrounded by dense sal woods.
  • Garjia Devi Temple — Riverside temple set atop a rock in the Kosi river.
