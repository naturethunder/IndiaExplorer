Coorg

State: Karnataka | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Year-round

Coorg (Kodagu) is a district in the Western Ghats known for its coffee plantations, spice estates, and the proudly independent Kodava people. Its misty hills, cascading waterfalls, and thick forests make it one of South India's most visited hill stations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Abbey Falls — Beautiful 70ft waterfall surrounded by coffee estates and spice plantations.
  • Raja's Seat — Hilltop garden with spectacular sunset views over the Coorg valleys.
  • Nagarhole National Park — Tiger reserve with elephants, leopards and rich birdlife. Safari essential.
  • Talacauvery — Source of the Cauvery river. Sacred temple on Brahmagiri peak.
  • Iruppu Falls — 170ft waterfall on the Brahmagiri hills. Serene and less crowded than Abbey Falls.
  • Mandalpatti Peak — Remote peak at 1,500m with 360° views of Coorg valleys. Jeep ride through forest.
