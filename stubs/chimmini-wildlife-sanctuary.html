Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Kerala | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located along the Western Ghats in Chalakudy taluk of Thrissur District of Kerala state in India. Established in 1984, the sanctuary with an area of about 85.067 km2 is on the western slopes of the Nelliyampathi Hills. The highest peak in sanctuary is Punda Peak (1116 m). Along with the neighboring Peechi-Vazhani Wildlife Sanctuary it forms a continuous protected area of 210 km2.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chimmini Dam — Chimmini Dam is situated in Echippara in Chalakudy taluk of Thrissur District of Kerala state of India. It is constructed across Kurumali river, a tributary of the Karuvannur river.
  • Peechi-Vazhani Wildlife Sanctuary — Peechi-Vazhani Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary headquartered in Peechi, Thrissur District of Kerala, India. The sanctuary was established in 1958 consisting of Palappilli- Nelliyampathi forests including the area of Chimmony Wildlife sanctuary and is the second-oldest sanctuary in Kerala.
  • Akathiyoor — Akathiyoor is a census town in Thrissur district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Palakkuzhi — Palakkuzhi is a village in Alathur taluk of Palakkad district, Kerala State. Vadakkenchery is the nearest town and is 14 km away from here.
  • Vellikulangara — Vellikulangara is a village in Thrissur district in the state of Kerala, India.
