Prachitgad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Prachitgad is a fort in the Sahyadri mountain range in Sangli district of Maharashtra state, India. It covers an area of 5 acres (20,000 m2).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chandoli National Park — Chandoli National Park is a national park established in Sangli district in May 2004. Earlier it was a Wildlife Sanctuary declared in 1985.
