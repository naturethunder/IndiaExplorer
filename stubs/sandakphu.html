Sandakphu

State: West Bengal | Category: adventure | Best Time: Oct – May

Sandakphu at 11,930 feet is the highest point in West Bengal, located on the Singalila Ridge along the Nepal border. It is famous for offering an unmatched vista of four of the world's five highest peaks — Everest, Kanchenjunga, Lhotse, and Makalu.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sandakphu Peak (3,636m) — Highest point in West Bengal offering views of Everest, Kanchenjunga, Lhotse and Makalu.
  • Sleeping Buddha Kanchenjunga View — Breathtaking Himalayan snow ridge resembling a giant reclining Buddha.
  • Kalipokhri Sacred Black Lake — High altitude dark sacred mountain lake surrounded by Buddhist prayer flags.
  • Tumling Ridge & Singalila National Park — Indo-Nepal border village with red panda forest trails.
  • Phalut Ridge Trek (3,600m) — Unbroken high altitude ridge walk connecting Sandakphu to Phalut peak.
  • Manebhanjan Vintage Land Rover Hub — Base village operating historic 1950s British Land Rover fleet.
