Manali

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – Jun (avoid Jul–Aug monsoon)

Manali sits at 2,050m in the Beas River valley and is one of India's most beloved mountain destinations. From the snow-capped Rohtang Pass to the apple orchards of Solang Valley, from ancient temples to adrenaline-pumping adventure sports, Manali offers something for every kind of traveller.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Rohtang Pass — At 3,978m, offers panoramic snow views. Permit required; book in advance.
  • Solang Valley — Paragliding, zorbing, skiing in winter. Cable car to 8,500 ft.
  • Hadimba Temple — 16th century wooden temple of Hadimba Devi surrounded by cedar forest.
  • Old Manali — Hippie cafes, bakeries, backpacker hostels and Manu Temple.
  • Naggar Castle — 15th century castle turned heritage hotel. Museum and valley views.
  • Beas Kund Trek — Source of the Beas river at 3,600m. Moderate trek through glacial meadows.
