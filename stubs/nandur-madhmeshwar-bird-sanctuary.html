Nandur Madhmeshwar Bird Sanctuary

State: Maharashtra | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Nandur Madhmeshwar Bird Sanctuary is located at Niphad Tehsil of Nashik District, known as the Bharatpur of Maharashtra. It is designated as Ramsar site and it's a Maharashtra's first Ramsar site. A stone pickup is constructed across river Godavari at Nandur Madhmeshwar. This resulted into the formation of rich environment for biological diversity.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Banganga River (Maharashtra) — Banganga is a small tributary of the Godavari River in the Nashik district, in the state of Maharashtra in western India. The Banganga rises a little to the north-west of Ramsej hill and flows in a general easterly course, passing by Ozar, where a dam crosses it to divert the water into canals on both sides for irrigation.
  • Datyane — Datyane is a village in the Niphad taluka, Nashik district of Maharashtra, India, situated on the river Banganga and on the Ozar-Kasabe Sukena connecting road.
