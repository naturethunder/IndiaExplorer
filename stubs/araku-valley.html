Araku Valley

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Araku Valley, in Eastern Ghats, Andhra Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its coffee, tribal culture, train ride, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Araku Coffee Museum — Museum tracing the valley’s celebrated tribal coffee.
  • Tribal Museum — Dioramas of the Eastern Ghats’ indigenous Adivasi life.
  • Vizag–Araku Train Ride — Scenic ride through 58 tunnels and the Borra hills.
