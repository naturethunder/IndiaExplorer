Mudhai Devi temple

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mudhai Devi temple is a temple of goddess Mudhai built as a Hemadpanti structure in village Waghali, in Chalisgaon tehsil of Jalgaon district in Khandesh region of Maharashtra, India. Due to its archaeological importance, the Archaeological Survey of India declared this temple as a monument of national importance in 1914.
