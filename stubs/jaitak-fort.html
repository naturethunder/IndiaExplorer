Jaitak Fort

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Jaitak Fort is situated about 15 km from district headquarters Nahan in Sirmaur district in the state of Himachal Pradesh in India. Situated 1,479 meters above sea level, Jaitak Fort is notable due to its location and its view of the surrounding countryside due to its strategic setting.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Battle of Jaithak — The Battle of Jaithak is the subsequent battle fought by the 53rd division of East India Company after the Battle of Nalapani against Nepalese forces. Nalapani had cost both sides dearly, but in Nahan and Jaithak further west, they were to suffer more.
  • Nahan — Nahan is a town located in the Sirmaur district of the Indian state of Himachal Pradesh. It serves as the administrative headquarters of Sirmaur district.
  • Roranwali — Roranwali is a village in Hanumangarh district, Rajasthan state of western India. The town of Hanumangarh is the district headquarters.
