Kovalam

State: Kerala | Category: beach | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Kovalam, in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its lighthouse beach, ayurveda, surfing, with the best months being Sep – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Lighthouse Beach — Crescent beach watched over by a candy-striped lighthouse.
  • Hawa Beach — Palm-lined cove popular for sunrise and fishing boats.
  • Vizhinjam Marine Aquarium — Small aquarium near the historic Vizhinjam fishing harbour.
