Frog Hill View Point

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Frog Hill View Point (also Valley View Point) is a tourist spot above Gudalur, Nilgiris district, Tamil Nadu, India. It is about 12 km (7.5 mi) from Gudalur on NH 181 from Ooty to Gudalur.
