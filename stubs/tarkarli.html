Tarkarli

State: Maharashtra | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – May

Tarkarli, in Konkan Coast, Maharashtra, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its scuba, backwaters, white sand, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tarkarli Beach — Clear-water white-sand beach ideal for scuba and snorkelling.
  • Sindhudurg Fort — Shivaji’s island sea fort off the Malvan coast.
  • Devbagh Sangam — Sandbar where the Karli river meets the Arabian Sea.
