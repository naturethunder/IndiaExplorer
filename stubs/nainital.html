Nainital

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Nainital, nestled in the Kumaon Himalayas of Uttarakhand at an elevation of 2,084m, is celebrated across India for its picturesque freshwater lakes and vibrant hill-town atmosphere. The town revolves around the emerald Naini Lake—widely dubbed the 'Mango Lake' due to its natural mango-shaped contour when viewed from upper viewpoints like Mango Lake View Point and Tiffin Top. Surrounded by seven verdant peaks and offering boating, heritage temples, and panoramic snow views of Nanda Devi, it is the crown jewel of Kumaon tourism.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Naini Lake (The Mango Lake) — The iconic emerald freshwater lake at the heart of Nainital. When observed from elevated vantage points like the famous Mango Lake View Point on Kilbury Road or Tiffin Top, the natural shoreline perfectly resembles a giant green mango—earning it its famous moniker 'Mango Lake'. Visitors enjoy colorful wooden rowboats, paddle boats, and heritage yachting amidst misty Kumaon hills.
  • Bhimtal Lake — The largest lake in the Kumaon region, featuring a picturesque natural island in its center with an aquarium and scenic walking promenades.
  • Naukuchiatal — The renowned "Lake of Nine Corners", the deepest lake in the region surrounded by verdant terraced hills, lotus ponds, and serene bird-watching trails.
  • Sattal (Seven Lakes) — A cluster of seven interconnected pristine freshwater lakes (Ram, Sita, Lakshman, and more) nestled deep in dense oak and pine Himalayan forests.
  • Khurpatal Lake — A secluded, emerald-green hidden lake perched at 1,635m, famous for crystal-clear reflection of surrounding cedar trees and peaceful angling.
  • Naina Devi Temple — Revered Shakti Peetha shrine situated directly on the northern bank of Naini Lake, where Goddess Sati’s eyes are believed to have fallen.
