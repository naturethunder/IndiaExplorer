Konni Forest Division

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Konni Forest Division is located in Kerala, India, and covers an area of about 331.66 square kilometres (128.05 sq mi). It is the first reserve forest in Kerala, which was declared on 9 October 1888 under the Travancore Forest Act of 1887. The division is part of the Western Ghats, which were designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1 July 2012. The forest area is a repository of many endemic species of the Western Ghats.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Muringamangalam Sreemahadevar Temple — The Muringamangalam Sreemahadevar Temple, located half a kilometre from Konni Junction, is more than 1000 years old. It used to belong to the Pandalam Royal Family.
  • Chengara — Chengara is a small village located in the Pathanamthitta district of Kerala, India. It is known for its natural environment, hills, and climate.
  • Konni, Kerala — Konni is a Grama Panchayat and Taluk headquarters in Pathanamthitta district of Kerala, India. Konni is known for its elephant cages, forests, and rubber plantations.
  • Vallicode-Kottayam — Vallicode-Kottayam is a village in Pathanamthitta district in the state of Kerala, India. Vallicode-Kottayam village is a part of Konni Taluk, Pathanamthitta District of Kerala State, India.
  • Bethel Marthoma Church, Chengara — Bethel Marthoma Church is a Mar Thoma Syrian Church located at Chengara, Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India. Bethel Marthoma Church belongs to the Ranni Nilackal Diocese of Mar Thoma Syrian Church.
  • Thannithode — Thannithode is a village in Pathanamthitta district in the state of Kerala, India. It is near Konni town and is mainly a plantation township.
