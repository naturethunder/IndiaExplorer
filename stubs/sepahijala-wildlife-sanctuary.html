Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Tripura | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary in the West Tripura district of the Indian state of Tripura.

  • Gakulnagar — Gakulnagar is a census town in West Tripura district in the state of Tripura, India.
