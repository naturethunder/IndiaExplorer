Most Holy Redeemer Church, Belthangady

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Most Holy Redeemer Church is a historic Roman Catholic Church situated in the locality of Belthangady, India. The current structure of the church was built in 2012 during the tenure of Fr. James Dsouza. The original structure was constructed near the site of the present-day church building.
