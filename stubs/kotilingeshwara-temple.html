Kotilingeshwara Temple

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kotilingeshwara Temple (Kannada: ಕೋಟಿಲಿಂಗೇಶ್ವರ) a Hindu temple in the village of Kammasandra in Kolar district, Karnataka, India. The presiding deity of the temple is Shiva. The temple has one of the largest Shivalingams in the world.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Guttahalli, Kolar Gold Fields — Guttahalli is a small village in the Kolar Gold Fields Taluk of Kolar district in Karnataka, India. It is situated about 4 kilometers from Kolar Gold Fields, forming the outskirts of the city.
  • Adampalli — Adampalli is a village in the southern state of Karnataka, India. It is located in the Bangarapet taluk of Kolar district in Karnataka.
  • Bethamangala — Bethamangala is a small town in the Kolar Gold Field taluk of Kolar district in the state of Karnataka, India. The town is centred around a large man-made lake created by damming the Palar river.
  • Kolar Gold Fields — Kolar Gold Fields (K.G.F.) is a mining region in K.G.F. taluk (township), Kolar district, Karnataka, India. It is headquartered in Robertsonpet, where employees of Bharat Gold Mines Limited (BGML) and BEML Limited (formerly Bharat Earth Movers Limited) and their families live.
  • Coromandel, KGF — Coromandel is a mining area in Kolar Gold Fields(KGF), Karnataka, India. Where major mining units are located.
  • King George Hall, Kolar Gold Fields — King George Hall is a colonial-era civic building located in Robertsonpet, part of the Kolar Gold Fields (KGF) township in Karnataka, India. The hall was constructed to commemorate the Delhi Durbar of 1911, which marked the formal coronation of George V as Emperor of India and the announcement of shifting the imperial capital from Calcutta to Delhi.
