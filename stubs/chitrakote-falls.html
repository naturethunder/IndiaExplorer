Chitrakote Falls

State: Chhattisgarh | Category: nature | Best Time: Jul – Feb

Chitrakote Falls is a magnificent 300-meter wide waterfall on the Indravati river in Bastar, Chhattisgarh. Known as the Niagara Falls of India, Chitrakote expands to over 300 meters during the monsoon season.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chitrakote Horseshoe Waterfall — 300-meter wide Niagara Falls of India plunging into Indravati river gorge.
  • Indravati River Boat Safari — Wooden boat rides taking travelers up close to the mist spray of the plunging waterfall.
  • Tirathgarh Waterfalls (Kanger Valley) — 300ft block-type cascading waterfall inside Kanger Valley National Park.
  • Kotumsar Cave & Blind Cavefish — Subterranean limestone cave home to rare blind cavefish species.
  • Bastar Palace & Danteshwari Temple — Historic palace of Kakatiya dynasty and revered tribal deity temple in Jagdalpur.
  • Tamda Ghumar Waterfalls — Picturesque monsoon waterfall falling into a deep forested canyon.
