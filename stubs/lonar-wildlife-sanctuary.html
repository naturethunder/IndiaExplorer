Lonar Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Maharashtra | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Lona Wildlife Sanctuary is situated around a lagoon called Lonar Lake, which was created by a meteorite impact about 50,000 years ago. The diameter of the Lonar lake is 1.83 km. This sanctuary located in Lonar taluka of Buldhana district of Maharashtra. The sanctuary is spread over an area of 365.16 Hectares .

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Lonar Lake — Lonar Lake, also known as Lonar crater, is a saline, soda lake, located near Lonar, 79 km from Buldhana city in Buldhana district, Maharashtra state, India. It has been designated as a National Geological Monument, and is the only crater lake of meteoritic origin in the country.
  • Lonar — Lonar is a town, just 79 km from Buldhana city and municipal council in Buldhana district of Vidarbha region of the Indian state of Maharashtra. The town is the headquarter of Lonar taluka and is located near Mehkar.
  • Daitya Sudan Temple — Daitya Sudan Temple is a temple near Lonar Lake in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
  • Deulgaon Kundpal — Deulgaon, commonly known as "Deulgaon Kundpal" and sometimes as"Deulgaon Kund", is a village located in Lonar taluka of Buldhana district, in state of Maharashtra.
