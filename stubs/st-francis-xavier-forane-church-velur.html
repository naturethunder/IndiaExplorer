St. Francis Xavier Forane Church, Velur

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Francis Xavier Forane Church is at the center of Velur, Kerala, India. It is a Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, and a protected monument in the Archdiocese of Thrissur with considerable antiquity and spiritual heritage. Historically this Forane church has been the mother church of many parishes.
