Bhagwan Birsa Biological Park

State: Jharkhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bhagwan Birsa Biological Park (also known as Ranchi Zoo) is located off Chakla village, Ormanjhi in Ranchi, Jharkhand, India. Ranchi Zoo was established in 1994 at the bank of Getalsud Dam and on the main Ranchi-Hazaribag highway. The park has two divisions the zoological section is dedicated in 83 hectare and botanical section is spread over 21 hectare.
