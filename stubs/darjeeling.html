Darjeeling

State: West Bengal | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – May, Sep – Nov

Darjeeling, at 2,042m in the Singalila Ridge, offers some of India's most dramatic Himalayan views including Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m). Its colonial-era architecture, tea estates, UNESCO-listed toy train and vibrant Tibetan culture make it utterly unique.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tiger Hill Sunrise — Best sunrise view of Mt. Kanchenjunga and Everest (on clear days). Wake up at 3:30 AM.
  • Happy Valley Tea Estate — Second oldest tea estate in Darjeeling. Guided tour through the plucking and processing.
  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway — UNESCO World Heritage toy train. The 2-hour loop ride is magical.
  • Batasia Loop — Famous spiral railway loop where the toy train spirals around a war memorial.
  • Ghoom Monastery — Highest station on the toy train route. Ancient Gelugpa monastery with a giant Maitreya.
  • Padmaja Naidu Zoo — Highest zoo in India at 2,134m. Red pandas, snow leopards and Siberian tigers.
