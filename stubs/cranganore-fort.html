Cranganore Fort

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Fortaleza da São Tomé, also known as Cranganore Fort or Kottapuram Fort, is situated in Kodungallur of Thrissur District in Kerala, India. It was of 18 feet in thickness and was made of laterite. The stone fort was built by the Portuguese in 1523 and was named after Thomas the Apostle. The fort was enlarged in 1565 and passed into the hands of the Dutch in 1663, who destroyed the fort.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kottapuram Fort Ramparts — 16th-century laterite coastal fort built by the Portuguese in 1503, strategically commanding the mouth of the Periyar river and ancient Muziris waterways.
  • Cheraman Juma Mosque — Historically venerated as the oldest mosque in India (built in 629 CE), designed with classical Kerala wooden gables and ancient bronze hanging lamps.
  • Kodungallur Bhagavathy Temple — Renowned 1,800-year-old temple complex dedicated to Goddess Bhadrakali, famed for sprawling granite-paved courtyards and ancient banyan groves.
  • Thiruvanchikulam Mahadeva Temple — Ancient Chera empire Shiva temple referenced in the Sangam epics, showcasing ornate wooden roof sculptures, mural paintings, and sacred temple tanks.
  • Keezhtali Mahadeva Temple — One of the largest surviving monolithic stone sanctums in Kerala, exhibiting colossal granite basements from the Second Chera Kingdom era.
  • Muziris Heritage Museum / Paliam Palace — Traditional two-story Dutch-Kerala residence of the hereditary prime ministers of Cochin (Paliath Achans), featuring wooden balconies and antique weapons.
