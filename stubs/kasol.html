Kasol

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: adventure | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Kasol, in Parvati Valley, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite adventure base. Visitors come for its parvati river, trekking, israeli cafes, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kheerganga Trek — Forest trek to a natural hot spring at 3,050m.
  • Tosh Village — Hippie hamlet at the valley’s end with snow-peak views.
  • Manikaran Sahib — Gurudwara and hot springs sacred to Sikhs and Hindus.
