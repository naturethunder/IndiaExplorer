Uparkot Fort

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Uparkot Fort is a heritage landmark located in Gujarat, India. Visitors come here for forts, architecture and history. Nearby places worth exploring include Navghan Kuvo, Jumma Masjid, Uparkot and Uparkot Caves. The best time to visit is Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Navghan Kuvo — Navghan Kuvo is a stepwell in Uparkot Fort, Junagadh, Gujarat, India.
  • Jumma Masjid, Uparkot — The Jumma Masjid or Jama Masjid is a former Friday mosque, now in partial ruins, in Uparkot Fort in Junagadh, in the state of Gujarat, India. The mosque was built in 15th century by converting a temple or a palace identified as Ranakdevi Mahal by local people and the Archaeological Survey of India.
  • Uparkot Caves — Uparkot caves, also Uperkot caves, are ancient man-made caverns. The caves are a part of the Junagadh Buddhist Cave Groups situated in the eastern part of Junagadh of the Indian state of Gujarat.
  • Adi Kadi Vav — Adi Kadi Vav or Adi Chadi Vav is a stepwell in Uparkot Fort, Junagadh, Gujarat, India. The date of its construction is uncertain.
  • Junagadh State — The State of Junagadh was a princely state in the Kathiawar Peninsula of western India, located in present-day Gujarat. Ruled by the Babi dynasty of Muslim Nawabs since 1654, it was one of the larger princely states in the region firstly within the Maratha Empire and later by British India.
  • Bava Pyara Caves — Bava Pyara caves (also known as Baba Pyara caves) are an example of ancient man-made caverns. The caves are a part of the Junagadh Buddhist Cave Groups situated in the eastern part of Junagadh of the Indian state of Gujarat.
