St Mary's Church, Vendore

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Mary's Church (Malayalam: സെന്റ് മേരീസ്‌ പള്ളി) is a Syro-Malabar Catholic church in Vendore, Thrissur district, Kerala, India. It is known for its patronage of lady Mother Mary and is part of the Archdiocese of Thrissur. Vendore Parish is also known by the name Vendore Amma (Malayalam: വെണ്ടോരമ്മ), comes under the jurisdiction of Pudukkad Forona, and is located between Amballur and Mannampetta 200 m from Vendore Junction.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Vendore — Vendore is situated in Amballur village in the Thrissur district of Kerala, India. Vendore is situated in between Amballur and Mannampetta.
  • Alagappa Nagar — Alagappa Nagar is situated at Amballur Village near Puthukkad town in Mukundapuram Taluk in Thrissur District in Kerala State. Alagappa Nagar is 12.7 km far from Thrissur city.
  • Nenmanikkara — Nenmanikkara is a census town in Thrissur district in the Indian state of Kerala. It lies nearer to NH544 near Amballur town.
  • Puthukkad — Puthukkad is a town on the banks of Kurumali River, also known as Mupliyam River, in Kerala. The town is 15 km (9.3 mi) to the south of Thrissur city, 16 km (9.9 mi) to the north of Chalakudy, and 1.2 km (0.75 mi) west of Cheruval.
  • Amballur, Thrissur — Amballur is a village near Pudukkad town in Thrissur district in Kerala. National Highway NH544 passes through this place.
  • Jerusalem Retreat Center — Jerusalem Retreat Center is a Catholic charismatic renewal centre in Thalore, Thrissur city. The centre is managed by the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate, Thrissur.
