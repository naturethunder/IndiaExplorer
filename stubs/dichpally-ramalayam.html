Dichpally Ramalayam

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Dichpally Ramalayam is a Lord Rama temple located in Dichpally Village of Nizamabad, Telangana built in 14th century by Kakatiya kings. As the temple has a lot of resemblance in its style and structure, it is also called the Indhoor Khajuraho or the Khajuraho of Nizamabad.
