Adhi Ratneswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Adhi Ratneswarar Temple is a major Hindu temple with Shiva as the presiding deity, located in the town of Tiruvadanai in the state of Tamil Nadu, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tiruvadanai — Tiruvadanai or Thiruvadanai is a town and a taluk headquarters of the Ramanathapuram district, in Tamil Nadu, India. The town is known for the Adhi Ratneswarar Temple and is mentioned in the Thevaram hymns.
  • Anjukottai — Anjukottai is a village in northern Ramnad district, Tamil Nadu, India, 4 kilometres (2.5 mi) from Tiruvadanai; and 16 kilometres (9.9 mi) from Thondi. It includes seven small villages: Vaniyenthal, Melavayal, Pottakottai, Manathadal, Karaiyakottai, Chinna Anjukottai and Periya Anjukottai.
  • Karkathakudi — Karkathakudi is a small town and panchayat located in the Ramanathapuram district of Tamil Nadu state, India. The villages under the panchayat of Karkathakudi are Siruvandal, Peruvandal, Melapanaiyur, Keelapanaiyur, Gudalur, and Karungudi.
  • Thiruvetriyur (Ramanathapuram) — Thiruvetriyur is a Village in Tiruvadanai taluk, Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu in southern India. The place is famous for ancient Bagampriyal Kovil.
  • Thondi — Thondi (தொண்டி) is a town located in the Ramanathapuram district of the southern Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is about 25 km from Tiruvadanai.
