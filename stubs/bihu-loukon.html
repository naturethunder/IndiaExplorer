Bihu Loukon

State: Manipur | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bihu Loukon is a heritage landmark located in Manipur, India. Visitors come here for historic architecture, cultural heritage and scenic views. Nearby places worth exploring include Phayeng, Kangchup and Patsoi. The best time to visit is Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Phayeng — Phayeng or Chakpa Phayeng is a large village located in the Lamshang division of Imphal West district, Manipur, India. It is located near the foothill of the Kangchup hill ranges.
  • Kangchup — Kangchup is a village in the Lamshang subdivision of Imphal West district, in the Indian state of Manipur. The village and nearby hamlets were impacted by the Meitei–Kuki ethnic violence that started in Manipur in May 2023.
  • Patsoi — Patsoi is a tehsil (sub-district) in the Imphal West District of Manipur. The census code of Patsoi block is 01880.
  • Loitang Khunou — Loitang Khunou is a village in the Lamshang subdivision of Imphal West district of Manipur, India. It is governed by the Phumlou gram panchayat.
  • Lamshang — Lamsang (also spelt as Lamshang) is a town and a Municipal Council in Imphal West district in the Indian state of Manipur.
  • Manipur Zoological Garden — Manipur Zoological Garden (Meitei: Manipur Sha-Uchek Thampham) is a zoo in Iroisemba, Manipur. It is the second habitat of Sangai (Cervus eldi eldi), the world's only dancing deer species, after the Keibul Lamjao National Park, the world's only floating national park.
