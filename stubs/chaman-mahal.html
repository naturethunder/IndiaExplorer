Chaman Mahal

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Chaman Mahal is a palace in the Bhopal district of Madhya Pradesh, India. It was built by Afghan Commander Dost Mohammad Khan, who was the ruler of Islamnagar in 1715. It is located 11 km away from Bhopal, capital of Madhya Pradesh. 'Chaman' means garden and hence the palace is also known as Garden palace, having an attractive garden in the middle of palace with multiple fountains.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Rani Mahal, Islamnagar — Situated directly adjacent to Chaman Mahal, Rani Mahal was the queen's royal residence built by Dost Mohammad Khan in 1720. It features beautiful colonnaded stone verandas, inner courtyards, and deep arches blending Malwa and Mughal architectural styles.
  • Islamnagar Fort Ramparts — The fortified bastions and monumental gateways of Islamnagar (formerly Jagdishpur), fortified in 1715 as the first capital of Bhopal's founding dynasty. Offers elevated views across the surrounding countryside and water reservoirs.
  • Kanchbavli Stepped Baoli — An ancient stone stepwell (baoli) that provided fresh water to the royal palaces and fountain network of Islamnagar. Features stone staircases and subterranean arched landings showcasing historic water engineering.
  • Bhopal Upper Lake (Bhojtal) — One of Asia's largest artificial lakes, created by Raja Bhoj in the 11th century. Offers serene sunset boat rides, water promenade walks, and birdwatching along the lush wetlands.
