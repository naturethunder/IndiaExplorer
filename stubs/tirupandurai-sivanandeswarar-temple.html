Tirupandurai Sivanandeswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sivanandeswarar Temple (திருப்பந்துறை சிவானந்தேசுவரர் கோயில் ])is a Hindu temple located at Tirupandurai in Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu, India. The presiding deity is Shiva. He is called as Sivanandeswarar. His consort is known as Mangalambikai.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Natchiarkoil — Natchiarkoil is a neighbourhood in Kumbakonam of Thanjavur district of Tamil Nadu state in the peninsular India. Nachiarkoil lamps, which are made of brass, are produced locally.
  • Thirunarayur Nambi Temple — Nachiyar Kovil or Thirunarayur Nambi Temple is a Sri Vaishnava Hindu temple in Thirunarayur, a village in the outskirts of Kumbakonam in the southern Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is dedicated to Niladevi, an aspect of the goddess Lakshmi, and her consort Vishnu.
  • Thukkachi Abatsahayesvar Temple — Thukkachi Abatsahayesvar temple (also known as Sri Abathsahayeswarar Sowndaranayaki Ambal temple) is a Shiva temple in Thukkachi village near Kumbakonam in Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Mathur, Kumbakonam — Mathur is a village in the Kumbakonam taluk, Thiruvidaimarudur block of Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Enanallur — Enanallur is a village in Kumbakonam taluk, Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu.
  • Andarkoil Swarnapureewarar Temple — Andarkoil Swarnapureewarar Temple (ஆண்டார்கோயில் சொர்ணபுரீசுவரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located in the village of Andankoil in Valangaiman taluk of Tiruvarur district in Tamil Nadu, India.
