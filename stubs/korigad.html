Korigad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Korigad (also called Koraigad, Koarigad or Kumwarigad) is a hill fort located about 18 km (11 mi) south of Lonavala in Pune district, Maharashtra, India. Its date of construction is not known but likely predates 1500. It is about 923 m above sea level. The planned township of Aamby Valley is built over the fort's southern and eastern foothills.
