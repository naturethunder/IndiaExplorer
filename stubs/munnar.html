Munnar

State: Kerala | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Munnar sits at 1,600m in the Western Ghats and is covered almost entirely by tea estates. The rolling green hills, misty mornings, cool temperatures and the rare Neelakurinji bloom (every 12 years) make it Kerala's most spectacular hill destination.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Eravikulam National Park — Home of the Nilgiri Tahr. Anamudi peak (2,695m) — South India's highest.
  • Tea Museum (KDHP) — Learn the history of Munnar's tea estates and see vintage machinery.
  • Mattupetty Dam — Scenic dam surrounded by tea estates. Boating available (₹150).
  • Top Station — Highest point accessible by road in Munnar at 1,868m. Stunning views into Tamil Nadu.
  • Pothamedu Viewpoint — Panoramic views of tea, coffee and cardamom plantations. Best at sunrise.
  • Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary — Dry deciduous forest hosting grizzled giant squirrel (endemic), elephants and leopards.
