Sri Talpagiri Ranganadha Swamy Temple, Nellore

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Sri Ranganthaswami Temple in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, India is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Ranganatha a resting form of Lord Vishnu. This temple, also called Talpagiri Ranganathaswami temple or Ranganayakulu is one of the oldest temples in Nellore. It is located on the banks of the Penna River and is believed to have been constructed in the 12th century. Just before the main entrance of the temple is a huge tower, called Gaaligopuram, which literally means "wind tower".

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Udayagiri Fort — Udayagiri Fort is a legendary 14th-century hill fortress rising to 3,079 feet in Nellore district. Built by the Gajapatis and expanded by Vijayanagara emperor Sri Krishnadevaraya, it boasts monumental stone ramparts and historic ruins.
  • Penna River Barrage (Nellore) — The Penna River Barrage in Nellore is an iconic scenic spot where the broad Penna river flows, offering panoramic evening sunset walks, river breeze, and expansive water views.
  • Mypadu Beach — Mypadu Beach is a pristine Bay of Bengal shoreline destination near Nellore, renowned for its golden sandy expanse, gentle coastal surf, and tranquil casuarina tree groves.
  • Pulicat Lake Bird Sanctuary — Pulicat Lake is the second-largest brackish water lagoon in India, spanning the southern tip of Nellore district. A world-famous winter haven for thousands of greater flamingos, pelicans, and migratory waterfowl.
