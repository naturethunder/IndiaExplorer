Little Flower Forane Church, Nilambur

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Little Flower Forane Church, Nilambur is a Syro-Malabar church situated at Nilambur in Malappuram district.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nilambur — Nilambur (Malayalam: [n̪ilɐmbuːr]) is a major town, a municipality and a Taluk in the Malappuram district of the Indian state of Kerala. It is located close to the Nilgiris range of the Western Ghats on the banks of the Chaliyar River.
  • Nilambur–Shoranur line — The Nilambur–Shoranur line is a 66 kilometres (41 mi)-long single-line, electrified broad-gauge (1676 mm) railway spur branch line of the Indian Railways connecting Nilambur Road railway station in Malappuram district with Shoranur Junction in Palakkad district in the state of Kerala. It is a branch line administered by the Palakkad railway division of the Southern Railway zone and one of the shortest broad-gauge railway lines in India.
  • Teak Museum — Teak Museum is located 6 km from Nilambur, a town in the Malappuram district of Kerala, South India. Teak occurs naturally in India with the main teak forests found in Kerala.
  • Eranhimangad — Eranhimangad is a village near Chaliyar Grama Panchayath, in Nilambur municipality, Kerala state, India.
  • Karimpuzha (Malappuram) — Karimpuzha is the largest tributary of Chaliyar river, Kerala, India. It is very near to Nilambur.
  • Akampadam — Akampadam is a town in Malappuram district in the state of Kerala, India. A parish in the town is Saint Mary Orthodox Church, Akampadam under the Diocese of Malabar of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church.
