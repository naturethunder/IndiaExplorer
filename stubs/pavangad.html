Pavangad

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Pavangad is a prominent northern gateway located in the historic Kozhikode (Calicut) district along the Malabar Coast of Kerala. Positioned along the tranquil Arabian Sea coastline, it serves as an idyllic base for exploring the legendary beaches of Kozhikode and Kappad, where historic voyages once docked. Surrounded by coconut groves, mangrove estuaries, and centuries-old cultural monuments including the Tali Shiva Temple and Beypore port, Pavangad embodies the timeless coastal serenity and culinary legacy of northern Kerala.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kozhikode Beach & Sea Pier — Historic beach along the Malabar Coast famous for its 150-year-old crumbling iron sea pier, lighthouse, and mesmerizing Arabian Sea sunsets.
  • Kappad Beach — Historic blue-flag certified beach where Portuguese explorer Vasco da Gama first landed on Indian soil in May 1498, flanked by scenic rocky headlands.
  • Beypore Port & Uru Shipyard — Ancient maritime shipbuilding hub famed for crafting giant handcrafted wooden dhow vessels (Uru) for Arab merchants for over 1,500 years.
  • Sarovaram Bio Park — Eco-friendly urban sanctuary preserving rare Malabar mangrove habitats, featuring winding wooden canal boardwalks, butterfly gardens, and birdwatching decks.
  • Mananchira Square — Grand historic heritage park surrounding a massive 15th-century clearwater tank built by Zamorin King Mana Vikrama, lined with traditional Kerala architecture.
  • Thali Shiva Temple — 14th-century temple complex constructed by the Zamorins, celebrated for traditional multi-tiered wooden eaves, copper roofs, and intricate stone carvings.
