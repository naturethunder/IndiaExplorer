Shri Devi Talab Mandir

State: Punjab | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Devi Talab Mandir is a Hindu temple, located in Jalandhar, Punjab, India. The temple is devoted to Goddess Durga and is visited by thousands of pilgrims every year. It is regarded as one of the 51 Shakta pithas in India. A model of the Amarnath Temple, dedicated to God Shiva, is located within the temple complex.
