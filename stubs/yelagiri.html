Yelagiri

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – Jun

Yelagiri, in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its quiet hills, paragliding, trekking, with the best months being Oct – Jun.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Swamimalai Hills Trek — Trek to Yelagiri’s highest peak through shola forest.
  • Punganoor Lake — Man-made boating lake with a musical fountain.
  • Jalagamparai Waterfalls — Seasonal falls on the Attaru river below the hills.
