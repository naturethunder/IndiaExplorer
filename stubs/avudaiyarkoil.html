Avudaiyarkoil

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Avudaiyarkoil is one of the blocks and Taluk in the Pudukkotai district of Tamil Nadu, India. The village has a temple named Tirupperunturai. It is about 14 kilometres (9 mi) South East of the Major town Aranthangi along State Highway 26 (SH 26), on the South bank of the Vellar River.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tirupperunturai — Tirupperunthurai (also called the Athmanathaswamy temple) is located in Avudaiyarkoil village, near Aranthangi in the Pudukkottai district of Tamil Nadu. It was built in 10 century to honor Lord Shiva.
  • Avadaiyarkoil taluk — Avadaiyarkoil taluk is a taluk of Pudukkottai district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. The headquarters of the taluk is the town of Avadaiyarkoil.
  • Irrumbaanadu — Irrumbaanadu is a village in the Arimalamrevenue block of Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Adikesava Perumal Temple, Paramandur — Adikesava Perumal Temple is a Hindu temple located at Paramandur in Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Okkur, Pudukkottai — Okkur is a village in the Avadaiyarkoil revenue block of Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Madagam — Madagam is a village in the Arimalam revenue block of Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
