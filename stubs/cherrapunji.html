Cherrapunji

State: Meghalaya | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Sep – May

Cherrapunji, in Khasi Hills, Meghalaya, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its living root bridges, waterfalls, caves, with the best months being Sep – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nohkalikai Falls — India’s tallest plunge waterfall at 340m.
  • Double Decker Living Root Bridge — Hand-grown root bridge reached by a 3,500-step trek.
  • Mawsmai Cave — Easy-to-walk limestone cave with lit formations.
