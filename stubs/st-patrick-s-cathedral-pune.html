St. Patrick's Cathedral, Pune

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. Patrick's Cathedral is a Roman Catholic religious building located adjacent to the 'Empress Garden' in Pune, India). Built in neo-gothic style in the middle of the 19th century, it was adopted as 'cathedral' church when Pune (then Poona) was made diocese (1886), of which it is still the main church. It also has the highest number of parishioners of the Pune diocese.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Roman Catholic Diocese of Poona — The Roman Catholic Diocese of Poona is a diocese located in the city of Pune in the ecclesiastical province of Archdiocese of Mumbai in India. The Poona Diocese consists of the civil districts of Pune, Satara, Solapur, Sangli & Kolhapur city.
  • Fatimanagar — Fatima Nagar is a neighbourhood in Pune. The neighbourhood derives its name from the "Congregation of Sisters of Our Lady of Fatima" located here.
  • Pune Race Course — Pune Race Course is a racecourse located in Pune Cantonment, western India. It is located 5–6 km from downtown Pune and 12–13 km from Pune airport.
  • St. Mary's Church, Pune — St. Mary's Church is Protestant church located in the Pune Cantonment, Maharashtra state, India.
  • National War Memorial Southern Command — The National War Memorial Southern Command is a war memorial in the city of Pune, India, dedicated to post-Independence war martyrs. This is the only war memorial in South Asia which has been erected by citizens' contributions.
  • Pune Club Ground — The Pune Club Ground or Poona Club Ground is a cricket ground in Pune, Maharashtra. It has been used for first-class cricket since 1935, most recently for a Ranji Trophy match between Maharashtra and Railways in 2001.
