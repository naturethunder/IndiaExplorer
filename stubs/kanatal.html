Kanatal

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kanatal is a small village nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas at an altitude of 2,600 metres. Famous for its dense deodar and pine forests, panoramic Himalayan views, and the stunning Tehri reservoir, it offers a perfect escape from city life. The village is known for adventure camping, forest treks, and a serene atmosphere that's hard to find even in more popular hill stations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Surkanda Devi Temple — 1.5km trek from Kaddukhal. Spectacular 360° Himalayan panorama at 3,030m.
  • Tehri Lake — Asia's largest man-made reservoir. Boating, jet ski, kayaking and paragliding.
  • Kodia Jungle — Dense oak and rhododendron forest. Popular sunrise walk with bird watching.
  • Dhanaulti — Snow views in winter, cedar forest eco-park. Perfect day trip from Kanatal.
  • Chamba Village — Nearest market town on NH94. Good local food, supplies and transport hub.
  • New Tehri Town — Modern township above Tehri Dam. Worth a visit for the dam and reservoir views.
