Thrikkariyoor Mahadeva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Thrikkariyoor Mahadeva Temple is an ancient, revered Hindu sanctuary located in Thrikkariyoor near Kothamangalam in Ernakulam district, Kerala. Believed to have been the original capital (Karur) of the ancient Chera dynasty and one of the 108 venerated Shiva Temples consecrated by Lord Parashurama, the temple boasts classic Kerala Dravidian architecture with a circular stone sanctum (Vatta Sreekovil), copper-plated conical roof, ornate Namaskara Mandapam, and a sacred temple tank.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Thrikkariyoor Heritage Village — Thrikkariyoor is an ancient rural settlement associated with early Chera history. The village features traditional Kerala homesteads (tharavads), lush spice plantations, and quiet countryside roads.
  • Bhoothathankettu Dam & Nature Reserve — Bhoothathankettu is a scenic dam and natural reservoir enveloped by dense tropical forests on the Periyar River. According to legend, demons attempted to dam the river here using mammoth boulders.
  • Thattekad Bird Sanctuary (Salim Ali Sanctuary) — Described by legendary ornithologist Dr. Salim Ali as the 'richest bird habitat in peninsular India', Thattekad lies along the Periyar River and shelters over 300 rare and endemic bird species in pristine lowland rainforests.
  • Kothamangalam Town & Gateway to High Ranges — Known as the 'Gateway of the High Ranges', Kothamangalam is a lively taluk town situated in the foothills of the Western Ghats, famous for its historic trade routes, academic institutions, and spices.
  • Marthoma Cheriyapally, Kothamangalam — Founded in 1455 AD, Marthoma Cheriyapally is one of the most famous Jacobite Syrian Christian pilgrimage centers in India, holding the sacred tomb of Saint Baselios Yeldo.
  • Ayyappanmudi Hill Viewpoint — A monolithic rock outcrop rising high above Kothamangalam, Ayyappanmudi features an ancient hilltop temple and sweeping 360-degree sunset panoramas across the Western Ghats and coconut plains.
