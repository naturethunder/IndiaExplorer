Adat Shiva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Adat Shiva Temple is located at Adat village in Thrissur district. The temple has two main deities, Sri Parameswara and Maha Vishnu. Both deities having separate temple complexes. Lord Sri Parameswara facing east and Maha Vishnu is facing west.
