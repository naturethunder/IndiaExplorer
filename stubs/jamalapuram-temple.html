Jamalapuram Temple

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sri Venkateshwara Swamy Temple is located in Jamalapuram, near to Errupalem in Khammam district of Telangana, India. The presiding deity in this temple is Lord Balaji and is said to be a swayambhu Lord, who self-manifested in this place. The legend has it that in ancient days Jabali Maharshi worshipped and did penance here at a place called Suchi Gutta and was said to have been blessed by Lord Venkateshwara. The temple here seems to have been in existence since considerable antiquity.
