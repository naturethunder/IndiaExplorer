Saptashrungi

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Saptashrungi or Saptashringi (Marathi: सप्तशृङ्गि, Saptaśr̥ṇgī) is a site of Hindu pilgrimage in the village of Vani, situated 60 kilometres (37 mi) from Nashik in Indian state of Maharashtra. According to Hindu traditions, the goddess Saptashrungi Nivasini dwells within the seven mountain peaks. (Sapta means seven and shrung means peaks.) It is located in Nanduri, Kalwan taluka, a small village near Nashik in India. The Marathas and some Hindu tribes have worshipped the goddess from a long time and some worship her as their kuldaivat.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Satmala Range — Satmala (pronunciation:[sɐtmaːla]) is a mountain range which runs across Nashik District, Maharashtra. They are an integral part of the Sahyadris range within Nashik.
  • Nanduri Dam — Nanduri Dam, is an earthfill and gravity dam on Tapti river near Ama Local Nallaher, Nashik district in the state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Javlya fort — Javlya Fort (Marathi: जवळ्या किल्ला, Hindi: जवळ्या किला, (transliteration: Marathi-"Javḷyā Killā and Hindi: Javḷyā Kilā or Qilā) is located 43 km from Nashik, Nashik district, of Maharashtra. Javlya fort and Ravlya fort are twin forts located on a single hill plateau, Ravlya on the west and Javlya on the east side of the hill plateau.
  • Ahivant Fort — Ahivant Fort is a fort located 55km from Nashik, in Nashik district, of Maharashtra. This fort is an important fort in Nashik district.
  • Dhunda fort — Dhunda Fort (Marathi: धुंदा किल्ला, Hindi: धुंदया किला, transliteration: Dhundya Qilа̄)is located 106 km from Nashik, Nashik district, of Maharashtra.The Dhundya fort is located on a single hillock west of the road from Malegaon to Satana.
  • Ravlya Fort — Ravalya Fort (Marathi: रवळ्या किल्ला, Hindi: रवल्या किला, transliteration: Ravalya Qilа̄)is located 43 km from Nashik, Nashik district, of Maharashtra. The Ravlya fort and Javlya fort are twin forts located on a single hill plateau.
