Kanthkot Fort

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kanthkot Fort is a heritage landmark located in Gujarat, India. Visitors come here for forts, architecture and history. Nearby places worth exploring include Wagad Formation, Katrol Formation and Patcham Formation. The best time to visit is Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Wagad Formation — The Wagad Formation is a Mesozoic Late Jurassic geologic formation in India. Fossilised sauropod and ornithopod tracks have been reported from the formation.
  • Katrol Formation — The Katrol Formation is a Mesozoic geologic formation in India. Fossil Dinosaur tracks have been reported from the formation.
  • Patcham Formation — The Patcham Formation or Pachchham Formation is a Bathonian geologic formation of Patcham Island, Kutch district, Gujarat, India. Dinosaur remains are among the fossils that have been recovered from the formation, although none have yet been referred to a specific genus.
