Mawlynnong

State: Meghalaya | Category: nature | Best Time: Oct – May

Mawlynnong is a picturesque village in Meghalaya famous for its eco-friendly practices and spotlessly clean streets. Referred to as God's Own Garden, Mawlynnong is maintained by local Khasi villagers who collect garbage in bamboo dustbins. Nearby Nohwet features an ancient single-decker living root bridge woven across a pristine river stream.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nohwet Living Root Bridge — 180-year-old bio-engineered double strand living ficus root bridge spanning Thyllong river.
  • Sky View Bamboo Observation Tower — 85ft high hand-crafted bamboo tower offering panoramic views of Bangladesh plains.
  • Mawlynnong Sacred Grove & Flower Trails — Spotless village cobbled paths lined with pitcher plants, orchids, and bamboo dustbins.
  • Balancing Rock of Mawlynnong — Massive natural boulder balancing precariously on a tiny rock base without falling.
  • Dawki Umngot River Boating — World famous crystal clear glass river where boats appear floating in mid-air.
  • Riwai Living Root Bridge — Easily accessible thick living root bridge surrounded by rainforest stream.
