Akhandalamani Temple

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Akhandalamani Temple (pronounced [akʰɔɳɖɔlɔmɔɳi]) at Aradi village, Odisha, India, is dedicated to Lord Shiva (Baba Akhandalamani). Aradi is about 37 kilometres (23 mi) away from Bhadrak.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Laddu Baba Temple — Laddu Baba Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva worshipped as Laddukeshwar but affectionately called Laddu Baba in the town of Sharanakula in Nayagarh district of Odisha, India. The temple dating back to medieval times shows a fusion of Kalinga and Dravidian temple architecture.
  • Sarankul — Sarankul is a small town under the district Nayagarh of Odisha.
