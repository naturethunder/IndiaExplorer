Bellary Fort

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Bellary Fort ("Bellary Kote") was built on top of a hill called the "Ballari Gudda" or the Fort Hill. It is situated in the historic city of Bellary, in the Bellary district, in Karnataka state, India. It was built in two parts, namely the Upper Fort and the Lower Fort. The Upper Fort was built by Hanumappa Nayaka, a feudatory of the Vijayanagara Empire.
