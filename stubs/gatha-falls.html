Gatha Falls

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Gatha Falls (or Gatha Seha) is a waterfall in Panna district in the Indian state of Madhya Pradesh. It is the 36th highest waterfall in India.

  • Pandav Falls — The Pandav Falls is a waterfall in the Panna district in the Indian state of Madhya Pradesh. About 30 metres (98 ft) high, it is located on a tributary of the Ken River, as it plunges over the falls to join it.
