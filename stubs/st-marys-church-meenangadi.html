St. Marys Church Meenangadi

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

E. A. E St. Mary's Soonoro Syriac Orthodox Church, Meenangadi (Malayalam: സെന്‍റ് മേരീസ് 'സൂനോറോ' തീര്‍ത്ഥാടന കേന്ദ്രം മീനങ്ങാടി), is a Marian Pilgrim center of the Syriac Orthodox Church located at Meenangadi in Kerala, India. Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Meenangadi — Meenangadi is an old town situated on the highway NH 766 between Kalpetta and Sulthan Bathery in Wayanad District, in Kerala, India. This place is one among the Provinces where the existence of Dolmens provide historical evidence of earlier civilisation.
  • Krishnagiri Village — Krishnagiri is a village in the Wayanad District of Kerala, India. It is in a rural area of Wayanad and one of the 15 villages in Sultan Bathery Tehsil.
  • Cochin Refineries Limited Ground — Cochin Refineries Limited Ground or CRL Ground is a multi purpose stadium in Ambalamugal, Kochi, Kerala. The ground is mainly used for organizing matches of football, cricket and other sports.
  • Krishnagiri Stadium — Krishnagiri Stadium is a cricket stadium in Wayanad, Kerala. The stadium is located in Krishnagiri Village in the Indian state of Kerala.
  • Karapuzha Dam — Karapuzha Dam located in the Wayanad district of Kerala, Karapuzha Dam is located in the greenish and natural regions of Wayanad, Kerala on the Karapuzha River, a tributary of the Kabini River. Construction on the dam began in 1977 and it was completed in 2004.
  • Phantom Rock — Phantom Rock is a tourist destination in Ambalavayal in the district of Wayanad in Kerala. It is a natural metamorphic rock formation resembling a human skull, and thus is known as Phantom Rock.
