Odogaon Raghunath Temple

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Odagaon Raghunath Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Rama in the town of Odagaon, Nayagarh district of Odisha in India. The temple dates back from the Middle Ages and is an important pilgrimage centre in Odisha. It is built in a typical Kalinga style of architecture and is topped by 3 golden kalashas. An important festival occurs here on Rama Navami.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Odagaon — Odagaon is a town and Notified area council in the Nayagarh district of the Indian state of Odisha. It is the location of the Lord Raghunath Temple.
