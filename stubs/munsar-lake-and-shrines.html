Munsar lake and shrines

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Munsar Lake (મુનસર તળાવ) is water body constructed by Minaldevi, mother of Jayasimha Siddharaja, of Chaulukya dynasty. It was named as Mansavor but due to Indiscretion it's widely known as Munsar. This lake is situated at Viramgam, near Ahmedabad.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Viramgam — Viramgam is a town and former princely state in the Indian state of Gujarat. The town is located in the Ahmedabad district, which contains the city of Ahmedabad, the state's largest city.
  • Bhojva — Bhojva is a village in Viramgam taluka, Ahmedabad district, Gujarat, India.
