Rishikesh

State: Uttarakhand | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Sep – Jun

Rishikesh sits at the foothills of the Himalayas where the Ganga emerges from the mountains. Known as the Yoga Capital of the World and gateway to the Char Dham pilgrimage, it balances spiritual depth with adrenaline-packed adventure sports.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat — Most mesmerising evening ritual on the Ganga banks. Arrive 30 min early.
  • White Water Rafting — Grade 1–4 rapids on the Ganga. Shivpuri to Rishikesh most popular stretch.
  • Ram Jhula & Laxman Jhula — Iconic suspension bridges over the Ganga lined with cafes and temples.
  • Neer Garh Waterfall — Pretty waterfall reachable via a jungle trek. Swimming pool at base. Very refreshing.
  • Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia) — Where the Beatles stayed in 1968. Atmospheric ruins with graffiti art in the forest.
  • Bungee Jumping at Mohan Chatti — India's highest fixed platform bungee at 83m over the Ganga. Also flying fox and swing.
