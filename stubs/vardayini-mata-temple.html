Vardayini Mata Temple

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Vardayini Mata Temple (Gujarati: વરદાયિની માતા મંદિર; Hindi: वरदायिनी माता मंदिर) is situated at Rupal, Gandhinagar and is dedicated to Goddess Vardayini Mata.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Rupal, Gandhinagar — Rupal is a village in Gandhinagar district of Gujarat state, India. It is located 13 km towards north from District headquarters Gandhinagar.
  • Thol Lake — Thol Lake is an artificial lake near Thol village in Kadi in Mehsana District in the Indian state of Gujarat. A freshwater lake surrounded by marshes, it was built as an irrigation tank in 1912.
  • Paliad — Paliad, also spelled Paliyad, is a village located in the district of Gandhinagar, Gujarat, western India.
  • Mahatma Mandir — Mahatma Mandir is a convention and exhibition centre located at sector 13 C in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India. It is inspired by the life and philosophy of Mahatma Gandhi.
