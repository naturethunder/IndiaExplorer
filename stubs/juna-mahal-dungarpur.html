Juna Mahal, Dungarpur

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Juna Mahal, also known as the Old Palace or Garh Palace, is a palace complex situated in the city of Dungarpur in the Indian state of Rajasthan. It is situated at the foothills of the Dhanmata Hill. The property is still owned by the former royal family of Dungarpur.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Adinath Temple, Dungarpur — Adinath Bhagwan Temple is a Jain Temple in Manek Chowk, Dungarpur, Rajasthan, India. It was built by Seth Sanvaldas Davda in 1469 CE (1526 VS).
  • Dungarpur state — Dungarpur State was a kingdom and later princely state during the British Raj. Its capital was the city of Dungarpur in the southernmost area of present-day Rajasthan State in India.
  • Dungarpur Mews — The Dungarpur Mews is an automobile museum in Dungarpur, Rajasthan, India.
  • Gaib Sagar Lake — Gaib Sagar Lake, also known as Gap Sagar Lake, is an artificial lake in present-day Dungarpur, Rajasthan, India. It was built by Maharawal Gopinath (Gaipa Rawal) of Dungarpur State in 1428.
  • Udai Bilas Palace — Udai Bilas Palace in Dungarpur, Rajasthan is the principal residence of the former royal family of Dungarpur and operates as a heritage hotel. A part of the palace is a museum.
  • Dungarpur, Rajasthan — Dungarpur is a city and a Municipality in the southernmost district in Rajasthan, India. Dungarpur is the headquarter of Dungarpur District.
