Umiya Temple, Unjha

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Umiya Mata Temple is a Hindu temple of the goddess Umiya, the clan-deity or kuldevi of the Kadava Patidars. It is located in the center of Unjha, Mehsana district, Gujarat, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Unjha — Unjha is a town and a municipality in the Mehsana district of the Indian state of Gujarat. Unjha is 26 km north of Mehsana and 102 km north of Ahmedabad.
  • Maktupur — Maktupur is a small village in North Gujarat, West India. It is in Mehsana district.
  • Aithor — Aithor is a village in Unjha Taluka of Mehsana district, Gujarat, India.
  • Bhankhar — Bhankhar is a village in Unjha Taluka of Mahesana district in Gujarat, India.
  • Unava — Unava is a village located near Unjha, in Mehsana district of Gujarat, India. It is on the SH41 (Mehsana-Palanpur).
  • Tundav — Tundav is a village in the Mehsana district in the north of Gujarat, India. As of 2011, it had a population of 4,280.
