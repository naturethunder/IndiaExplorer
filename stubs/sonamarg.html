Sonamarg

State: Jammu & Kashmir | Category: hill_station | Best Time: May – Oct

Sonamarg, in Ganderbal, Jammu & Kashmir, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its meadow of gold, thajiwas glacier, sindh river, with the best months being May – Oct.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Thajiwas Glacier — Pony ride to a glacier with year-round snow.
  • Zoji La Pass — Dramatic high pass gateway to Ladakh.
  • Sindh River Banks — Trout-rich river meadows ideal for picnics.
