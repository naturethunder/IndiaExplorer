Chennai Om Sri Skandhashramam

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Chennai Om Sri Skandashramam is a Hindu temple dedicated to the deity Murugan (Swaminatha Swami) in Chennai, India. It is located at Selaiyur, Tambaram, a southern neighbourhood of Chennai.
