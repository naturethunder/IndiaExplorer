Arthat St. Mary's Cathedral

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Arthat St. Mary's Orthodox Cathedral (Arthat Valiyapally), also called Kunnamkulam-Chattukulangara Church, is an ancient Oriental Orthodox church located in Arthat village of Thalapilly taluk, one mile south of Kunnamkulam town, in Thrissur district, Kerala, India. It is one of the oldest Christian churches in the region. This church did not participate in the Synod of Diamper.
