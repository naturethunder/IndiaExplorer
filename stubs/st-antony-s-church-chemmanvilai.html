St. Antony's Church, Chemmanvilai

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Antony's Church, Chemmanvilai[1] is a Roman Catholic Church located in Kanyakumari district, Tamil Nadu, India. St. Antony's Church, located right in the middle of the Chemmanvilai village, is an important worship center that plays an important role in the lives of the local people.
