Kohima

State: Nagaland | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Oct – May

Kohima, in Naga Hills, Nagaland, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its hornbill festival, wwii history, tribes, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kohima War Cemetery — Moving WWII memorial to the Battle of Kohima.
  • Kisama Heritage Village — Host of the Hornbill Festival showcasing all Naga tribes.
  • Dzukou Valley — Valley of seasonal flowers and rolling green hills.
