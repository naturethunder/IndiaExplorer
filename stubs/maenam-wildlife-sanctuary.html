Maenam Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Sikkim | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Maenam Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife reserve located in Ravangla, near Namchi City in the Namchi district of the Indian state of Sikkim covering an area of around 35 square kilometres (14 sq mi). The literal meaning of maenam-la is "treasure-house of medicines", and the flora of the sanctuary is rich in a number of plants of medicinal value. Established in 1987, Maenam wildlife sanctuary lies 34 kilometres (21 mi) from Namchi City, the district headquarter, 65 kilometres (40 mi) southwest of the state capital Gangtok and is a popular tourist destination.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Buddha Park of Ravangla (Tathagata Tsal) — World-renowned spiritual sanctuary featuring an awe-inspiring 130-foot gilded statue of Shakyamuni Buddha set within landscaped gardens against the majestic backdrop of Mount Narsing.
  • Ralang Monastery (Palchen Choeling Institute) — One of the most sacred Kagyu Buddhist monasteries in Sikkim, renowned for magnificent Tibetan murals, golden stupas, and grand annual Mahakala mask dances.
  • Bhaleydhunga Cliff & Skywalk Trail — A thrilling high-altitude rocky promontory resembling a rooster’s head, offering jaw-dropping views of the Teesta River flowing 3,000 feet below and the proposed glass skywalk.
  • Temi Tea Garden (South Sikkim) — The sole commercial tea estate in Sikkim, cascading over 440 acres of rolling slopes producing world-class organic tea amidst cherry blossom trees and Kanchenjunga views.
  • Bon Monastery, Kewzing — One of only two monasteries in India belonging to the ancient pre-Buddhist Yungdrung Bon tradition, adorned with rare iconography and peaceful forest surroundings.
  • Rayong Sunrise Viewpoint — A panoramic viewpoint situated along the Ravangla-Damthang road offering unobstructed vistas of the first rays of dawn illuminating Mount Kanchenjunga and Chola range.
