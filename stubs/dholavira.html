Dholavira

State: Gujarat | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Mar

Dholavira on Khadir Bet island in Kutch is one of the most prominent archaeological sites of the Indus Valley Civilization. Dating back to 2600 BCE, Dholavira features world-first stone water reservoirs, a planned grid citadel, and the famous 'Road to Heaven' highway.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Dholavira Harappan Excavation Site — UNESCO World Heritage Indus Valley metropolis (2600 BCE) featuring stone citadel and ancient market streets.
  • Great Water Reservoirs of Dholavira — World's oldest sophisticated stone-cut rainwater harvesting reservoirs.
  • Road to Heaven (Khadir Bet Salt Highway) — Sensational 30km highway cutting straight through glowing white salt lake waters.
  • Wood Fossil Park Khadir Bet — Jurassic era 180-million-year-old petrified tree wood fossils site.
  • Kuntasi Harappan Port Ruins — Ancient Harappan jetty and copper workshop site near Gulf of Kutch.
  • Chhari Dhand Flamingo Bird Sanctuary — Seasonal wetland lake hosting over 50,000 migratory flamingos.
