Talley Valley Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Arunachal Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Situated at altitudes ranging from 1,200 to 3,000 meters above sea level, Talley Valley is a designated biodiversity hotspot near Ziro. The sanctuary protects a unique plateau ecosystem of Pleioblastus simonii bamboo, blue pine woodlands, and mossy cloud forests, serving as a vital sanctuary for clouded leopards, Himalayan black bears, and rare pheasants.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ziro — Ziro is a town in and the district headquarters of the Lower Subansiri district of the Indian state of Arunachal Pradesh. The part of the town which is the centre of economic activities and where the administrative offices are located is called Hapoli, or locally known by the Apatanis as "Hao-Polyang".
  • Hapoli — Hapoli is a locality in the town of Ziro, the headquarters of Lower Subansiri district, Arunachal Pradesh, India. The Apatanis are the natives of this place and also form the majority of the population.
  • Lempia — Lempia is a traditional Apatani heritage village on the outskirts of Ziro, featuring ancient bamboo and pine groves, indigenous sustainable irrigation channels, and traditional tribal homestays.
