Shekhawati

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shekhawati is a semi-arid region in Rajasthan famed as the world's largest open-air art gallery. Built by wealthy Marwari merchants during the 18th-19th centuries, thousands of grand havelis in Mandawa, Nawalgarh, and Fatehpur are decorated with intricate wall frescoes.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Castle Mandawa Fort & Armor Museum — 18th-century fort hotel featuring royal armor museum and painted archways.
  • Murmuria & Jhunjhunwala Havelis — Intricately painted merchant havelis featuring frescoes of steam locomotives and myths.
  • Nawalgarh Poddar Haveli Museum — Meticulously restored haveli containing over 750 detailed fresco paintings.
  • Fatehpur Le Prince Haveli — Restored French-Indian heritage haveli turned cultural art center.
  • Dundlod Fort & Marwari Horse Stables — Historic fort famed for Marwari horse breeding stables and library.
  • Churu Sethani Ka Johra Stepwell — Massive reservoir stepwell with carved sandstone chatris.
