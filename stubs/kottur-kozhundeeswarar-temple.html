Kottur Kozhundeeswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kottur Kozhundeeswarar Temple (கோட்டூர் கொழுந்தீஸ்வரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located at Kottur in Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, India. The temple is dedicated to Shiva, as the moolavar presiding deity, in his manifestation as Kozhundeeswarar. His consort, Parvati, is known as Thenambikai. The historical name of the place is Indirapuram.
