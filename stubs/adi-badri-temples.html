Adi Badri temples

State: Uttarakhand | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Adi Badri Temples is one of the Panch Badri — the five sacred Vishnu shrines of the Garhwal Himalayas in Uttarakhand. Situated at an altitude of 1,000 m (3,280 ft) near Karnaprayag in Chamoli district, Adi Badri is unique among the Badri shrines because it is not a single temple but a complex of fourteen ancient stone temples set on a stepped hillock. The main temple enshrines a 1-metre black basalt idol of Vishnu holding a conch shell (Shankha) and lotus (Padma), believed to be installed by Adi Shankaracharya in the 8th century CE. The surrounding thirteen smaller temples are dedicated to deities including Kubera, Garuda, Annapurna, Lakshmi, Triphala Narayan, and Shiva. Unlike the high-altitude Badrinath temple, Adi Badri remains accessible throughout the year, making it a favoured pilgrimage for year-round devotees. The temple complex exudes a tranquil mountain ambience overlooking forested slopes of the Pindar valley.
