Santoshgad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Santoshgad (also referred to as Tathavade, the name of the closest village) is a fort in Phaltan taluka of Satara district in Maharashtra, India. Santoshgad hill fort lies in the north-west corner of the Phaltan taluka, about 12 miles (19 km) south-west of Phaltan.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Diskal — Diskal is a village in the Satara district of the state of Maharashtra in India. It is situated on the Pusegaon-Phaltan road in Khatav taluka.
  • Upalve — Upalve village is under the Phaltan Taluka, and district is Satara. It is located to the south of the Phaltan and beside the Sitamai, from where the River Banganga started.
  • Lalgun — Lalgun is a village in Khatav taluka of Satara district in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is located on Pusegaon-Phaltan State Highway (SH-147) and comes under Koregaon Assembly constituency and Satara Lok Sabha constituency.
