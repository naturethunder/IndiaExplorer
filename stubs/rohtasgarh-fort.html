Rohtasgarh Fort

State: Bihar | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Rohtasgarh or Rohtas Fort is located in the Son River valley, in the small town of Rohtas in Bihar, India.

  • Banjari, Bihar — Banjari is a small town at the south-western tip of Bihar state, India. It is located on the Sone River, the largest of the Ganges' southern tributaries, in Rohtas district.
