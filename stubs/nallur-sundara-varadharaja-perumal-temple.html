Nallur Sundara Varadharaja Perumal Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Varadharaja Perumal Temple is a 500 year old vaishnavite temple at Nallur neighbourhood in Tiruvannamalai of Tamil Nadu state in India. The presiding deity in this temple is Sundara Varadharaja Perumal and goddess is Sundaravalli Thaayaar. Garudaallhvar is at the feet of the main deity in worshipping posture.
