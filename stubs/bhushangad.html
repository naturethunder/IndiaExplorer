Bhushangad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bhushangad is a hill fort in the Khatav taluk of the Satara district of Maharashtra, India. It is oval in shape, and rises 600 feet (180 m) above the surrounding plain. Its top is flat, and measures to be about 300 square yards (250 m2) in area. The origins of the fort are unclear.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mhasurne — Mhasurne is a village in Khatav taluk and Satara district, Maharashtra, India. It is located 64 km to the east of the district headquarters, Satara, 21 km from Khatav, 30 km from Karad, and 285 km from the state capital Mumbai.
  • Aundh State — Aundh State was a Maratha princely state during the British Raj, in the Deccan States Agency division of the Bombay Presidency. The Principality of Aundh covered an area of 1298 square kilometers with a population of 88,762 in 1941.
  • Aundh, Satara — Aundh is a town in India 26 mi. southeast of Satara.
