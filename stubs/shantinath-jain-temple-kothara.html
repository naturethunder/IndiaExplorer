Shantinath Jain temple, Kothara

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Shantinath Jain temple is located in Kothara village of Kutch district, Gujarat, India. The temple is dedicated to the 16th Tirthankara Shantinatha and is an important place of pilgrimage for the followers of Jainism.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kothara, Kutch — Kothara is a village and a Jain pilgrimage center located in Abdasa Taluka of Kutch district of Gujarat, India.
  • Bhanada — Bhanada is a panchayat village in Gujarat, India. Administratively it is under Abdasa Taluka, Kutch District, Gujarat.
  • Khirasara — Khirasara is an archaeological site belonging to the Indus Valley Civilization. This site is located in Nakhatrana Taluka of Kutch district in the western Indian state of Gujarat, on the bank of Khari river, 85 kilometres (55 mi) from Bhuj, the district headquarters.
  • Vinjhan — Vinzan or Vinjhan is a village in Abdasa Taluka in Kutch district of Gujarat, India.
