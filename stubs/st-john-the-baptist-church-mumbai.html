St. John the Baptist Church, Mumbai

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. John the Baptist Church is an abandoned and ruined church presently located within the SEEPZ Industrial Area, in Andheri, Mumbai, India. It was built by the Portuguese Jesuits in 1579 and opened to public worship on the feast of John the Baptist that year. It also had an attached graveyard.
