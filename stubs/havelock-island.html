Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

State: Andaman & Nicobar | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Apr

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep) is the premier destination in the Andaman Islands, celebrated for Radhanagar Beach, scuba diving spots, elephant beach sea-walking, and mangrove kayaking.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7) — Voted among Asia's best beaches by Time Magazine. Renowned for its powdery soft white silica sand, turquoise waves, and legendary sunsets framed by ancient rainforest mahua trees.
  • Elephant Beach (Snorkeling & Coral Reefs) — The premier snorkeling and water sports haven of Havelock. Vibrant shallow coral gardens teem with exotic marine life, sea turtles, and colorful parrotfish amidst crystal-clear turquoise waters.
  • Kalapathar Beach (Sunrise & Black Rocks) — A serene beach celebrated for its dramatic contrast of unique black volcanic limestone boulders against powdery golden sands and vibrant aquamarine lagoons. The prime sunrise spot on Swaraj Dweep.
