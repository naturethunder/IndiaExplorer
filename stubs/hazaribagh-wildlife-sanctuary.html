Hazaribagh Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Jharkhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Hazaribagh Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary in Jharkhand, India, about 55 miles (89 km) north of Ranchi. It was established in 1955. It is home to many wild animals including deer, monkey and birds.
