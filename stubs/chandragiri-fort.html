Chandragiri Fort

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Chandragiri Fort is a historical fort located near Tirupati in Andhra Pradesh, India. The fort, with its rectangular bastions and steep moat, was built around 1000AD during the reign of Immadi Narasimha Yadavaraya, who ruled from Narayanavanam.

  • Pullaiahgaripalli — Pullaiahgaripalli (P.G.Palli) is a neighbourhood village of Tirupati. It is a part of Tirupati urban agglomeration and located in Chandragiri mandal.
  • Naravaripalle — Naravaripalle is a village near Tirupati. It is located in Chandragiri mandal in Tirupati district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh.
  • Cherlopalle — Cherlopalle is a neighbourhood located in Tirupati city in Tirupati district of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It forms a part of Tirupati urban agglomeration and is located in Tirupati revenue division.
  • Sri Venkateswara Zoological Park — Sri Venkateswara Zoological Park is located in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India.
