St. Stanislaus Forane Church, Mala

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Stanislaus Forane Church is an active Syro-Malabar church located in Mala, Thrissur district, in Kerala, India.

  • Mala, Kerala — Mala is a small town in Thrissur district of Kerala state, India. There is a Jewish synagogue in Mala town.
  • Kuruvilassery — Kuruvilassery is a village in Thrissur district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Ashtamichira — Ashtamichira is a village in Thrissur district in the Indian state of Kerala. The vast paddy fields and coconut groves that surround Ashtamichira represent the core strength of the area, which is agriculture.
  • Kakkulissery — Kakkulissery is a small village in the southern end of Thrissur district in Chalakkudy taluka of Kerala, India. The language spoken by the majority of people in this area is Malayalam.

  • Iranikulam Sree Mahadeva Temple — Iranikulam Sree Mahadeva Temple is in Iranikulam, Mala in Thrissur district in the Indian state of Kerala. The temple has two main deities, Thekkadathappan and Vadakkedathappan, both versions of Shiva.
