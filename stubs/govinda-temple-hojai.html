Govinda Temple, Hojai

State: Assam | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Jagannath Govinda Temple is a Hindu temple in Gauranagar, in the Indian state of Assam which was established in 1928.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Komorakata — Komorakata (alternative spelling includes Kumurakata) is a village situated in Hojai district (formerly in Nagaon district) state of Assam, India. According to the 2011 Census of India, it has a population of 3,357.
  • Hojai — Hojai is a town and a municipal board in Hojai district in the Indian state of Assam. It is located on the banks of the two tributaries of Brahmaputra, namely Kapili and Jamuna.
  • Kolong river — The Kolong River or Kailang is an anabranch of the Brahmaputra River, which diverts out from the Brahamputra river in Hatimura region of Jakhalabandha (Nagaon district, Assam, India), and meets the same at Kolongpar near Guwahati. The tributary is about 250 kilometres (160 mi) long and flows through the districts of Nagaon, Morigaon and Kamrup.
  • Lanka (town) — Lanka is a town located in Hojai District of Assam, and a municipal board with 11 wards. It serves as a trade and commerce hub for nearby areas such as Udali, Nokhuti, Laskar Pather, Karikhana, Kheroni, Dablong, and Doiyong sides.
