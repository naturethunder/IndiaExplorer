Pokkunny Siva Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Pokkunny Siva Temple (Malayalam: പൊക്കുന്നി ശിവ ക്ഷേത്രം) is an ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva is situated at Vadavannur village of Alathur of Palakkad district in Kerala state in India. The temple is a part of 108 famous Shiva temples in Kerala. The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva. Legend has it that Lord Parasurama installed the Shiva Linga at the pond (Perumkulam) in Alathur.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Vadavannur — Vadavannur is a village and grama panchayat near to Kollengode Town in the Palakkad District of Kerala, India.
  • Oottara — Oottara is a small locality in Vadavannur Panchayat near Kollengode Town in Palakkad District of Kerala State in India. The Kollengode Railway station is located at Oottara.
  • Kollengode, Palakkad — Kollengode is a town in Palakkad district, Kerala, India. Kollengode Town is the headquarters of Kollengode Grama Panchayat and Kollengode Block Panchayat.
  • Pallassena — Pallassana is a village and gram panchayat in the Palakkad District, state of Kerala, India. It is close to Kollengode Town and Koduvayur and beside the river Gayatri, one of the tributaries of Bharatapuza.
  • Meenkulathi Temple — The Meenkulathi Temple is located in Pallassena, a village in southern India.
  • Puthunagaram — Puthunagaram is a town and gram panchayat in the Palakkad district, state of Kerala, India. It is about 10 km south of Palakkad and 8 Km from Kollengode.
