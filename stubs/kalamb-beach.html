Kalamb Beach

State: Maharashtra | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kalamb Beach is a long, isolated beach located in Nala Sopara, near Ahmedabad highway, Nala Sopara, near Nirmal, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Vasai Fort (Fort Bassein) — Magnificent 16th-century Portuguese fortress spanning over 110 acres, containing ancient stone ramparts, carved archways, cathedral ruins, and watchtowers overlooking Ulhas River.
  • Arnala Beach & Arnala Sea Fort — Picturesque island sea fort situated 12 km north of Vasai, constructed on an island off Arnala village with massive bastions surrounded by Arabian Sea waters.
  • Rajodi Beach — Popular beach for surfing, kite-boarding, and coastal cafes, flanked by scenic coconut groves and peaceful sea breezes.
