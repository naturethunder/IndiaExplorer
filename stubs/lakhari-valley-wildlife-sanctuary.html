Lakhari Valley Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Odisha | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Lakhari Valley Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Ganjam District, Odisha, India. The wildlife sanctuary has an area 118 km2 (46 sq mi), in the Lakhari Valley of the Eastern Ghats range. The plant communities include coastal Sal (Shorea robusta) forests and mixed deciduous forests. Lakhari Valley is located in the Eastern Highlands moist deciduous forests ecoregion.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Padmasambhava Mahavihara monastery — The Padmasambhava Mahavihara Monastery, also known as Thupten Mindolling Monastery located at Jeerango, Gajapati district, in the state of Odisha, India, as the main monastery belonging to the Ripa Lineage. It is said to be the largest Buddhist monastery in Eastern India.
