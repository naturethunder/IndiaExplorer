Nilakkal St.Thomas Church

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Nilakkal St. Thomas Ecumenical Church is an ecumenical Christian church located in Angamoozhy, Kerala, India. It is traditionally regarded as the modern successor to one of the Ezharappallikal (seven and a half churches) believed to have been originally established by St. Thomas in the 1st century AD. The modern structure was completed and consecrated in 1985 as the first ecumenical church of its kind, functioning as a shared place of worship managed by various Episcopal denominations in India.
