Bir Billing

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: adventure | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Bir Billing, in Kangra, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite adventure base. Visitors come for its paragliding, monasteries, tea gardens, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Billing Take-off — World-class paragliding launch site at 2,400m.
  • Bir Landing Site — Famous paragliding landing meadow with cafes.
  • Sherab Ling Monastery — Serene Tibetan monastery set among pine and tea slopes.
