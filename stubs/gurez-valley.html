Gurez Valley

State: Jammu & Kashmir | Category: hill_station | Best Time: May – Oct

Gurez is a pristine Himalayan valley located at 8,000 feet in northern Kashmir along the ancient Silk Route. Dominated by the pyramid-shaped Habba Khatoon peak, Gurez features crystal turquoise Kishanganga river waters and log cabin villages.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Habba Khatoon Mountain Peak — Iconic pyramid-shaped mountain peak named after legendary 16th-century poetess Habba Khatoon.
  • Kishanganga River & Dawar Town — Crystal clear turquoise alpine river cutting through log-cabin wooden houses of Dawar.
  • Tulail Valley Log Cabins — Untouched high altitude Himalayan valley past Sheikhpura featuring traditional wooden homes.
  • Habba Khatoon Natural Spring — Pure sweet spring water gushing out of limestone mountain rock.
  • Khandiyal Top 360 Viewpoint — High hill viewpoint offering 360-degree vistas over Kishanganga dam and Gurez valley.
  • Pentalwan Meadow Trek — Alpine wildflower meadow surrounded by dense pine forests.
