Vadakkan Koyikkal Devi Temple Puthiyavila

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Vadakkan Koyikkal temple, a Devi temple or Bhagavathi temple near Kayamkulam, is situated in Puthiyavila village, Alappuzha in Kerala. Sree Parvathy Devi is mainly worshiped in the temple. The other main idols of this temple are lord Shiva, Bhadrakaali and Sree Dharma Shastav. Idols of Naga devada, Khandakarna, Yekshi, Yogishwara and Brahmarakshas are also worshiped in this temple.
