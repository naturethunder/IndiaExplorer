Varanasi

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Varanasi is the spiritual heart of India and one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities. The Ganga ghats are the lifeblood of the city — from morning boat rides watching the sunrise to the spectacular evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Dashashwamedh Ghat Aarti — The most spectacular ghat ceremony in India. 7 priests perform simultaneous aarti.
  • Dawn Boat Ride on Ganga — See the ghats wake up from the river. Most meditative experience in India.
  • Kashi Vishwanath Temple — One of the 12 Jyotirlingas. Recently renovated with a grand new corridor.
  • Sarnath — Where Buddha gave his first sermon. Dhamek Stupa and Ashoka Pillar.
  • Manikarnika Ghat — The sacred cremation ghat — fires have burned continuously for centuries. Profound and humbling.
  • Assi Ghat — Southern-most and liveliest ghat for students, yogis and locals. Morning yoga on the steps.
