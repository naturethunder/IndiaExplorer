Chengalamma Parameshwari Temple

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Chengalamma Parameshwari Temple is located in Sullurpeta town of Tirupati district in the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh. It is located at the southern tip of Sullurpeta, on the banks of Kalangi river. The temple is situated 79 km, 84 km and 97 km from Chennai, Tirupati and Nellore respectively. It was established during the fourth and fifth centuries.

  • Nelapattu — Nelapattu is a small village in Doravarisatram mandal, Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh, India. It is within 10 km of the town of Sullurpeta.

  • Mavillapadu — Mavillapadu is a village in Varadaiahpalem mandal in Tirupati district in the state of Andhra Pradesh in India.
