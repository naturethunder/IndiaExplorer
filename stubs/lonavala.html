Lonavala

State: Maharashtra | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Jun – Sep, Oct – Feb

Lonavala, in Sahyadri, Maharashtra, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its waterfalls, forts, chikki, caves, with the best months being Jun – Sep, Oct – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bhushi Dam — Stepped dam where monsoon water cascades over the steps.
  • Tiger’s Leap — Cliff viewpoint with a sheer 650m drop over the valley.
  • Karla & Bhaja Caves — 2,000-year-old Buddhist rock-cut chaityas and viharas.
