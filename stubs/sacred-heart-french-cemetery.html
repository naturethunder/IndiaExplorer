Sacred Heart French Cemetery

State: West Bengal | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sacred Heart French Cemetery is a Roman Catholic cemetery located at Laldighi in Chandannagar, West Bengal, which is associated with the Sacred Heart Church.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Krishna Bhabini Nari Siksha Mandir — Krishna Bhabini Nari Siksha Mandir is a well-known girls school in Chandannagar, West Bengal, India. It was established in 1926 by Harihar Sett in the name of his mother Krishnabhabini Das.
  • Kanailal Vidyamandir — Kanailal Vidyamandir is one of the oldest schools in Hooghly district in the state of West Bengal, India. Established in 1862, the school is located at Grand Trunk Road in Chandannagar.
  • Sacred Heart Church, Chandannagar — The Sacred Heart Church (French: Église du Sacré-Cœur, pronounced [e.ɡliz dy sakʁe kœʁ]; pronounced [ˈpobit̪ːɾoˑ ˈɦɾid̪ɔe̯ ɡiɾdʒaˑ]) is a Roman Catholic heritage church situated at Chandannagar in Hooghly district of the Indian state of West Bengal. It is under the Archdiocese of Calcutta, and it is a heritage site declared by the West Bengal Heritage Commission.
  • Chandannagar — Chandannagar (Bengali pronunciation: [t͡ʃɔn.d̪ɔn.nɔ.ɡoɾ]), also known by its former names Chandernagor (French pronunciation: [ʃɑ̃.dɛʁ.na.ɡɔʁ]) and Chandernagore, is a city in the Hooghly district in the Indian state of West Bengal. It is headquarter of the Chandannagore subdivision and is a part of the area covered by Kolkata Metropolitan Development Authority (KMDA).
  • St Joseph's Convent, Chandannagar — St Joseph's Convent (SJC) is a Catholic all-girls school in Chandannagar, established in 1861 by the Sisters of St. Joseph of Cluny in the Archdiocese of Calcutta.
  • Sri Aurobindo Vidyamandir, Chandannagar — Sri Aurobindo Vidyamandir is an English medium school in Hatkhola, Chandannagar, West Bengal, India established in 1973.
