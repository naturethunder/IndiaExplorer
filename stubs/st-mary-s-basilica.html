St. Mary's Basilica

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Mary's Basilica is a church dedicated to Our Lady of the Nativity in Mulagumoodu, Tamil Nadu, India. It is the seventh basilica in the state of Tamil Nadu and the first in Kanyakumari district. The celebrations from 6 to 15 September every year are very popular.
