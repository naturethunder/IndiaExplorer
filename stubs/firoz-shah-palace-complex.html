Firoz Shah palace complex

State: Haryana | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Hisar-e-Firoza fort city with rampart (including the protected archaeological Firoz Shah palace complex, Lat-Ki-Masjid Complex with an Ashokan pillar) along with the Gujari Mahal and Charbagh Garden outside the rampart, located in the modern-day Hisar in Haryana state of India, was built by Firoz Shah Tughlaq of the Delhi Sultanate in 1354 CE on the Sindhu-Saraswati Valley Civilisation (SSVC) and Vedic Period archeological mound of ancient Isukāra city. Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gujri Mahal — 14th-century baradari palace built by Sultan Firoz Shah Tughlaq for his beloved Gujri queen, featuring dark stone construction and nine intricately carved bays.
  • Lat Ki Masjid & Ashokan Pillar — Historic mosque within the palace courtyard featuring an ancient polished stone Ashokan pillar repurposed from an earlier Buddhist sanctuary.
  • Pranpir Badshah Tomb — Magnificent 14th-century domed red sandstone mausoleum set upon a raised platform, commemorating the revered Sufi spiritual guide Baba Pranpir.
  • Blue Bird Lake — Lush wetland lake operated by Haryana Tourism, serving as a seasonal sanctuary for migratory waterfowl with boating decks and shaded picnic paths.
  • St. Thomas Church, Hisar — 1860s Gothic revival Victorian church consecrated in the British cantonment era, noted for pointed stone arches, bell belfry, and stained glass windows.
  • Hansi Fort (Asigarh Fort) & Barsi Gate — Ancient hill fortress attributed to Prithviraj Chauhan with the monumental 12th-century Barsi Gate displaying Sultanate archways and defensive bastions.
