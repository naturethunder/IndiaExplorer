Bekal Fort

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Bekal Fort is a medieval fort built by Shivappa Nayaka of Keladi in 1650 AD, at Bekal. It is the largest fort in Kerala, spreading over 40 acres (160,000 m2).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bekal Beach & Coastal Promenade — Broad golden sand beach sweeping along the base of Bekal Fort, equipped with illuminated walking paths and scenic Arabian Sea vantage points.
  • Chandragiri Fort — 17th-century square fortress built by Sivappa Nayaka of Bednore, commanding panoramic views of the Payaswini River merging into the Arabian Sea.
  • Ananthapura Lake Temple — Unique 9th-century Hindu temple standing serenely in the center of a rectangular sacred lake, regarded as the original seat of Ananthapadmanabha Swami.
  • Ranipuram Hills — Lush mountain ridge rising 750 meters above sea level, celebrated as the Ooty of Kerala with sweeping shola grasslands, evergreen forests, and trekking paths.
  • Malik Deenar Mosque — One of India's earliest mosques founded by Malik Ibn Dinar in the 7th century, showcasing quintessential Kerala timber architecture and ornate woodwork.
  • Kappil Beach, Kasaragod — Secluded golden coastline framed by swaying coconut groves and dramatic rocks, with Kodi cliff providing a majestic vantage over the Arabian Sea.
