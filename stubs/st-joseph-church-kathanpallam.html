St. Joseph Church, Kathanpallam

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The St. Joseph Church is a Roman Catholic parish church located in the heart of Kathanpallam village in Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu, India. This church comes under the administration of the Dhramapuri Diocese.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kathanpallam — Kathanpallam is a village located under the jurisdiction of Orappam Grama panchayat, Bargur block, Krishnagiri district in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. This village is 10 km (6.2 mi) away from the capital Krishnagiri.
  • Our Lady of Refuge Church, Elathagiri — Our Lady of Refuge Church, known locally as Adaikala Madha Church, is a 100-year-old church located in Elathagiri village, Tamil Nadu, India. It is the first parish church in Krishnagiri district.
  • Elathagiri — Elathagiri or Eluthagiri is a small village located in Krishnagiri district in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. This village is located 3 km away from Bangalore - Chennai Highway also 9 km away from the Krishnagiri District's capital, also called Krishnagiri.
  • Kandikuppam — Kandikuppam is a panchayat in Bargur Block, Krishnagiri district in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. The economy is dependent on mango cultivation.
  • Vinnarasi Madha Church, Kandikuppam — The Vinnarasi Madha Church, also called Our Lady of Queen of Heaven Church, is a Roman Catholic Parish Church in Kandikuppam village, Krishnagiri district, in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is situated on the Krishnagiri - Bargur National Highway NH48.
  • Kurumber Theru — Kurumber Theru (Kurumber street) is a village in Kandikuppam Panchayat, Bargur Block in Krishnagiri district in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. The village is surrounded in all directions by small numerous hillocks, greenery trees and plantations.
