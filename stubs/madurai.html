Madurai

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Madurai, in Vaigai Plains, Tamil Nadu, is one of India’s favourite spiritual town. Visitors come for its meenakshi temple, temple city, markets, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Meenakshi Amman Temple — Towering 14-gopuram temple complex, a marvel of Dravidian art.
  • Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace — 17th-century Indo-Saracenic palace with a nightly light show.
  • Gandhi Memorial Museum — Museum in a Nayak palace holding the cloth Gandhi wore when shot.
