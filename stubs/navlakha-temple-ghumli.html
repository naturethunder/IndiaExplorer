Navlakha Temple, Ghumli

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Navlakha Temple at Ghumli, Gujarat, India, is a 12th Century Sun temple built by Jethwa dynasty rulers.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ghumli — Ghumli is a village located 45 km from Jamkhambhaliya, at foothills of Barda, in Devbhumi Dwarka district of Gujarat, India.
  • Bhanvad — Bhanvad is a city and a municipality in Devbhoomi Dwarka district in the state of Gujarat, India.
  • Barda Wildlife Sanctuary — Barda Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Gujarat, India. It is situated approximately 31 kilometres (19 mi) from Ranavav, 49 kilometres (30 mi) from Porbandar and 100 kilometres (62 mi) west of Gir Forest National Park.
