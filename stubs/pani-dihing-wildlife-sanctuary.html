Pani Dihing Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Assam | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Pani-Dihing Bird sanctuary is a 33.93-square-kilometre (13.10 sq mi) bird sanctuary located in Sivasagar district, Assam. It is 22 km (14 mi) away from Sivasagar town. This protected area was established as a Bird Sanctuary in August 1996 by the Government of Assam. It was identified as a potential bird sanctuary in late 1980s.
