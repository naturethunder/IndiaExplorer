Singalila National Park

State: West Bengal | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Singalila National Park is a national park of India located on the Singalila Ridge at an elevation of more than 2,300 metres above sea level, in the Darjeeling district of West Bengal. It is well known for the trekking route to Sandakphu that runs through it.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Rimbick — Rimbick is a village in the Darjeeling Pulbazar CD block in the Darjeeling Sadar subdivision of the Darjeeling district in West Bengal, India.
  • Okhrey — Okhrey is a village situated in West Sikkim district, India.
  • Lodhoma — Lodhoma is a village and gram panchayat in the Darjeeling Pulbazar CD block in the Darjeeling Sadar subdivision of the Darjeeling district in the state of West Bengal, India.
  • Sandakphu — Sandakphu or Sandakpur at 3,636 m (11,929 ft) is a mountain peak in the Singalila Ridge on the border between India and Nepal. It is the highest point of the ridge and of the state of West Bengal, India.
  • Tumling — Tumling is a small hamlet in Jogmai VDC, Ilam District of Nepal. A tourist attraction in Eastern Himalayas at an altitude of 2970m(10,000 ft), its population is composed mostly of Gurung families, with the total population being a mere 15 in number.
  • Ambotey — Ambotey is a village between Daramdin and Sombaria in Soreng district, Sikkim, India. Several schools are located in or near Ambotey.
