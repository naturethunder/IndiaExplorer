Kaldurg Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kaldurg Fort is east of Palghar, Maharashtra, India, in the Sahyadri mountain range of northern Konkan. Its height is at least 475 meters above sea level, giving views of the Arabian Sea and the industrial town of Palghar to the west, as well as views of the Surya River, to the east. This rectangular shape of a fort can easily be located from a distance. There is no historic sign.
