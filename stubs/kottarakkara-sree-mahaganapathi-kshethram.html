Kottarakkara Sree Mahaganapathi Kshethram

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kottarakkara Sree Mahaganapathy Temple is a Hindu temple in Kottarakkara in the Indian state of Kerala. Though the central deity is Shiva, the temple is known for the shrine of Shiva's son Ganesha (Mahaganapathy). Non-Hindus are permitted.
