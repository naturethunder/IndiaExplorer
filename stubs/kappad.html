Kappad

State: Kerala | Category: beach | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kappad, or Kappakadavu locally, is a beach and village near Koyilandy, in the Kozhikode district, Kerala, India. A stone monument installed by government commemorates the "landing" by Vasco da Gama with the inscription, 'Vasco da Gama landed here, Kappakadavu, in the year 1498'. In 2007 a Rs. 1.5 crore program to beautify the beach was launched by [Kerala] Tourism Minister Kodiyeri Balakrishnan.

  • Chengottukavu — Chengottukavu is a village in Kozhikode district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Thalakkulathur — Thalakkulathur is a small village near Kozhikode city, along the road to Atholi, in the state of Kerala.
