Fort of São Tiago of Banastarim

State: Goa | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Fort of São Tiago of Banastarim in India, also known as Fort St. James Banastarim or Benastari Castle, is located at on the right bank of Cumbarjua Canal, on the eastern tip of Old Goa, North Goa district in the state of Goa on the west coast of India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Adcolna — Adcolna is a village in Ponda taluka, Goa, India. It is part of the Bhoma-Adconla village panchayat.
  • Tivrem — Tivrem is a village in the Ponda taluka (sub-district) of Goa, India.
  • Boma (Ponda) — Boma is a village in the Ponda taluka (sub-district) of Goa. It is located in the northern part of Ponda taluka.
  • Orgao — Orgao is a village in Ponda taluka, North Goa district in the Indian state of Goa. For the purposes of the census, it is considered a "Census Town".
  • Marcel, Goa — Marcel, also known as Mashel, is a village-turned-census town in the Ponda Sub-District, North Goa in the state of Goa, India. It is located in the Novas Conquistas region of the state.
  • Cumbarjua Canal — The Cumbarjua Canal is a distributary channel formed by the merger of the Mandovi and Zuari rivers in Goa, India. Its flow has led to the formation of river deltas; the most prominent of them are Ilhas de Goa, Cumbarjua and St Estevam.
