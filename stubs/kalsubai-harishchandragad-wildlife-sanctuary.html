Kalsubai Harishchandragad Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Maharashtra | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kalsubai Harishchandragad Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located in the northern Western Ghats of Maharashtra, India. Spanning approximately 361.71 km2, it is situated primarily within the Akole Tehsil of the Ahmednagar district. The sanctuary houses Mount Kalsubai, the highest peak in Maharashtra (1,646 m), and the historic hill fort of Harishchandragad. The rugged terrain of the sanctuary features moist deciduous and semi-evergreen forests.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ghanchakkar — Ghanchakkar 1,532 metres (5,026 ft) is the third highest peak in the Maharashtra State under Sahyadri mountain ranges in western India. Its neighbouring peak Muda 1,520 metres (4,990 ft) is the fourth highest peak in the Maharashtra State under Sahyadris.
  • Harishchandragad — Harishchandragad is a hill fort in the Ahilyanagar district of India. Its history is linked with that of Malshej Ghat, Kothale village and it has played a role in guarding and controlling the surrounding region.
  • Ratanwadi — Ratanwadi is a village in Ahmednagar district, Maharashtra, India.
  • Amruteshwar Temple — Amruteshwar Temple is an intricately carved Shiva temple in Ratanwadi. It is over 1200 years old and was built by the rulers of Shilahara dynasty in the 9th century CE. This is one of twelve Shiva temples built by King Jhanjha.
  • Bhandardara — Bhandardara is a holiday resort village on the Pravara River near Igatpuri, in the Western Ghats of India. The village is located in the Akole tehsil, Aahilyanagar district of the state of Maharashtra, about 185 kilometers (115 mi) from Mumbai, and 73 kilometers (45 mi) from Nashik.
  • Ratangad — Ratangad is a fort in Ratanwadi, Maharashtra, India, overlooking Bhandardara in the Ahmadnagar district of Maharashtra bordering the Thane district. The fort is about 400 years old.
