Torna Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Torna Fort, also known as Prachandagad, is a large fort located in Pune district, in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is historically significant because it was the first fort captured by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1646, at the age of 16. The hill has an elevation of 1,403 metres (4,603 ft) above sea level, making it the highest hill-fort in the district. The name derives from Prachanda (Marathi for huge or massive) and gad (Marathi for fort).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gunjwani Dam — Gunjwane Dam, is an earthfill and gravity dam on Kanandi river near Velhe, Pune district in state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Rajgad — Rajgad (literal meaning ruling fort) is a Hill region fort situated in the Pune district of Maharashtra, India. Formerly known as Murumbdev, the fort was the first capital of the Maratha Empire under the rule of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj for almost 26 years, after which the capital was moved to the Raigad Fort.
