Vagator Beach

State: Goa | Category: beach | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Vagator Beach a beach in Bardez Taluka, in the state of Goa, India. It is located across the Chapora River from the town of Morjim.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chapora Fort — Chapora Fort (Konkani pronunciation: [ʃɑːpoːɾɑː], Devanagari: शापोरा), located in Bardez, Goa, rises high above the Chapora River. The site is the location of a fort built by Adil Shahi dynasty ruler Adil Shah and called Shahpoora, whose name was altered to Shapora (chapora) by Malvankar on the request of the Portuguese.
  • Anjuna — Anjuna (, an-JOO-nə; Konkani pronunciation: [ɦɔɳzuɳẽ]) is a census town located on the coast of North Goa, India. It is one of the twelve comunidades of Bardez.
  • St. Michael's Church, Anjuna — St. Michael's Church is a historic Roman Catholic church located in Anjuna, Goa state, India.
  • Siolim — Siolim ([ʃiwoːlĩː]) is a village in Bardez taluka, and a census town on the central west coast of India, in the North Goa district of Goa. The 2001 population was 10,311, and 10,936 in 2011.
  • Zagor (folk drama) — Zagor (Konkani: जागर), (nocturnal vigil), mainly celebrated in Siolim, in Bardez taluka of Goa is a festival including dance, drama and music.
  • Little Vagator (Ozran Beach) — Little Vagator, also known as Ozran Beach, is a secluded cove nestled beneath dramatic laterite red cliffs. Renowned for its sculpted Shiva rock carving, tranquil palm-fringed shoreline, and spectacular sunset views over the Arabian Sea.
