Kugti Sanctuary

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Kugti Sanctuary is the second largest sanctuary in the state of Himachal Pradesh. Its situated at an altitude ranging from 2,195 m to 5,040 m. On the west side, Tundah Sanctuary surrounds this sanctuary by a forest corridor. The landscape of the sanctuary varies, and it is blessed with an abundance of flora and fauna.
