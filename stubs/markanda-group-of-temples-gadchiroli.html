Markanda Group of Temples, Gadchiroli

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Markanda Mahadev Temple Complex is situated at the banks of the Wainganga River near Chamorshi town in the Gadchiroli District of Maharashtra. This 8th Century Temple Complex is called Mini Khajuraho. The nearest village is Marakand Deo.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chamorshi — Chamorshi is a town and a tehsil in Gadchiroli district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
  • Por River — Por river (Marathi: पोर नदी) is a minor river in Gadchiroli district of Maharashtra. It is a minor tributary of Wainganga river and falls into it near Chamorshi.
  • Dhanora — Dhanora is a Village and a tehsil in Gadchiroli district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
