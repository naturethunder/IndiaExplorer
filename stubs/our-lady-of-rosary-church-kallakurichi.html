Our Lady of Rosary Church, Kallakurichi

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Our Lady of Rosary Church is a Roman Catholic, Kallakurichi, situated in Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu, India. Our Lady of Rosary Church, Kallakurichi has been recognized as a Parish under Diocese of Pondicherry.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gomukhi Dam & Reservoir — Gomukhi Dam is a major scenic reservoir located at the foothills of the Kalvarayan Hills near Kallakurichi. It offers picturesque views of misty mountain ranges, serene water expanse, and landscaped gardens ideal for picnics and nature walks.
  • Kalvarayan Hills — The Kalvarayan Hills are a major range of hills situated in the Eastern Ghats near Kallakurichi. Known for evergreen forests, cool altitude climate, tribal settlements, and cascading mountain streams.
  • Periyar Falls — Periyar Falls is a popular seasonal waterfall nestled within the dense Kalvarayan hill range. The crystal-clear waters cascade down natural rocky terraces, drawing travelers for refreshing dips and nature photography.
  • Somanatheeswarar Temple, Somandargudi — An ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva situated on the northern bank of the Gomuki River in Somandargudi village. Features historic granite mandapam, carved pillars, and tranquil riverfront atmosphere.
  • Kallakurichi Heritage Town — The bustling cultural and commercial hub surrounding the historic church, renowned for traditional artisan handlooms, local agrarian culture, and regional Tamil culinary heritage.
