Kallaperambur Lake Bird Sanctuary

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Kallaperambur Lake Bird Sanctuary is a protected area and bird sanctuary located in Thanjavur of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. The sanctuary covers an area of 0.64 km2 (0.25 sq mi) and was notified in 2015.
