Kamanda Mahadev

State: Uttarakhand | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Kamanda Mahadev (also revered locally as Kamadeshwar Mahadev) is an ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva situated in the scenic hills of Pauri Garhwal, approximately 2 kilometres north of Satpuli town. Perched amidst quiet pine-clad ridges overlooking the valley of the Purvi Nayar River, the temple is a venerated pilgrimage retreat known for its tranquil Himalayan surroundings, seasonal festivals, and deeply rooted spiritual sanctity.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Satpuli & Nayar River Valley — Tranquil riverside town at the confluence of the Eastern and Western Nayar rivers, known for angling, peaceful riverbanks, and scenic Garhwal hillscapes.
  • Jwalpa Devi Temple — Revered Shakti pilgrimage shrine on the banks of the Navalika (Nayar) River along the Kotdwar-Pauri road, surrounded by scenic green hills.
  • Lansdowne — Quiet, historic cantonment hill station set amidst dense pine and oak forests, offering panoramic snow views of the Chaukhamba and Trishul peaks.
  • Tarkeshwar Mahadev Temple — Ancient Shiva sanctuary situated at 2,092m altitude, nestled inside a dense sacred grove of towering deodar and cedar trees in the Garhwal Himalayas.
