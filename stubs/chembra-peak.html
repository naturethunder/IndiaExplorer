Chembra Peak

State: Kerala | Category: adventure | Best Time: Sep – May

Chembra Peak stands at 2,100 meters in Wayanad, offering one of Kerala's most scenic trekking routes. En route to the summit lies 'Hradayathadakam', a natural heart-shaped mountain lake that is believed to have never dried up.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Hradayathadakam (Natural Heart Lake) — Sacred natural mountain lake shaped like a heart situated at 1,500m elevation.
  • Chembra Peak Trailhead & Watchtower — Gateway through Banasura tea gardens offering views of Wayanad valley.
  • Banantara Tea Estate & Factory — Rolling green tea plantations offering guided factory visits and fresh tea tasting.
  • Soochipara (Sentinel Rock) Waterfalls — Three-tiered waterfall dropping down rock cliffs into a natural plunge pool.
  • Banasura Sagar Dam & Speedboating — Largest earthen dam in India featuring island chain views and speedboating.
  • Meppadi Ridge Viewpoint — Misty ridge viewpoint overlooking endless green Wayanad hills.
