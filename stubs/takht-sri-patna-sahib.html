Takht Sri Patna Sahib

State: Bihar | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Takht Sri Patna Sahib also known as Takhat Sri Harimandir Ji, Patna Sahib, is one of the five takhts of the Sikhs, located in Patna, Bihar, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Patan Devi — Patan Devi, also called Maa Patneshwari, is the oldest and one of the most sacred temples of Patna, Bihar. It is regarded as one of the 51 Siddha Shakta pithas in India.
  • Kamaldah Jain temple — The Kamaldah Jain temple, located in Patna, Bihar, is one of the oldest Jain temples belonging to the Śvētāmbara sect of Jainism.
  • Gulzarbagh — Gulzarbagh is a neighbourhood of Patna in the Bihar state of India. It is located at 25°36'0N 85°12'0E with an altitude of 45 metres (150 feet).
  • Gaighat, Patna — Gaighat is a neighbourhood of Patna in the eastern state of Bihar in India. It is located on the southern bank of the Ganges River in Patna.
  • Agam Kuan — Agam Kuan ("unfathomable well") is an ancient well and archaeological site in Patna, Bihar, India. It is said to date back to the period of Mauryan emperor, Ashoka (304–232 BCE).
  • Mahatma Gandhi Setu — Mahatma Gandhi Setu (also called Gandhi Setu or Ganga Setu) is a bridge over the river Ganga in Bihar, India, connecting Patna in the south to Hajipur in the north. Its length of 5,750 metres (18,860 ft) makes it the fourth-longest river bridge in India.
