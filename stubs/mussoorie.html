Mussoorie

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Mussoorie, in Garhwal, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its queen of hills, kempty falls, mall road, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kempty Falls — Mussoorie’s most popular tiered waterfall with bathing pools.
  • Gun Hill — Second-highest point, reached by ropeway, with Doon valley views.
  • Camel’s Back Road — 3-km scenic promenade ending at a sunset point over the hills.
