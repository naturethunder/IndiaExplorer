Chaya Someswara Swamy temple

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Chaya Someshwara Temple is a Shaivite Hindu temple located in Panagal, Nalgonda district of Telangana, India. It was built around the mid-11th century during the rule of the Kunduru Chodas (a branch of Telugu Cholas), supported and embellished further by later Hindu dynasties of Telangana. Some date it to late 11th to early 12th-century. The temple, now partially restored, was in ruins in mid 20th-century.
