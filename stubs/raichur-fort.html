Raichur Fort

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Raichur Fort is a fortress located on a hilltop in the heart of the Raichur in North Karnataka. The Raichur region (Raichur Doab) has been ruled by several families; the Kakatiya dynasty, Rashtrakutas, Vijayanagar Empire, Bahmanis and Nizams.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Raichur Fort Citadel & Inscriptions — Massive Kakatiya and Vijayanagara stone bastion complex famous for colossal mortarless cyclopean masonry and historic 13th-century rock inscriptions.
  • Ek Minar Mosque — 15th-century Bahmani architectural marvel featuring a unique 65-foot freestanding stone minaret with two external balconies and Persian inscriptions.
  • Malliabad Fort — Ancient mud and stone fortress built by the Kakatiyas in the 13th century, featuring colossal stone horse stables, ruined granaries, and perimeter trenches.
  • Krishna River Vistas at Raichur — Dramatic boulder-strewn rapids and expansive sandbanks of the sacred Krishna River, forming natural river pools amidst basalt outcrop landscapes.
  • Tungabhadra-Krishna Doab — The historic fertile interfluvial belt between the Tungabhadra and Krishna rivers, renowned for medieval watchtowers and riverine vistas.
  • Anegundi Heritage Village — Ancient mythological capital of the Kishkindha monkey empire, boasting prehistoric rock shelters, monolithic stone temples, and Tungabhadra river ghats.
