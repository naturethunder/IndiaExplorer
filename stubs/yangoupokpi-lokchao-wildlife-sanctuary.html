Yangoupokpi-Lokchao Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Manipur | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Spanning over 184 square kilometres along the Chandel and Tengnoupal border hills, Yangoupokpi-Lokchao Wildlife Sanctuary is one of Manipur’s most biologically diverse nature reserves. Featuring the perennial Lokchao River, dense teak forests, and bamboo thickets, it provides crucial habitat for serow, Himalayan black bear, barking deer, and rare hornbills.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Lokchao River Wildlife Corridor — The life-giving perennial river that bisects the sanctuary, creating lush riparian habitats where wild boars, sambar, and rare migratory waterfowl gather along boulder-strewn banks.
  • Tengnoupal Hill Viewpoint & Ridge — The highest mountain pass on the Imphal-Moreh route offering breathtaking 360-degree vistas across the emerald Manipur valley and the distant misty ranges of Myanmar.
  • Moreh Border Town & Friendship Bridge — A bustling commercial trading town on the international border with Myanmar, featuring Buddhist pagodas, vibrant Southeast Asian markets, and the historic Friendship Bridge across the border stream.
  • Maha Eco Park, Chandel — A scenic community-managed eco-park in Chandel set amidst tall pine and oak groves, offering tranquil walking trails, natural water springs, and birdwatching observation points.
