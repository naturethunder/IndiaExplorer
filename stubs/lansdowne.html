Lansdowne

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Lansdowne, in Garhwal, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its quiet, cantonment, pine forests, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tip in Top (Tiffin Top) — Viewpoint over the Shivalik range and the Garhwal Himalayas.
  • Bhulla Tal — Tranquil army-built lake with paddle boating.
  • St. Mary’s Church — 1896 colonial church now a small museum of Lansdowne’s history.
