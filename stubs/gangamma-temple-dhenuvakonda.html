Gangamma Temple, Dhenuvakonda

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Goddess Gangamma Temple is in the outskirts of Dhenuva Konda, Garlapadu village in Prakasam District of Andhra Pradesh, India. Sunday is preferable day to offer pooja in the temple. Goddess Gangamma is a powerful figure to the Yadava community. They celebrate Jatara for her on the full moon in April.
