Sivankoil (Raja Raja Choleshwar Mahadevar Temple)

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sivankoil, formally known as the Raja Raja Choleshwar Mahadevar Temple, is an ancient granite Shiva temple located in Thiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu. Commissioned during the reign of the illustrious Emperor Raja Raja Chola I in the early 11th century, the temple exemplifies classical Chola Dravidian architecture with finely carved granite bas-reliefs, a square sanctum vimana, ancient Tamil epigraphical inscriptions recording royal land endowments, and a tranquil rural temple setting.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Thiruvallur Veeraraghava Swamy Temple — One of the 108 Divya Desams, this grand Vaishnavite temple dedicated to Lord Veeraraghava features a colossal nine-tiered Rajagopuram and the massive Hrittapanasani Pushkarini tank.
  • Thiruvalangadu Vadaranyeswarar Temple — One of the five premier cosmic dance halls of Lord Shiva (Rathina Sabhai), where Nataraja performed the Oordhava Thandavam against Goddess Kali. Revered in the Thevaram hymns.
  • Jalandeeswarar Temple, Thakkolam — An ancient Paadal Petra Sthalam dating back to the Pallava and Chola eras, situated on the banks of the Kusasthalai River. Famous for its exquisite Dakshinamurthy shrine and historic inscriptions.
  • Othandeeswarar Temple — An ancient Chola-period Shiva temple at Thirumazhisai featuring intact granite inscriptions, ornate dvarapalaka statues, and a tranquil village theertham tank.
  • Cooum River Headwaters & Rural Wetlands — The pristine rural origin of the Cooum River near Kesavaram features expansive fresh wetlands, ancient water sluices, and agricultural greenery supporting regional water birds.
  • Perambakkam Historic Village — Perambakkam is a historic settlement renowned for its role in the 1780 Battle of Pollilur during the Anglo-Mysore War. Today it offers peaceful rural walks and traditional village temples.
