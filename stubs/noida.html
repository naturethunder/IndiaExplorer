Noida & Greater Noida

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: city | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Noida (New Okhla Industrial Development Authority) is a well-planned city in Uttar Pradesh bordering Delhi. Known for its wide tree-lined avenues, bird sanctuaries, motorsport circuits in Greater Noida, and premier shopping malls.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Okhla Bird Sanctuary — 4 sq km sanctuary at the Yamuna river entry host to 300+ bird species.
  • DLF Mall of India — One of India's largest shopping malls featuring 330+ international brands.
  • Buddh International Circuit — Formula 1 racing track in Greater Noida designed by Hermann Tilke.
  • Botanic Garden of Indian Republic — 160-acre botanical reserve preserving rare and endangered Indian plant species.
