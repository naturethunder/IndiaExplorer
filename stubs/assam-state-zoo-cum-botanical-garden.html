Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden

State: Assam | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Assam State Zoo cum Botanical Garden (popularly known as Guwahati Zoo) is the largest of its kind in the North East region and it is spread across 432 acre (175 hectare). The zoo is located within the Hengrabari Reserved Forest at Guwahati, India. The zoo is home to about more than 1125 numbers of animals, birds and reptiles representing over 115 species of animals and birds from around the world. Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Guwahati City Centre Mall — Guwahati City Centre Mall is a shopping complex located in Guwahati, Assam, India. It is located in Christian Basti and it is also one of the largest malls of Assam and Northeast India.
  • Ganeshguri — Ganeshguri is a locality in Guwahati, Assam, India, and is named after lord Ganesh. There was a Heramba kingdom.
  • Bhangagarh — Bhangagarh is a locality in Guwahati, Assam, India. It is surrounded by the localities of Paltan Bazaar, Chandmari and Ganeshguri.

  • Janata Bhawan — Janata Bhawan is the secretariat complex of the Government of Assam, located in Dispur, the capital of the Indian state of Assam. The complex houses the administrative offices of the state government and serves as the principal seat of executive governance in Assam.
  • Hengrabari — Hengrabari is a district in the capital city of Guwahati of Assam, a state on the north-east of India. Densely populated, Hengrabari resembles the character of a small town in the heart of Guwahati.
