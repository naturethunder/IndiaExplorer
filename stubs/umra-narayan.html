Umra Narayan

State: Uttarakhand | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Umra Narayan is a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu. The temple is 5–7 km from the main town of Rudraprayag in Uttarakhand, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Rudraprayag — Rudraprayag is a city and a municipality in Rudraprayag district in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. Rudraprayag is one of the Panch Prayag (five confluences) of Alaknanda River, the point of confluence of rivers Alaknanda and Mandakini.
  • Mandakini River — The Mandakini River is a tributary of the Alaknanda River in the Indian state of Uttarakhand. The river runs for approximately 81 kilometres (50 mi) between the Rudraprayag and Sonprayag areas and emerges from the Chorabari Glacier.
