Panchasara Parshwanath temple

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Panchasara Parshwanath Temple is a Śvetāmbara Jain temple located in Patan, Gujarat. The temple was constructed in 8th century during the reign of Vanaraja Chavda of Chavda dynasty.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Patan, Gujarat — Patan () is a city and headquarters of Patan district in the Indian state of Gujarat. Patan was the capital of Gujarat's Chavda and Chaulukya dynasties in medieval times.
  • Rani ki Vav — Rani ki Vav (lit. 'The Queen's Stepwell') is a stepwell situated in the town of Patan in Gujarat, India. It is located on the banks of the Saraswati River.
  • Sahasralinga Tank — Sahasralinga Tank or Sahasralinga Talav is a now-defunct medieval artificial water tank in Patan, Gujarat, India that was commissioned during the Chaulukya (Solanki) dynasty. It is a Monument of National Importance protected by the Archaeological Survey of India (N-GJ-161).
  • Odhava — Odhava is a small village located in Patan Taluka in Northern district of Patan, Gujarat, India.
  • Charup — Charup is a village in Saraswati Taluka of Patan district of Gujarat state of India.
