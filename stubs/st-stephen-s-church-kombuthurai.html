St. Stephen's Church, Kombuthurai

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

St. Stephen's Shrine is a Roman Catholic church dedicated to Saint Stephen, located in the village of Kombuthurai in the Thoothukudi district of Tamil Nadu, India. The church was built by St. Francis Xavier in May 1544.
