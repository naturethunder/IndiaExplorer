Adhirangam Ranganathaswamy temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Adhirangam Ranganathaswamy temple or Old Srirangam Ranganatha Perumal Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu located in Adhi Thiruvarangam in the Taluk of Sankarapuram, Tamil Nadu, India. Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, it is believed to have been built by the Medieval Cholas and Vijayanagar kings. It covers an area of 5 acres (20,000 m2) and has a historical grain storage container. It is classified one among the 108 Abhimana Kshethram of Vaishnavate tradition.
