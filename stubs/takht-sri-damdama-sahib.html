Takht Sri Damdama Sahib

State: Punjab | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Takht Sri Darbar Sahib Damdama Sahib, is one of the five takhts or Seat of Temporal Authority of Sikhism, located in Talwandi Sabo, near the city of Bathinda in Bathinda district of Punjab, India. At this place Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Sikh Guru, prepared the full version of the Sikh scriptures called Sri Guru Granth Sahib in 1705. The other four Takhts are the Akal Takht Sahib, Takht Sri Keshgarh Sahib, Takht Sri Patna Sahib and Takht Sri Hazur Sahib.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Qila Mubarak (Bathinda Fort) — Monumental 1,400-year-old brick fortification built in the 6th century, famous as the site where Razia Sultana was imprisoned with 30-meter high ramparts.
  • Gurdwara Likhansar Sahib — Venerated shrine where Guru Gobind Singh Ji sanctified the reeds and inkwells before Bhai Mani Singh Ji transcribed the sacred Guru Granth Sahib.
  • Damdama Sahib Holy Sarovar — Sacred water reservoir reflecting the majestic white marble sanctum and gold-plated domes of Takht Sri Damdama Sahib.
  • Chetak Park & Bathinda Lakes — Expansive municipal green parkland with serene recreational boating lakes, landscaped flowerbeds, and shaded walking avenues.
  • Maiser Khana Temple — Renowned historic shrine located on the Bathinda-Mansa road, constructed to honor Goddess Jwala Ji with traditional northern Indian temple architecture.
  • Bathinda Rose Garden — Beautiful 10-acre botanical garden cultivating dozens of rare rose varieties, illuminated water fountains, and manicured lawns.
