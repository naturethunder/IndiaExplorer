Sakhigopal Temple

State: Odisha | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sakhigopal Mandir (formally Satyabadi Gopinatha Mandir) is a medieval Hindu temple dedicated to Gopinath located in Sakhigopal on the Bhubaneshwar - Puri Highway in Odisha. The mandir is built in the Kalinga Architecture style.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sakhigopal — Sakhigopal or Satyabadi is a town in Puri district in the Indian state of Odisha.
  • Lokanatha Temple — Lokanatha Temple is a Hindu temple in the town of Puri, Odisha, India. It is dedicated to the god Shiva as Lokanatha.
  • Patuari — Patuari is a village in the borough of Sakhigopal on the River Bhargavi in Puri district of the Indian state of Odisha. It is located about 17 km north from district headquarters Puri and 42 km from state capital Bhubaneswar.
  • Biranarasingh Pur — Biranarasingh Pur is one of the oldest sasana village in Puri district in Odisha. The village is named after the great king of Odisha Gajapati Narasingh Dev.
  • Raghurajpur — Raghurajpur is a heritage crafts village in Puri district, Odisha, India, known for its master Pattachitra painters, an art form which dates back to 5 BC in the region, and Gotipua dance troupes, the precursor to the Indian classical dance form of Odissi. It is also known as the birthplace of the Odissi exponents Padma Vibhushan Guru and Kelucharan Mohapatra and the Gotipua dancer Padma Shri Guru Maguni Charan Das.
  • Malatipatapur — Malatipatapur is a village in Sadar Tehsil in Puri District of India's Odisha state. It is a small village located 6 km towards North from District headquarters Puri and 53 km from State capital Bhubaneswar.
