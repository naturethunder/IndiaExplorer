Basilica of Our Lady of the Assumption, Secunderabad

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Basilica of Our Lady of the Assumption (commonly known as St. Mary's Church, Secunderabad) is a minor basilica located in Secunderabad, India. The decree designating it as a basilica was issued on 7 November 2008. The church is located on Sarojini Naidu Road in Secunderabad.
