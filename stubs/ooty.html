Ooty

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov

Ooty (Udhagamandalam) is the principal hill station of the Nilgiri Hills at 2,240m. Famous for its Botanical Gardens, the UNESCO-listed Nilgiri Mountain Railway (toy train), Ooty Lake, and the surrounding tea and eucalyptus estates.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Government Botanical Gardens — 55-acre garden with a 20 million year old fossil tree trunk.
  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway — UNESCO toy train from Mettupalayam to Ooty via Coonoor. Breathtaking views.
  • Doddabetta Peak — Highest peak in the Nilgiris at 2,637m. Telescope house at the summit.
  • Ooty Lake Boating — Artificial lake created in 1824. Row boats and motor boats on the eucalyptus-fringed lake.
  • Rose Garden — Largest rose garden in India with 2,000+ varieties. Best in April–May bloom.
  • Avalanche Lake — Pristine trout-stocked lake surrounded by sholas and grasslands. Great for fishing.
