St. Sebastian's Church

State: Delhi | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. Sebastian's Church is located in Dilshad Garden in the city of Delhi, India. The church is dedicated to Saint Sebastian.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Dilshad Garden — Dilshad Garden is a residential area of Shahdara district, which was planned and developed by Delhi Development Authority in the late 1970s and early 1980s. It falls under the both Shahdara and Seemapuri constituency of the legislative assembly.
  • Ziauddin Pur — Ziauddin Pur is a village and census town in Mustafabad in North East district in the Indian state of Delhi.
  • Jhilmil metro station — The Jhilmil Station is located on the Red Line of the Delhi Metro.
  • Dilshad Garden metro station — The Dilshad Garden metro station is a station of the Red Line of the Delhi Metro. It was the terminal station of the Red Line till 7 March 2019.
  • Mansarovar Park metro station — The Mansarovar Park metro station is located on the Red Line of the Delhi Metro, India.
  • Shaheed Nagar metro station — The Shaheed Nagar is a metro station located on the Red Line of the Delhi Metro. It is located in the Sahibabad Industrial Area locality of Ghaziabad of Uttar Pradesh.
