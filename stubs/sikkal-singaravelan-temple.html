Sikkal Singaravelan Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sikkal Singara Velar Temple is one of the most popular Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Muruga and a contender for the not unofficial seventh Padaiveedu of Muruga, along with the popular Arupadaiveedu (six abodes of Lord Muruga). It is one of the rare traditional Hindu temples that has both Shiva and Vishnu deities in the same complex.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sikkal — Sikkal is a small township located between Thiruvarur and Nagapattinam in Tamil Nadu, India. It is 6 km west of Nagapattinam, 18 km east of Thiruvarur.
  • Puliyur, Nagapattinam — Puliyur is a village in Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu, India. It is situated 7 km from Nagapattinam, District Headquarters and 3 km from Sikkal.
  • Nagapattinam taluk — Nagapattinam taluk is a taluk of Nagapattinam district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
  • Nagapattinam block — Nagapattinam block is a revenue block in the Nagapattinam taluk of Nagapattinam district, Tamil Nadu, India. There are a total of 27 panchayat villages in this block.
  • Nagapattinam division — Nagapattinam division is a revenue division of Nagapattinam district in Tamil Nadu, India. It comprises the taluks of Kilvelur, Nagapattinam, Thirukkuvalai and Vedaranyam.
  • Loganatha Perumal Temple — The Loganatha Perumal Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu located 2 km away from Sikkal, Tamil Nadu, India on the Tiruvarur-Nagapatnam highway. Constructed in the Dravidian style of architecture, the temple is glorified in the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, the early medieval Tamil canon of the Alvar saints from the 6th–9th centuries CE. It is one of the 108 Divya Desams dedicated to Vishnu, who is worshiped as Loganatha Perumal and his consort Lakshmi as Loganayagi.
