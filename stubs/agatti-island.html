Agatti Island

State: Lakshadweep | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – May

Agatti Island is an idyllic 7.6 km long coral atoll in the Union Territory of Lakshadweep. Celebrated for its breathtaking shallow turquoise lagoon, world-class scuba diving, colorful living coral reefs, and sea turtle habitats, Agatti serves as the scenic entry point to the Lakshadweep archipelago.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Agatti Turquoise Lagoon & Coral Reef — An expansive shallow coral lagoon extending across several square kilometers. World-renowned for crystal transparency, snorkeling among parrotfish and sea turtles, kayaking, and glass-bottom boating.
  • Eastern Jetty & White Sand Beach — A serene white coral sand beach flanked by tall coconut palms and a wooden jetty. Offers calm waters for swimming and spectacular views of local fishing catamarans during sunrise.
  • South Point Sandspit & Coral Shoals — The narrow southern sandspit where the calm turquoise lagoon converges with the deeper sapphire waters of the Arabian Sea. Ideal for sunset walks and observing marine bird life.
