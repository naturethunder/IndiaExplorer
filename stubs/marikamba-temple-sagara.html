Marikamba Temple, Sagara

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Marikamba Temple is located in Sagara in the Shimoga district in the Indian state of Karnataka. It features the image of the goddess Marikamba Devi. The temple was built in the center of the city during the reign of Venkatappa Nayaka who ruled over Keladi and Ikkeri kingdom during the 16th century. Marikamba was the family deity of the Nayakas of Keladi.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sagara, Karnataka — Sagara is a city located in the Indian state of Karnataka. It is also a sub divisional and a taluk headquarters.
  • Ikkeri — Ikkeri (Kannada: [ikːeːri]) is a hamlet situated in Sagara taluk (township) about 6 km to the south of the town centre in Sagara. It is known for the Aghoreshvara Temple, dedicated to an avatar of Shiva.
  • Keladi — Keladi (Kannada: [keɭɐd̪i]) is a temple town in Sagara Taluk of the state of Karnataka in India. Keladi is located about 8 km from the town of Sagara.
  • Adaranthe — Adaranthe is a village in the southern state of Karnataka, India. It is located in the Sagara taluka of Shimoga district in Karnataka.
  • Heggodu — Heggodu is a village located in the Sagar Taluk of Karnataka. It is a very important cultural and educational hub in South India, because of the organizations at this place.
  • Holebaagilu, Sagara — Holebaagilu is situated on the banks of the Sharavati River about 30 km (19 mi) from Sagar, Karnataka. Drivers have to take a diversion from B. H. Road towards Ikkeri Road to get there.
