Shri Vaijnath Temple

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shri Vaidyanatha Temple (Marathi श्री वैजनाथ मंदिर ) is a temple to Shiva in Parli Vaijnath in the Beed district of Maharashtra, India. While performing the rite of Abhishekam male devotees are not allowed to wear clothes above the waist. It's the 5th Jyotirlinga among 12 jyotirlinga of lord Shiva.

  • Wan Dam, Ambejogai — Wan Dam, also known as Daunapur Dam, is an earthfill dam on Wan river near Ambejogai, Beed district in the state of Maharashtra in India.
