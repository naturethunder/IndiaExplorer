Camp Hanuman Temple

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Shree Camp Hanuman Temple (Hindi: कैंप हनुमान मंदिर) is one of the biggest Hanuman temples in India. It is situated in Ahmedabad Cantonment in Shahibaug, Ahmedabad in the Indian state of Gujarat. The temple is owned by Shri Hanumanji Mandir Camp Trust Ahmedabad.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • AEC metro station — AEC is an elevated metro station on the North-South Corridor of the Red Line of Ahmedabad Metro in Ahmedabad, India. Around this metro station holds the main and upcoming Sabarmati HSR Terminal railway station along with AEC Garden Park and Torrent Power Limited.
  • Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Memorial — The Moti Shahi Mahal is a palace built by the Mughal emperor Shahjahan between 1616 and 1623, when he was still a prince. It hosts the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Memorial, a museum and exhibition centre dedicated to Vallabhbhai Patel in Shahibaug, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, near Civil Hospital, Ahmedabad.
  • Shahibaug — Shahibaug is a neighborhood in the city of Ahmedabad, India. It is primarily inhabited by the Marwadi and Jain communities, while the Dudheshwar area in the west of Shahibaug has sizeable populations of Muslims and migrants from other states.
  • Bhadra Fort & Teen Darwaza — Historic 15th-century royal fortified citadel and triumphal arched gateway located in historic Ahmedabad.
  • Subhash Bridge — Subhash Bridge is a beam bridge over the Sabarmati river in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, It was built in 1973 and demolished in 2026.
  • Miya Khan Chishti's Mosque — The Miya Khan Chishti's Mosque, or more correctly, the Miya Khan Chishti's Mosque and Tomb, is a Sufi mosque and dargah complex, located on the bank of Sabarmati River in the Shahibaug area of Ahmedabad, in the state of Gujarat, India.
