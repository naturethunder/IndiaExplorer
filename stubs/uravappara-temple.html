Uravappara Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sree Subramanya Swami Temple, also known by the name 'Malayala Palani' (Palani of Kerala), is located in Olamattom near Thodupuzha in Idukki district in the Indian state of Kerala. It is situated at a height of 500 ft (150 m) above mean sea level. Lord Muruga presides there in the form 'Bala Subramanya'. The legend has it that the idol here is a self-born one.
