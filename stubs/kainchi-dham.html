Kainchi Dham

State: Uttarakhand | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Kainchi Dham also known as Neem Karori Ashram is a spiritual ashram and Hindu temple complex, located at Kainchi in the Nainital district, Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, India. It is known for its association with Neem Karoli Baba, a revered Hindu saint. The ashram was established by Neem Karoli Baba in 1964 on land donated by a local resident, Purnanand. Kainchi Dham is situated at a distance of 9 km from Bhowali and 17 km from Nainital, on the Nainital–Almora Road.
