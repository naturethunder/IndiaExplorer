Bethel Marthoma Church

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Bethel Marthoma Church is a Mar Thoma Syrian Church located at Chengara, Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India. Bethel Marthoma Church belongs to the Ranni Nilackal Diocese of Mar Thoma Syrian Church. It is the biggest church in Chengara in terms of membership. Like all other Malankara Churches, St James liturgy is used in Bethel Marthoma Church for worship.

  • Thannithode — Thannithode is a village in Pathanamthitta district in the state of Kerala, India. It is near Konni town and is mainly a plantation township.
  • Malayalappuzha — Malayalapuzha is a village outside the city suburbs of Pathanamthitta in Kerala. It is seven kilometers away from Pathanamthitta central junction and five kilometers from Kumbazha junction.
  • Muringamangalam Sreemahadevar Temple — The Muringamangalam Sreemahadevar Temple, located half a kilometre from Konni Junction, is more than 1000 years old. It used to belong to the Pandalam Royal Family.
  • Konni, Kerala — Konni is a Grama Panchayat and Taluk headquarters in Pathanamthitta district of Kerala, India. Konni is known for its elephant cages, forests, and rubber plantations.
  • Mount Bethany EHSS — Mount Bethany English Medium Higher Secondary School is a school in Kerala, India. The school began providing education to lower primary level classes in the late 1970s.
