Rangana Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Rangana Fort/ Prasiddhagad (Marathi: रांगणा किल्ला) is located in the Bhudargad taluka of Kolhapur district. This fort is one of the important forts in the district. It is located on the Sahyadri mountain ridge, on the boundary of the Sindhudurg and Kolhapur districts.
