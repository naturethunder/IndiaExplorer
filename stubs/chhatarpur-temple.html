Chhatarpur Temple

State: Delhi | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shri Aadya Katyayani Shakti Peeth is a Hindu temple dedicated to the Hindu deity Katyayani. The entire complex of the temple is spread over a wide area of 28 hectares (70 acres). It is located in Chhatarpur, on the southwestern outskirts of the New Delhi. It is the 2nd largest temple in Delhi, after the Akshardham Temple.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chhatarpur metro station — Chhatarpur is an elevated station on the Yellow Line of the Delhi Metro. It is located in the Chhatarpur locality of the South West district of Delhi, India.
  • Qutab Minar metro station — Qutab Minar is an elevated station on the Yellow Line of the Delhi Metro. It was inaugurated on 21 June 2010 as part of the 14.47 km completely elevated corridor from Qutab Minar – HUDA City Centre.
  • Hauz-i-Shamsi — Hauz-i-Shamsi (literally "sunny water tank"), also known as Shamsi Talab, is a water reservoir built by Iltutmish of the Slave Dynasty in 1230 CE. According to legend, its location was revealed to him in a dream by the Islamic prophet Muhammad. A palace known as the Jahaz Mahal was constructed on the eastern edge of the same reservoir during the Lodi dynasty in the 16th century, serving as a retreat or inn for pilgrims.
  • Jahaz Mahal — Jahaz Mahal (lit. 'Ship Palace') is located next to Hauz-i-Shamsi in Mehrauli, Delhi. It was so named, since its reflection (illusion) in the surrounding reservoir looked like a ship floating on a lake.
  • Sultanpur metro station — The Sultanpur metro station is located on the Yellow Line of the Delhi Metro. During Peak hours, it serves as an alternate southern Terminus for the yellow line along with Qutab Minar.
  • Sultanpur, Delhi — Sultanpur is a village in South Delhi district of Delhi. According to the 2011 census, there are 3280 houses, two government schools and three national banks.
