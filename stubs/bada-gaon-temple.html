Bada Gaon temple

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bada Gaon temple is a sacred temple located in Uttar Pradesh, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Trilok Teerth Dham, Bada Gaon and Supercomputing Facility for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology. The best time to visit is Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Trilok Teerth Dham — Trilok Teerth Dham is a Jain temple in Bada Gaon, Baghpat, Uttar Pradesh, India.
  • Bada Gaon — Rawan Urf Bada Gaon is a village near Khekra, a town in the district of Bagpat, Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for Maa Mansa Devi Temple, This Temple was established by Ravana.
  • Supercomputing Facility for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology — Supercomputing Facility for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, also known as SCFBio IIT Delhi, is a center for Bioinformatics & Computational Biology in Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi. This facility was established in 2002 under the aegis of the Department of Chemistry, IIT Delhi.
  • Nangla Badi — Nangla Badi is a village located in the Khekra tehsil, Baghpat district in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. It is a rapidly developing village near Delhi.
  • Rataul — Rataul is a town and a nagar panchayat located in the Khekra tehsil, in the Baghpat district, Uttar Pradesh state, India. It is 25 km (16 mi) from the Indian capital of Delhi, and also 7 km (4.3 mi) from Loni.
  • Chamrawal — Chamrawal is a village in Bagpat district, Uttar Pradesh, India. Chamrawal is located at a distance of 6.556 km from Pilana town, and 16.25 km from Baghpat town.
