Manesar & Damdama Lake

State: Haryana | Category: nature | Best Time: Sept – Apr

Manesar & Damdama Lake form a relaxing rustic retreat just 45 km from Delhi. Surrounded by the rolling Aravalli ranges, Damdama Lake is one of Haryana's biggest natural lakes, offering boating, rock climbing, camping, and eco-resorts.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Damdama Lake — Scenic 3,000-acre lake under the Aravalli hills ideal for boating, kayaking, and camping.
  • Heritage Village Manesar — Sprawling Rajasthani haveli-style resort with lush lawns and wellness spa.
  • Botanix Nature Resort — 30-acre adventure farm park nestled on the banks of Damdama Lake.
