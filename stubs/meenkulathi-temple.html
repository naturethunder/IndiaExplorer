Meenkulathi Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Meenkulathi Temple is a sacred temple located in Kerala, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Pallassena, Kakkayur and Koduvayur. The best time to visit is Nov – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pallassena — Pallassana is a village and gram panchayat in the Palakkad District, state of Kerala, India. It is close to Kollengode Town and Koduvayur and beside the river Gayatri, one of the tributaries of Bharatapuza.
  • Kakkayur — Kakkayur is a small village between Koduvayur, Pallavur and Kunisseri in Palakkad district of Kerala state, south India. Kakkayur is on the Palakkad Nemmara route about 13 km from Palakkad town.
  • Koduvayur — Koduvayur is a Census Town and Grama Panchayat in Palakkad district in the state of Kerala, India. Koduvayur Panchayat comprises Koduvayur-I and Koduvayur-II villages.
  • Elavancherry — Elavanchery is a village and gram panchayat near Kollengode Town in Palakkad district, Kerala, India. It is situated on the foothills of Nelliyampathy mountain ranges, a part of the Western Ghats.
  • Vadavannur — Vadavannur is a village and grama panchayat near to Kollengode Town in the Palakkad District of Kerala, India.
  • Pallavur — Pallavur is a village situated in the Chittur Taluk, Palakkad district of Kerala, India. It is close to Kollengode Town, Nemmara and Koduvayur.
