Bajwara Fort

State: Punjab | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Bajwara Fort, also known as Qila Bajwara or Kila Bajwara, is a qila fortress located in Bajwara, Hoshiarpur, Punjab, India that is spread over six to seven acres. The fortress' origin is unknown and it fell into the hands of various rulers. The fortress is endangered due to lack of maintenance and is at risk of total collapse.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bajwara — Bajwara is an old historic town situated in Hoshiarpur District. It is birthplace of Mata Sundri, first wife of Guru Gobind Singh.
  • Ram Colony Camp — Ram Colony Camp is a suburb of Hoshiarpur city situated on Chandigarh-Hoshiarpur highway. It is 138 km from state capital Chandigarh.
  • Hoshiarpur — Hoshiarpur (Punjabi: [ɦʊʃɪˈaːɾpʊɾ] ) is a city and a municipal corporation in Hoshiarpur district in the Doaba region of the Indian state of Punjab. It was founded, according to tradition, during the early part of the fourteenth century.
  • Boothgarh — Boothgarh is a medium-size village located in Khanna Tehsil of Ludhiana district in Punjab, India with a total of 254 families residing. The Boothgarh village has a population of 1190 of which 616 are males while 574 are females as per Population Census 2011.
  • Badial — Badial is a small village in the area of Dona in Kapurthala, Punjab, India with a population around 500. It is situated on the road Kala Sanghia-Sidwa Dona.
  • Singriwala — Singriwala is a village in Hoshiarpur district, in Punjab, India.
