Sri Trilinga Sanghameshwara swamy temple

State: Telangana | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sri Sri Sri Trilinga Sanghameshwara Swamy Devalayam is located in Vilasagar village of Karimnagar district in the state of Telangana, India. It is one of the unique ancient Shiva temples where three linga's in three inner sanctums (garbha gruha) in one temple facing east, north and south. Sri Trilinga Sanghameshwara swamy temple is situated 23 kilometers from Karimnagar town and 12 kilometers from Vemulawada known as “southern kasi”.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bommalagutta — Bommalagutta (also known as Siddhula Gutta, Bommalamma Talli Gutta and Vrushabhadri Hill) is a Jain centre situated near Kurikyala village of Karimnagar district in Telangana. This Jain center is situated 3 km from another famous Jain center Kulpakji.
  • Kothapalli (Haveli) — Kothapalli (Haveli) (Telugu: కొత్తపల్లి), or Kothapalli, is a Revenue Village and a semi-town in Kothapalli Mandal, Karimnagar district, Telangana, India. The village used to be in the Karimnagar mandal of the old Karimnagar district.
  • Boinpalle mandal — Boinpalle or Boinpalli is a mandal in Rajanna Sircilla district in the state of Telangana in India.
