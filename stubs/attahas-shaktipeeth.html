Attahas Shaktipeeth

State: West Bengal | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The temple of Attahas, is believed to be one of the 51 Shakta pithas at which the body parts and jewelry of the Hindu goddess Shakti fell to Earth. The goddess is so large that the lower lip is about 15 to 18 feet wide. The temple is a site of Hindu pilgrimage throughout the year. December is a particularly popular time for visitors to picnic at the site.
