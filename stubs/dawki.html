Dawki

State: Meghalaya | Category: nature | Best Time: Nov – Apr

Dawki is a scenic border town in Meghalaya world-famous for the incredible transparency of the Umngot River. Visitors can enjoy wooden boat rides, riverside camping at Shnongpdeng, and views of the Bangladesh border.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Umngot River — Crystal-clear river where boats appear to float in mid-air over visible riverbed pebbles.
