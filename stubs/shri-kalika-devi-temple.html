Shri Kalika Devi Temple

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Shri Kalika Devi Temple is a sacred temple located in Karnataka, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Basidoni, Kadlikoppa and Manikatti. The best time to visit is Nov – Feb.

  • Kadlikoppa — Kadlikoppa is a village in Belagavi in Ramdurg Karnataka, India. Kadlikoppa has a population of 1,864 people.
  • Manikatti — Manikatti is a village in Belgaum district of Karnataka, India.
  • Lakhanayakanakoppa — Lakhanayakanakoppa is a village in Belgaum district in Karnataka, India.
  • Dodamangadi — Dodamangadi is a village in Belgaum district in the southern state of Karnataka, India.
