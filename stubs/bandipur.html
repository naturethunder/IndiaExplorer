Bandipur

State: Karnataka | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – May

Bandipur, in Chamarajanagar, Karnataka, is one of India’s favourite wildlife reserve. Visitors come for its tigers, elephants, safari, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bandipur Jeep Safari — Tiger reserve safari through teak and bamboo forest.
  • Himavad Gopalaswamy Betta — Misty hilltop temple, highest point in the Bandipur range.
  • Moyar River Gorge — Dramatic gorge teeming with raptors and crocodiles.
