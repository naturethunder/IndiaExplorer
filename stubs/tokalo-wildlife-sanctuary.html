Tokalo Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Mizoram | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Tokalo Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located in the Siaha district of Mizoram, India. Established on October 1, 2007, under the administration of the Mara Autonomous District Council (MADC), it spans 250 km2 of moist deciduous and semi-evergreen forests. Situated near the Indo-Myanmar border at an altitude of 700 to 940 meters, the sanctuary is a critical biodiversity hotspot. It supports rare fauna including the Clouded Leopard (Neofelis nebulosa), Hoolock Gibbon, and Phayre's leaf monkey, and it has a rich diversity of butterflies.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kaisi Tlang — Kaisi Tlang is a majestic mountain range rising above the Tokalo wilderness in southern Mizoram, offering unspoiled panoramic views and virgin trekking trails.
