Majuli

State: Assam | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Majuli, in Brahmaputra, Assam, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its river island, satras, mask making, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kamalabari Satra — Neo-Vaishnavite monastery of dance, music and manuscripts.
  • Mask Making at Samaguri — Traditional workshop crafting expressive bamboo-and-clay masks.
  • Brahmaputra Sunset Point — Sweeping sunset views over the world’s largest river island.
