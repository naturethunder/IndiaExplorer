Muthyalamaduvu

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Muthyalamaduvu is a tourist attraction located near Anekal, Bengaluru Urban, Karnataka. The place is also known as Pearl Valley. It is located 5km from Anekal and 40km from Bangalore. From the Anekal-Thally Road, visitors take a right turn to reach Muthyalamaduvu.
