Anniyur Agnipureeswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Anniyur Agnipureeswarar Temple (அன்னியூர் அக்கினிபுரீஸ்வரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located at Vanniyur in Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, India. The presiding deity is Shiva. He is called as Agnipureeswarar. His consort is known as Gowri Parvathi.
