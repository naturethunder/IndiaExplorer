Sitala Mata Temple, Butapaldi

State: Gujarat | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Shitala Mata Temple is a c. 12th century Hindu temple dedicated to goddess Shitala located in Butapaldi village of Mehsana district, Gujarat, India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bhandu — Bhandu is a village in Visnagar Taluka of Mahesana district in Gujarat, India.
  • Bokarvada — Bokarvada is a village in Visnagar Taluka of Mahesana district in Gujarat, India.
  • Dudhsagar Dairy plant — The Dudhsagar Dairy plant is a milk processing plant located in Mehsana, Gujarat, India. Owned and operated by Dudhsagar Dairy, a cooperative, it is a postmodern brutalist building designed by Achyut Kanvinde.
  • Biladi Baug — Biladi Baug, officially known as Mahatma Gandhi Garden, is an urban park in Mehsana, Gujarat, India. It was established in 1946 and is being redeveloped in 2025 by the Mahesana Municipal Corporation.
  • Rajmahal, Mehsana — Rajmahal is a palace in Mehsana, Gujarat, India. Built in 1904 by Sayajirao Gaekwad III of Baroda State, it was used as the government office and later as the court until 2017.
  • Sardar Patel Stadium, Mehsana — Sardar Patel Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium and sports complex at Municipal Ground, Mehsana, Gujarat, India. It was built at the total cost of ₹11 crore (US$1.2 million) and opened in October 2022.
