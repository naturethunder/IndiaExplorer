Sree Shivashakthi Mahaganapathi temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sree Shivashakthi Mahaganapathi temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, Devi Parvati and Lord Ganapathi.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chenkal — Chenkal is a village in Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India. Until the land-reforms ordinance enacted by the Communist regime in the 1950s, the village formed part of the estate of the jenmi (Yejamanan) of Kandamath.
  • Arayoor Sree Mahadevar — Arayoor Major Sree Mahadevar Temple (ആറയൂർ ശ്രീ മഹാദേവർ) is one of the major worship places in Arayoor in Thiruvananthapuram district, Kerala, very close to Tamil Nadu border. It is a Shiva temple.
  • Kulathoor, Thiruvananthapuram — Kulathoor, is a village in Neyyattinkara Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India.
  • Thirupuram — Thirupuram is a village in Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India. Thirupuram village is 6 km away from Neyyattinkara, 25 km away from the capital city Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Arumanoor — Arumanoor is a village on the banks of the river Meenachil in Kottayam district, Kerala, India. It is between the towns of Ettumanoor and Ayarkunnam, and has a population of 2,000.
  • Parasuvaikkal — Parasuvaikkal is a village in Thiruvananthapuram district in the state of Kerala, India. Parasuvaikkal has four Hindu temples, Parasuvaikkal major Sree Bhagavathy temple governed by Travancore Devaswom Board, Ponnamkulam Devi Kshethram, Idanatukonam Sree Dharma Sastha Temple and Kottakkakam Sree Mahadeva Kshethram Trust.
