Our Lady of Grace Church

State: Goa | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Our Lady of Grace Church is a Roman Catholic church in Chorão, Goa, India. It is bounded on its north and west by the Mapusa River, with Salvador do Mundo remaining exactly opposite to it. To the south is the river Mandovi with Ribandar opposite to it and to its east is the St Bartholomew's Church.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Real Colégio de Educação de Chorão — Real Colégio de Educação de Chorão is a defunct Catholic seminary that was founded on 2 April 1761 in Chorão, Portuguese Goa. It belonged to the parish of Our Lady of Grace Church.
  • Chapel of St. Jerome (Chorão Island) — The Chapel of St. Jerome is a historic Roman Catholic chapel on the island of Chorão in Goa, India.
  • Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary — Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary is an estuarine mangrove habitat, which is declared as the bird sanctuary, and located on western tip of the Island of Chorão along the Mandovi River, Goa, in India. The sanctuary is named after Salim Ali, the eminent Indian ornithologist.
  • Salvador do Mundo — Salvador do Mundo (Portuguese for Saviour of the World) is a village in Bardez taluka, Goa, in India. The village is located south west of Socorro and east of Porvorim.
  • Ribandar — Ribandar is a town in Tiswadi, Goa, located between the cities of Panjim (Nova Goa) and Old Goa (Velha Goa).
  • Epidemics in Chorão Island — The Chorão Epidemic killed over a thousand people in Chorão, an island along the Mandovi River near Ilhas, Goa, India. It also caused thousands to flee the island, and led to the closure of Real Colégio de Educação de Chorão in 1859.
