Karyat Sree Ayyappa Temple Vanimal

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Karyat Sree Ayyappa-Bhagavathi Temple is situated in Kallachi Vanimel road in Vanimal, kerala. The presiding deity in this temple is Lord Ayyappa. It was started as a small Bhajana Madam in the year 1987. The temple's ISIC codes are 9491 and SIC codes are 8661.
