Udupi Sri Krishna Matha

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Udupi Shri Krishna Temple is a well-known historic Hindu temple dedicated to Krishna and Dvaita Matha, located in the city of Udupi in Karnataka, India. The Matha area resembles a living ashram, a holy place for daily devotion and living. Surrounding the Shri Krishna Temple are several temples namely the Udupi Anantheshwara Temple which is over a thousand years old.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sri Krishna Temple & Kanakana Kindi — Historic 13th-century Krishna temple complex featuring the silver-plated Kanakana Kindi window, Madhwa Sarovara holy tank, and divine daily pujas.
  • Malpe Beach & Sea Walkway — Vibrant coastal beach with golden sands, modern sea walkway extending into the Arabian Sea, and scenic sunset vistas.
  • St. Mary's Islands — Geological national monument renowned for distinct hexagonal columnar basaltic lava rock formations formed millions of years ago in the Arabian Sea.
  • Kapu (Kaup) Beach & Historic Lighthouse — Picturesque sandy beach featuring a 100-foot British-era black-and-white stone lighthouse erected in 1901 upon a rocky promontory.
