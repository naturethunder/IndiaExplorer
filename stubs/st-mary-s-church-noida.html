St. Mary's Church, Noida

State: Uttar Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

St. Mary's Church is the first Catholic parish in the city of Noida, India and is dedicated to the Assumption of Our Lady. It comes under the Agra Diocese of the Roman Catholic Church. The parish lies on the north-western tip of Agra Diocese, bordering with the Delhi Diocese on the west and Meerut Diocese on the north.
