Polo Forest

State: Gujarat | Category: nature | Best Time: Aug – Feb

Polo Forest (Vijaynagar) is a dense green forest surrounding ancient 15th-century ruined temples. Flanked by the Aravalli hills and bisected by the Harnav river, Polo Forest features stone carved Jain and Shiva temple ruins overgrown with moss.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Sharaneshwar Shiva Temple Ruins — 15th-century stone temple with carved pillars in dense teak forest.
  • Harnav River Walk & Dam — Shallow jungle river stream ideal for forest wading and nature walks.
  • Vireshwar Temple & Sweet Spring — Ancient temple beside a perennial sweet water natural spring.
  • Lakhena Jain Temple Ruins — Detailed carved stone Jain temple ruins overgrown with green moss.
  • Idar Rock Formations & Fort — Towering granite boulders and hilltop fort citadel in Idar.
  • Vanaj Forest Reservoir — Calm green reservoir framed by Aravalli mountain ridges.
