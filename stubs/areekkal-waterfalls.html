Areekkal Waterfalls

State: Kerala | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Areekkal Waterfalls is located in Pampakuda around 35 kilometers away from the Ernakulam main city on the Thodupuzha- Ernakulam road.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Pampakuda — Pampakuda is a village and Panchayath part of Muvattupuzha Taluk. It lies between Muvattupuzha and Piravom towns.
  • Kakkoor — Kakkoor is a village situated in the eastern part of Ernakulam district in Kerala, south of India. Kakkoor is famously known for Kakkoor Kalavayal the agricultural fest conducted every year in the month of Kumbham in the Malayalam calendar.
  • Onakkoor — Onakkoor is a village in Ernakulam district in the Indian state of Kerala. The main attractions in the village are Onakkoor River and Onakkoor Temple.
  • Marady — Marady is a village in Ernakulam district in the Indian state of Kerala. Marady Grama Panchayath office is located in South Marady.
  • Ramamangalam — Ramamangalam is a village in Ernakulam district in the Indian state of Kerala, it lies between Kolenchery, Piravom and Muvattupuzha towns. This small town lies in the Muvattupuzha taluk.
  • Meenkunnam — Meenkunnam is a village with waterfalls, rocky terrains, small hills, and paddy fields. It is located within Arakuzha and Marady Panchayat, Kerala.
