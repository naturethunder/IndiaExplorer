Gurudwara Guptsar Sahib

State: Punjab | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Guptsar Sahib (Punjabi: ਗੁਪਤਸਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ) is a holy Gurdwara (English: Sikh shrine) located on the outskirts of Chhattiana village of Sri Muktsar Sahib district in Punjab, India.

  • Bhalaiana — Bhalaiana, sometimes spelled Bhallaiana is a village in the Giddarbaha tehsil of Sri Muktsar Sahib district in Eastern Punjab, India.
  • Buttar Sarinh — Buttar Sarinh , other spellings include Buttar Shrin and Buttar Shri, is a small village in the Giddarbaha tehsil of Sri Muktsar Sahib district in Punjab, India. It's located on the Sri Muktsar Sahib-Bathinda national Highway 754.
  • Doda, Punjab — Doda is a village-city in the Sri Muktsar Sahib district of Punjab, India.
  • Kauni, Sri Muktsar Sahib — Kaoni is a village in the Sri Muktsar Sahib district of Punjab, India. Its located 18km to east of Muktsar and 28km to North of Gidderbaha town.
