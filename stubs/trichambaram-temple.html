Trichambaram Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Trichambaram Temple is a Krishna temple located in Taliparamba, in the Kannur district of Kerala, South India. The main deity of the temple is Krishna after "Kamsavadham" sitting in His "Raudra" posture (ferocious posture). The Temple is among the Abhimana Kshethrams, which is a list of holy Vaishnavite shrines. The temple is also mentioned in the 11th century CE Sanskrit kāvya "Mūṣikavaṃśa".
