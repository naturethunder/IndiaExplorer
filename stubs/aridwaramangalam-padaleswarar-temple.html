Aridwaramangalam Padaleswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Aridwaramangalam Padaleswarar Temple (அரித்துவாரமங்கலம் பாதாளேஸ்வரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located at Aridwaramangalam in Tiruvarur district, Tamil Nadu, India. The temple is dedicated to Shiva, as the moolavar presiding deity, in his manifestation as Padaleswarar. His consort, Parvati, is known as Alankaravalli. The place is also known as Aradhai Perumpazhi.
