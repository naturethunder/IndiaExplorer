Kothaligad

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kothaligad (also called Kotligad/ Kothligad/Peth) is a small fort (3100 ft) situated to the east of Karjat near Karjat-Murbad Road in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is a famous trek in the Karjat area, because of its small height and easy climbing. It is also known as the Fort of Peth because of its vicinity to Peth village at its base.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ambivali Buddhist Caves — The Ambivali Caves (Ambivali Leni) are an ancient group of 31 rock-cut Buddhist caves cut into a concave hillside along the riverbank near Neral. Featuring historic chaityas, rock-cut pillars, and inscribed verandas dating to the 2nd century BCE.
  • Peth Base Village & Trek Trail — The scenic rural village of Peth serves as the primary base camp for the Kothaligad ascent. The trail winds through dense teakwood forests, emerald paddy fields, and natural mountain streams before ascending the plateau.
  • Bhimashankar Wildlife Sanctuary — A biodiverse wildlife sanctuary covering the crest of the Western Ghats near Karjat. Home to the elusive Indian Giant Squirrel (Shekru), rich endemic avian life, and pristine evergreen forest canopies.
  • Ulhas Valley & Karjat Waterfalls — A dramatic mountain gorge through which the Ulhas River flows, famous during the monsoon for cascading white-water rapids, sheer cliff vistas, and breathtaking Sahyadri trekking routes.
