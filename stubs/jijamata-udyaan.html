Jijamata Udyaan

State: Maharashtra | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Jijamata Udyaan, also known as Victoria Gardens and the Byculla Zoo, is a zoo and garden covering 50 acres located at Byculla, in the heart of Mumbai, India. It is the oldest public garden in Mumbai. After Indian independence, it was officially renamed after Jijamata, the mother of Shivaji, the first Maratha emperor; the original name remains popular. In 1835, the British administration granted a large plot of land in Sewri to the Agro Horticultural Society of Western India for a botanical garden known as Victoria Gardens, after Queen-Empress Victoria.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Byculla — Byculla (ISO: Bhāykhaḷā; pronunciation: [bʱaːjkʰəɭaː]) is an area of South Mumbai.
  • Jewish Cemetery, Chinchpokli — Jewish Cemetery, Chinchpokli, is a cemetery in Chinchpokli, Bombay, laid out near the Chinchpokli railway station by Elias David Sassoon in 1878. Covering two acres, the burial ground now contains more than a thousand graves, and new burials continue to take place.
  • Aga Khan's Maqbara — The Aga Khan's Maqbara or Aga Khan's Dargah is the mausoleum of Aga Khan I, located in Mazgaon, Mumbai, India. It is also known as the Hasanabad Monument since the personal name of Aga Khan I was Hasan.
  • Chinchpokli — Chinchpokli (Marathi: चिंचपोकळी) is a neighbourhood in South Mumbai. It is also the name of a railway station on the Central line of the Mumbai suburban railway.
  • Dagdi Chawl — Dagdi Chawl is a neighbourhood in Byculla, Mumbai, used to house mill workers from the nearby industries. Now it is known for the fortified home of mafia don turned politician Arun Gawli.
  • Khada Parsi — Khada Parsi, Ubha Parsi (transl. Standing Parsi) or Cursetjee Fountain is a Grade 1 heritage monument located in Byculla, Mumbai, India. Installed in 1867, the monument features a life-sized cast iron statue of Cursetjee Manockjee (19 August 1763 — 7 May 1845), a prominent Parsi businessman and philanthropist, atop a pillar and surrounded by mermaid fountains.
