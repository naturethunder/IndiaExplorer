Sidhhanath Temple, Kharsundi

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Siddhanath is believed to be incarnation of Lord Shiva. Siddhanath is the patron god of Atpadi and adjacent regions, and is one of several regional protective (Kshetrapal) gods of Maharashtra. Historical temple of lord Siddhnath, a big temple built in 450 years ago. The God in this temple is reincarnation of Lord Shiva even called as NathBaba.
