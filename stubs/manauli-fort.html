Manauli Fort

State: Punjab | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Manauli Fort, also known as Quila Manauli, is a fortress (qila) located in the middle of the Manauli hamlet in Mohali district, Punjab, India. It is 11 kilometres (7 mi) from Mohali city and Chandigarh and nearly 4 km away from International Chandigarh Airport Road Mohali. Manauli is a small hamlet with a population of 3,919 and 693 households (2011 Census). As per land record area of the village is 738 hectares (1,820 acres) (s.no.
