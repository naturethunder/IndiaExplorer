Arulmigu Koniamman Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Arulmigu Koniamman Temple, is a historic Hindu temple located on the northern bank of the Noyyal River of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. It is dedicated to the goddess Koniamman, a form of Parvati. The temple is at the center of the city of Coimbatore in the core of the city, Town Hall, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. Koniamman is the "Guardian Deity" of the city.

The mosque is situated approximately one kilometre (one thousand one hundred yards) from the Coimbatore Junction railway station.
