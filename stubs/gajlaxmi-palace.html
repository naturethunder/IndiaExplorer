Gajlaxmi palace

State: Odisha | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Dhenkanal Palace (Odia: ରାଜବାରି) is the official palace of the Bhoi dynasty of Dhenkanal princely state, India. Built by the Bhois of Dhenkanal, this palace is located in Dhenkanal town, Odisha. This palace was turned into a homestay in 1992.

  • Dhenkanal — Dhenkanal is a town and a municipality in Dhenkanal district in the state of Odisha, India.
  • Dhenkanal State — Dhenkanal State was one of the princely states of India during the period of the British Raj. The area of the former state is now referred to as Dhenkanal district, Odisha, with Dhenkanal town as its district headquarters.Dhenkanal state was ruled by several rulers from the year 1530 to 1948, meaning an astounding 418 years.Dhenkanal state had the major parts of Dhenkanal, Angul and some parts of Keonjhar and southern areas.
  • Tarava — Tarava (officially Tarabha), is a Gram panchayat in Odisha's Dhenkanal district. It is 7 km away from Dhenkanal Town.
  • Gohirakhol — Gohirakhol is a small village between Bhapur and Dhenkanal, India. It is about 7 km from Dhenkanal.
  • Janhitaila — Janhitaila is a village in Dhenkanal district, Odisha, India.
