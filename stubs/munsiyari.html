Munsiyari

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Munsiyari, in Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its panchachuli views, trekking base, glaciers, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Panchachuli Viewpoint — Front-row views of the five snow peaks of Panchachuli.
  • Khaliya Top — Alpine meadow trek with sweeping Himalayan vistas.
  • Birthi Falls — 126m waterfall on the road to Munsiyari.
