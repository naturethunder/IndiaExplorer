Srikanteshwara Temple, Nanjangud

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

The Nanjundeshwara Temple (also called Srikanteshwara Temple) is an ancient temple dedicated to Shiva in the Hindu pilgrimage town of Nanjanagudu in Karnataka, India. It is located on the right bank of the Kapila River (also known as the Kabini River), a tributary of the Kaveri River, a tributary of the Kaveri River. Nanjanagudu is also known as The "Dakshina Kashi" or "Kashi of South". Nanju in Kannada means "to poison".
