Rajmachi Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Rajmachi Fort (Killa) is a historical fort in the hills of the Sahyadri mountains (Western Ghats). It consists of two twin fortresses Shriwardhan and Manaranjan, with a wide machi (plateau) surrounding the two Balekillas. Udhewadi is a small village of about 60 households (as per 2011 census report) situated on the machi, at the southern foot of Manaranjan Balekilla of Rajmachi Fort.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kondana Caves — The Kondhana Caves are located in the small village of Kondhane, 33 km (21 mi) north of Lonavala and 16 km (9.9 mi) northwest of Karla Caves. This cave group has 16 Buddhist caves.
  • Somavadi — Somavadi is a village in India, situated in the Mawal taluka of Pune district in the state of Maharashtra. It encompasses an area of 317 ha (783 acres).
  • Kusavali — Kusavali is a village and gram panchayat in India, situated in Mawal taluka of Pune district in the state of Maharashtra. It encompasses an area of 366 ha (904 acres).
  • Jambhavali — Jambhavali is a village in India, situated in Mawal taluka of Pune district in the state of Maharashtra. It encompasses an area of 972 ha (2,402 acres).
  • Badhalawadi — Badhalawadi is a village in Mawal taluka of Pune district in the state of Maharashtra, India. It encompasses an area of 814.66 ha (2,013 acres).
  • Dhak Bahiri — Dhak Bahiri is a cave in the Sahyadri hill range, situated near the village Jambhivli off village-Malavali in the Pune district of India. It was used by pilgrims and sages for the pilgrimage to the god Bahiri.
