Bangaram Island

State: Lakshadweep | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – May

Bangaram is a teardrop-shaped uninhabited island in Lakshadweep surrounded by a shallow turquoise lagoon and protective coral reefs. Famous for calm glass-like waters, wreck diving, and bioluminescent plankton strolls at night.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bangaram Lagoon & Coral Reef — Crystal-clear glass-like lagoon ideal for kayaking, paddleboarding, and snorkeling.
  • Shipwreck Wreck Dive Site — Sunken cargo vessel teeming with sea turtles, stingrays, and vibrant tropical reef fish.
  • Tinnekara Island Sandbar — Neighboring uninhabited islet accessible by kayak or glass-bottom boat.
