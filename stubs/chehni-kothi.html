Chehni Kothi

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Chehni Kothi is a traditional Kath Kuni-style fortified tower with a temple inside, located in Chehni village, Banjar, Himachal Pradesh, India. The 350-year-old 12-storey-high structure is made in the "Kath Kuni" method (Kath meaning "wood", and Kuni, "corner"), using only stones and wood without cement or nails. It has a temple inside, but was originally built as a fort. The tower’s age is uncertain, but it is generally regarded as a several-century-old structure (often dated to the 17th century) and is one of the largest surviving wooden towers of its kind in the Western Himalayas.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Chehni Kothi Fort Tower — Massive 17th-century fortified defensive tower constructed by King Dhadhu of Kullu, standing as the tallest indigenous structure in the Western Himalayas.
  • Shringi Rishi Temple (Bagi) — Magnificent multi-tiered pagoda temple dedicated to sage Shringi Rishi, the patron deity of the Banjar valley, showcasing ornate wood carvings.
  • Jalori Pass & Serolsar Lake — Majestic high pass at 10,800 feet connecting Kullu and Shimla districts, with a pristine forest trek leading to sacred Serolsar Lake surrounded by oak trees.
