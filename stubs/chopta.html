Chopta

State: Uttarakhand | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov

Chopta, in Garhwal, Uttarakhand, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its mini switzerland, tungnath, meadows, with the best months being Apr – Jun, Sep – Nov.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tungnath Temple — Highest Shiva temple in the world at 3,680m.
  • Chandrashila Summit — Trek peak with 360° Himalayan views incl. Nanda Devi.
  • Deoria Tal — Emerald lake reflecting the Chaukhamba peaks.
