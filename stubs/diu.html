Diu

State: Daman & Diu | Category: beach | Best Time: Oct – May

Diu, in Kathiawar Coast, Daman & Diu, is one of India’s favourite beach town. Visitors come for its portuguese fort, quiet beaches, seafood, with the best months being Oct – May.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Diu Fort — Sprawling 1535 Portuguese sea fort with a lighthouse.
  • Nagoa Beach — Horseshoe palm-fringed beach, the island’s best for swimming.
  • St. Paul’s Church — Baroque 1610 church, one of the oldest in the region.
