St. Joseph's Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Chennamkary

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Chennamkary St.Joseph's Syro-Malabar Catholic Church is a Catholic church in Kerala. It belongs to the Syro-Malabar Church under the Archeparchy of Changanacherry.
