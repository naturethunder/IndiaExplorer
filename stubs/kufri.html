Kufri

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Dec – Feb

Kufri, in Shimla, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its snow, skiing, himalayan zoo, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Dec – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kufri Fun World — Amusement park with the world’s highest go-kart track.
  • Himalayan Nature Park — Zoo of snow leopards, musk deer and Himalayan monals.
  • Mahasu Peak — Pony-ride viewpoint over snow ranges, top sledging spot.
