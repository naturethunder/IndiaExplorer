Siddhivinayak Temple, Mumbai

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

The Shri Siddhivinayak Ganapati Mandir is a Hindu temple dedicated to Ganesha. It is located in Prabhadevi neighbourhood of Mumbai, Maharashtra. It was originally built by Laxman Vithu and Deubai Patil on 19 November 1801. It is one of the most popular Hindu temples in Mumbai.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Prabhadevi & Shree Siddhivinayak Mandir — The iconic Shree Siddhivinayak Ganapati Mandir in Prabhadevi is one of the most revered and visited Hindu temples in India, dedicated to Lord Ganesha.
  • Bandra-Worli Sea Link & Promenade — The Bandra-Worli Sea Link is an iconic cable-stayed bridge spanning the Arabian Sea, offering panoramic coastal views and connecting Prabhadevi and Worli with the western suburbs.
  • Shivaji Park, Dadar — Located just north of Prabhadevi, Shivaji Park is Mumbai's largest public park and a historic civic landmark established in 1925, home to open sports grounds and the statue of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.
  • Portuguese Church (Our Lady of Salvation) — The Church of Our Lady of Salvation, popularly referred to as Portuguese Church, is one of the oldest churches in Mumbai dating back to 1596, located on S. K. Bole Road in Dadar.
  • Chaitya Bhoomi & Dadar Beach — Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Chaitya Bhoomi Memorial is a revered Buddhist monument and cremation place of the architect of the Indian Constitution, situated right along Dadar Chowpatty beach.
