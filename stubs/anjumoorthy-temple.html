Anjumoorthy Temple

State: Kerala | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Anjumoorthy Temple is a Hindu temple at Anjumoorthy neighbourhood in Palakkad district, Kerala in India. It is one of the 108 Shiva Temples in India.
