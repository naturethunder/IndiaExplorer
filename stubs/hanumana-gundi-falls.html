Hanumana Gundi Falls

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Hanumana Gundi Falls, also known as Suthanabbe Falls or Soothanabbi Falls, is located in the hilly surroundings of the Kudremukh National Park in the Chikkamagaluru district of Karnataka, India. Hanumanagundi Falls is located between Karkala and Lakya Dam in the Kudremukh national park. Hanumanagundi Falls has an elevation of 996 m (3,268 ft). The water falls from a height of 22 m (72 ft) and is a tiered waterfall.
