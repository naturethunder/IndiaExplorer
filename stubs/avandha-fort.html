Avandha Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Avandha Fort, or Avandha Killa (Marathi: औंढ़ा/ अवंढा किल्ला), is a fort situated in Aundhewadi village between Nasik and Ahmadnagar in Maharashtra. This fort is close to Patta Fort. The residents of Patta Killa are known as Pattekar, meaning "residents of Fort Patta".

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kadwa Dam — Kadwa Dam, is an earthfill dam on Kadwa river near Igatpuri, Nashik district in state of Maharashtra in India.
  • Bharvir Khurd — Bharvir Khurd is a village of Igatpuri Taluka in Nashik District in the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is located near the Kadwa Reservoir.
  • Pandhurli — Pandhurli is a village and a municipal council in Sinnar taluka of Nashik district in the Indian state of Maharashtra.
  • Lake Beale — Lake Beale is a lake located in Nashik District, in Maharashtra, India. The estimate terrain elevation above sea level is 547 metres.
