Kawal Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Telangana | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Kawal Wildlife Sanctuary, designated as the Kawal Tiger Reserve, is a pristine wildlife sanctuary situated in Jannaram mandal of Mancherial district in northern Telangana. Nestled in the catchment basin of the Godavari and Kadam rivers, this expansive protected reserve of over 890 square kilometres forms one of the richest dry deciduous teak forest ecosystems in the Deccan Peninsula. Providing a vital habitat and migration corridor for Bengal tigers, leopards, Indian gaurs, nilgai, sambar deer, and over 300 avian species, Kawal offers nature lovers an authentic wilderness experience.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jannaram Forest Gateway & Interpretation Centre — The primary tourism gateway into Kawal Tiger Reserve. It houses the wildlife interpretation centre, forest safari boarding point, and Haritha forest resort eco-cottages.
  • Kadam Dam & Reservoir — Constructed across the Kadam River tributary of Godavari, this picturesque masonry dam is enclosed by verdant forest hills, offering boating, sunset views, and scenic picnic grounds.
  • Godavari River Basin at Mancherial — The life-giving Godavari River flows majestically through the southern border of the sanctuary landscape, nourishing riverine forests and providing scenic rocky sandbars.
  • Gandhari Vanam & Nature Park — A sprawling eco-park and deer reserve featuring walking nature trails, medicinal plant enclosures, and the historic remains of the medieval Gandhari Fort atop a nearby hillock.
  • Kuntala Waterfalls (Western Adilabad) — Telangana's highest waterfall, where the Kadam River cascades 147 feet down red sandstone cliffs in two distinct tiers, enclosed by the Sahyadri mountain ranges.
