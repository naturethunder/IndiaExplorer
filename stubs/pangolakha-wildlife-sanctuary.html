Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Sikkim | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Pangolakha Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary encompassing an area of 124 km2 (48 sq mi) in the Pakyong District of the Indian state of Sikkim. It was established in 2002 and includes the hamlets of Aritar, Dakline Lingtam, Phadamchen, Dzuluk, Gnathang Monastery Kupup. It is about 28 km (17 mi) east of Rorathang and about 40 km (25 mi) by road from Rangpo city.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Nathang Valley — Nathang Valley (Gnathang) is a breathtaking high-altitude Himalayan valley situated at 4,300 meters along the Old Silk Route. Known as the Ladakh of the East, it features rolling alpine meadows and panoramic vistas.
  • Kupup (Elephant Lake) — Kupup Lake, affectionately called Elephant Lake (Bitan Cho) for its distinctive elephantine contour, is a sacred glacial lake at an elevation of nearly 13,000 feet surrounded by rocky Himalayan heights.
  • Zuluk (Dzuluk) — Zuluk is a historic Silk Route settlement perched at 9,400 feet, celebrated for its legendary 32 hairpin loops ascending toward Thambi View Point with dramatic sunrise panoramas over Mount Kangchenjunga.
  • Aritar Lake (Lampokhari) — Lampokhari Lake in Aritar is one of the oldest natural freshwater lakes in Sikkim, shaped like a pristine green boot and nestled amid verdant pine slopes at the base of Pangolakha ridge.
