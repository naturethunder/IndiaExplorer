San Thome Basilica

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

San Thome Church, officially known as the National Shrine of St. Thomas Cathedral Basilica, is a church of the Catholic Church in India, at the Santhome neighbourhood of Chennai, in Tamil Nadu. It is the cathedral of the Archdiocese of Madras-Mylapore, and a minor basilica dedicated to Saint Thomas the Apostle. The present structure dates back to 1523 AD, when it was built by the Portuguese over the tomb of Thomas.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Archdiocese of Madras and Mylapore — The Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Madras and Mylapore (Latin: Madraspolitan(us) et Meliaporen(sis)) is an archdiocese based in the city of Madras (now Chennai), in India. It merged with the ancient diocese of Mylapore which now a part of Chennai.
  • San Thome Museum & Crypt — Historic museum housing ancient artifacts and sacred relics associated with Saint Thomas the Apostle.
  • Santhome — Santhome, also written San Thome, is a locality in Mylapore in Chennai city (formerly Madras) in India. It was a Portuguese settlement in the 16th century.
  • Foreshore Estate — Foreshore Estate (Tamil: பட்டினப்பாக்கம் Pattinapakkam) is a neighbourhood in Chennai, India. It is situated along the southern stretch of the Marina Beach.
  • Kutchery Road metro station — Kutchery Road is an upcoming underground metro station on the East-West Corridor of the Yellow Line of Chennai Metro in Chennai, India. This metro station will be among the 30 stations of Corridor IV and 12 underground stations along Corridor IV of the Chennai Metro, Poonamallee Bypass–Lighthouse stretch.
  • Virupakshiswarar Temple, Mylapore — Virupakshiswarar Temple is a Hindu temple located in Mylapore in Chennai, India. It is dedicated to Shiva.
