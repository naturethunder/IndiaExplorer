Jaisalmer

State: Rajasthan | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Jaisalmer's golden sandstone fort rises dramatically from the flat Thar Desert. It is one of India's finest UNESCO-listed forts and, uniquely, still inhabited with hotels, shops and temples inside. The Sam Sand Dunes offer classic Rajasthani camel safari sunsets.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Jaisalmer Fort — UNESCO living fort. 99 bastions, maze of lanes, Jain temples. 1 in 4 residents live inside.
  • Sam Sand Dunes — Classic Rajasthani camel safari at sunset. Overnight desert camps available.
  • Patwon Ki Haveli — Five interconnected havelis with extraordinary jaali stonework. 19th century.
  • Gadisar Lake — 14th century reservoir with carved ghats, temples and boat rides. Magical at sunrise.
  • Kuldhara Ghost Village — Abandoned 13th century Paliwal village. Entire settlement deserted overnight in 1825.
  • Desert National Park — Home of the Great Indian Bustard and black-naped hares. Best jeep safari from Jaisalmer.
