Orchha

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Orchha, in Bundelkhand, Madhya Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its cenotaphs, palaces, betwa river, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Orchha Fort Complex — Jahangir Mahal and Raj Mahal palaces on a Betwa island.
  • Chhatris (Cenotaphs) — 14 riverside royal memorials, stunning at sunset.
  • Ram Raja Temple — Unique temple where Lord Rama is worshipped as a king.
