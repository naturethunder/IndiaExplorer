Huligina Maradi Temple

State: Karnataka | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Huligina Maradi is a hill located in the Gundlupete Taluk, Chamarajanagar district of the state of Karnataka. It has a temple dedicated to Lord Srinivasa. It is also known as Huligadri, Dakshina Seshadri and Vyagrachala. This place is popularly known as "Dakshina Sheshadri", as narrated in ‘Srimad Varaha Purana’.
