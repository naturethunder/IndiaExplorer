Tirundudevankudi Karkadeswarar Temple

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Tirundudevankudi Karkadeswarar Temple(திருந்துதேவன்குடி கற்கடகேசுவரர் கோயில்) is a Hindu temple located at Tirundudevankudi in Thanjavur district of Tamil Nadu, India. The historical name of the place is Karkteeswaram and Nandankoil. The presiding deity is Shiva. He is called as Karkadeswarar.
