Sonanadi Wildlife Sanctuary

State: Uttarakhand | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Sonanadi Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area in the Pauri Garhwal district of Uttarakhand, India. Established in 1987, it encompasses an area of 301.18 km² and serves as a vital component of the Corbett Tiger Reserve. The sanctuary is named after the Sonanadi River (meaning "River of Gold"), which flows through the sanctuary.
