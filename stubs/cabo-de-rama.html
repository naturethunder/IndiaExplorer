Cabo de Rama

State: Goa | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Praça do Cabo de Rama, also known as Cabo de Rama Fort, is a medieval hillfort located in Khola village, on the coast of Canacona, Goa in India.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Khola, Goa — Khola is a village located in Canacona, Goa, India. "Cola" is the variation of the same name.
  • Betul, Goa — Betul is a coastal village located in the Quepem taluka, South Goa district, Goa, India. It is the southernmost village of Quepem and is well-known for its beach, lighthouse, and fort.
  • Baradi, Goa — Baradi (Konkani: Barade) is a scenic neighborhood located within the village of Velim, Goa, neighbouring Tollecanto and Betul. It is situated in the Salcete taluka and falls under the South Goa district, in the Indian coastal state of Goa.
  • Mobor — Mobor is a town in South Goa in the state of Goa, India.
  • Cutbona — Cutbona (Konkani: Kutbonn) is a neighborhood located within the Velim village, neighbouring Betul. It is situated in the Salcete taluka and falls under the South Goa district, in the Indian coastal state of Goa.
  • Fatorpa — Fatorpa is a village in Quepem taluk, Kushavati district, Goa. There are two famous temples of Shree Shantadurga in this Village.
