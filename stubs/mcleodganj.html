McLeod Ganj

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

McLeod Ganj, the seat of the Tibetan government-in-exile, is a small hill town above Dharamsala. With its Buddhist monasteries, Tibetan cafes, meditation centres and the dramatic backdrop of the Dhauladhar range, it is one of India's most unique cultural destinations.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tsuglagkhang Complex — Main Tibetan temple with a large Shakyamuni Buddha. Tibetan Museum inside.
  • Triund Trek — Most popular day trek in Himachal. 360° views of Dhauladhar and Kangra Valley.
  • Bhagsu Waterfall — Short hike to a small but scenic waterfall. Cafes and rocks for relaxing.
  • Dal Lake Dharamsala — Small cedar-fringed lake in upper Dharamsala. Far more tranquil than its Srinagar namesake.
  • Namgyal Monastery — Personal monastery of the Dalai Lama. Monks in debate; prayer flags all around.
  • Kangra Fort — One of India's oldest forts. Hilltop panoramas of Kangra and Beas valleys.
