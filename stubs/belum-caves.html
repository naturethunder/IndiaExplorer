Belum Caves

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: heritage | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Belum Caves are a limestone cave system located in Nandyal district of Andhra Pradesh, India. Extending over 3.23 km (2.01 mi), it is the second-largest cave network in the country. Archaeological evidence indicates human activity in the caves as early as 4500 BCE, and the site was later used by Buddhist and Jain ascetics. The caves were formed by persistent hyrdological erosion during wetter climatic conditions that existed during the Pre-Pliocene period.
