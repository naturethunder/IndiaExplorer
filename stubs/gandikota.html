Gandikota

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: nature | Best Time: Sep – Mar

Gandikota is a small village in Kadapa known as the Grand Canyon of India due to its breathtaking gorge. Carved out by the Pennar river cutting through the Erramala hills, the massive red granite gorge and the 13th-century Gandikota fort offer unreal views and cliff camping experiences.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Gandikota Fort & Pennar Gorge Viewpoint — Spectacular red granite river gorge over Pennar river dubbed the Grand Canyon of India.
  • Madhavaraya Swamy Temple — 15th-century Vijayanagara style stone temple with carved gopuram and granite pillars.
  • Jamia Masjid Gandikota — Medieval Islamic architecture with twin minarets positioned adjacent to the fort wall.
  • Raghunathaswamy Temple — Ancient red granite temple complex inside fort walls with intricate stone carvings.
  • Gandikota Granary (Rayanachervu) — Ancient stone grain storage facility used during Vijayanagara reign.
  • Mylavaram Dam & Reservoir — Serene blue water dam reservoir over Pennar river offering sunset views.
