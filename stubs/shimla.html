Shimla

State: Himachal Pradesh | Category: hill_station | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Dec – Jan

Shimla, in Lower Himalayas, Himachal Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite hill station. Visitors come for its mall road, toy train, ridge, colonial, with the best months being Mar – Jun, Dec – Jan.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • The Ridge & Mall Road — Open promenade with colonial buildings, cafes and Christ Church.
  • Jakhoo Temple — Hilltop Hanuman temple with a 33m statue and resident monkeys.
  • Kalka–Shimla Toy Train — UNESCO narrow-gauge railway through 102 tunnels.
