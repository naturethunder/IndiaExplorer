Mumba Devi Temple

State: Maharashtra | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Mumba Devi Temple is a Hindu temple in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, dedicated to the goddess Mumbā, the local incarnation of the Parvati (Mother goddess). Mumba Devi is the goddess of the city of Mumbai. The name Mumbai is derived from Mumba Devi. The creek and fort are now deteriorated to a point at which they are but derelict reminders of the city's past.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Bhuleshwar — Bhuleshwar (Old spelling Bholeśvar) is a neighbourhood in Mumbai. It is situated in South Mumbai and to the north of the Fort area.
  • Zaveri Bazaar — Zaveri Bazaar is a jewellery market and a major hub for B2B and B2C jewellery industry in Mumbai, India. Located at Bhuleshwar in South Mumbai, just north of Crawford Market, Zaveri Bazaar is a muddle of narrow lanes, dotted with hundreds of jewellery shops that sell gems and jewels, notably Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri (TBZ), Dwarkadas Chandumal, Dhirajlal Bhimji Zaveri & UTZ. 65% of all gold trading and dealing in India is estimated to originate from the market.
  • Jama Mosque, Mumbai — The Jama Masjid (Marathi: जामा मशीद) is a Sunni Mosque, located in the Kalbadevi, near Crawford Market South Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. The mosque was completed in stages during the 19th century, in the Indo-Islamic architecture.
  • Mandvi, Mumbai — Mandvi is an area in South Mumbai near Masjid Bunder. It is located near Pydhone.
  • Dava Bazaar — Dava Bazaar (also spelled Dawa Bazaar and Dava Bazar) is an area in South Mumbai famous for medical and scientific instruments, and lab chemicals. It is located near Lohar Chawl, Crawford Market and opens into Princess Street.
  • Cavel — Cavel is a neighbourhood in South Mumbai. It is located to the northeast of Dhobitalao near the Chira Bazaar area.
