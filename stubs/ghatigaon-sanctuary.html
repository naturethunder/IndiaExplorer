Ghatigaon Sanctuary

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ghatigaon Wildlife Sanctuary is a wildlife sanctuary situated at Ghatigaon in India. Ghatigaon covers 511.0 km² in Madhya Pradesh state, west of the city of Gwalior. It extends to Tigra Dam, and includes part of the reservoir. It was established in 1981 to protect habitat of the great Indian bustard (Ardeotis nigriceps).

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ghatigaon — Ghatigaon (Gháṭígaon) is a village and corresponding community development block in Gwalior district of Madhya Pradesh, India. As of 2011, the village population was 5,641, in 1,088 households.
