Solomon's Temple, Aizawl Mizoram, India

State: Mizoram | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Solomon's Temple is a church located in Aizawl, Mizoram, India. The temple was constructed by the religious group Kohhran Thianghlim, translated as 'The Holy Church' in English. It was founded by Dr. L.B. Sailo in 1984 and is non-denominational.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Kohhran Thianghlim — Kohhran Thianghlim (The Holy Church) is the non-denominational Christian fellowship in Mizoram that envisioned and constructed Solomon's Temple at Chawlhhmun.
  • Federation Enclave — A scenic elevated residential and government sector in Aizawl offering panoramic viewpoints of the surrounding mountain ranges and terraced valleys.
  • Mizo Conference of Seventh-day Adventist — The historic headquarters and church mission centre of the Seventh-day Adventist church in Mizoram, established on the quiet wooded hills of Aizawl.
  • Mizoram State Museum — Located at McDonald Hill, this renowned ethnographic museum houses an invaluable collection of traditional Mizo textiles, weapons, musical instruments, and tribal heritage artifacts.
  • Aizwal Mosque — A prominent Islamic place of worship in the heart of Aizawl, featuring elegant dome architecture nestled harmoniously on the hillsides of the capital.
  • Lammual Stadium — Also known as the Assam Rifles Ground, Lammual is the historic sports arena in the center of Aizawl, celebrated for passionate Mizo football culture and Chapchar Kut celebrations.
