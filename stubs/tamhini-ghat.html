Tamhini Ghat

State: Maharashtra | Category: nature | Best Time: Jul – Nov

Tamhini Ghat is a mountain pass located between Mulshi and Kolad in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Mulshi Dam Lake Backwaters — Vast calm lake backwaters surrounded by green Sahyadri hills.
  • Plus Valley Viewpoint & Canyon Trek — Plus-shaped canyon gorge offering monsoon cloud mist views.
  • Tamhini Cascading Waterfalls — Hundreds of temporary monsoon waterfalls tumbling down roadside cliffs into green valleys.
  • Kolad White Water Rafting (Kundalika) — Only year-round white water rafting spot in Maharashtra on Kundalika river.
  • Andharban Dark Forest Trek Trailhead — Dense mist-covered rainforest canopy trek through Sahyadri ridges.
  • Devkund Plunge Pool Waterfall — Pristine blue plunge pool waterfall deep in the Western Ghats jungle.
