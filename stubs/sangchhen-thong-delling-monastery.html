Sangchhen Thong Delling monastery

State: West Bengal | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Ging Gompa (also known as Sangchen Thongdrol Ling) (Tibetan: གསང་ཆེན་མཐོང་གྲོལ་གླིང) is a Buddhist monastery in Darjeeling, West Bengal, India. The monastery is located in Ging, about 10 km (6.2 mi) from Darjeeling. It is one of the oldest monasteries in Darjeeling and subscribes to the Nyingmapa tradition of Tibetan Buddhism. On historical grounds, the monastery is still under the administrative control of the Government of Sikkim.
