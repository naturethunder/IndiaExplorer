Sessa Orchid Sanctuary

State: Arunachal Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov

Spanning over 100 square kilometers across the steep Bhalukpong-Tenga mountain ranges, Sessa Orchid Sanctuary is India's first protected area dedicated to preserving native orchids. The sanctuary features deep river gorges, misty subtropical valleys, and lush tree fern canopies that provide ideal microclimates for exotic epiphytic and terrestrial orchid species.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Singchung — Singchung is a scenic mountain valley town in West Kameng, renowned for the Singchung Bugun Village Community Reserve which protects rare endemic avian species like the Bugun Liocichla amidst pristine montane forests.
