Indori Fort

State: Maharashtra | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Indori fort also known as Induri Fort (Marathi: इन्दुरी किल्ला)is one of the many Land forts of Maharashtra state in India. Situated close to the hill station Lonavala and 35 km (22 mi) north of Pune, Indori fort rises to an elevation of 1,033 m (3,389 ft) above sea level. The fort is located on the banks of Indrayani River. The fort was under the Maratha Empire for the majority of the time.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Ghorawadi Caves — Ancient 3rd-century Buddhist rock-cut caves and rock-carved Shiva sanctum nestled on a high hill overlooking the old Mumbai-Pune highway.
  • Prati Shirdi Temple, Shirgaon — Renowned temple complex modeled as an authentic replica of the sacred shrine in Shirdi, surrounded by ornate sanctums and peaceful gardens.
  • Talegaon Dabhade Heritage Lake — Historic 18th-century stone reservoir built by the noble Dabhade family (Senapati of the Maratha Empire), fringed by ancient trees and heritage temples.
  • Bhandara Dongar — Sacred hill overlooking the Indrayani river valley where saint-poet Sant Tukaram meditated, offering breathtaking panoramic vistas of the Sahyadri ranges.
  • Dehu Gatha Mandir — Venerated pilgrimage complex constructed on the holy banks of the Indrayani River, enshrining the sacred abhangas carved in stone.
  • Lohagad Fort — Majestic Maratha hill fortress towering 1,033 meters above sea level, famed for its formidable stone bastions and the iconic Vinchukata spur.
