Sanwariaji Temple

State: Rajasthan | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Sanwariaji, officially called Mandaphia, is a sacred Hindu temple town in the Chittorgarh district of Rajasthan, renowned as the seat of Shri Sanwaliya Seth — a beloved manifestation of Lord Krishna as a dark (sanwala) divine companion. The town has grown organically around its grand temple complex, which houses three separate shrines dedicated to Sanwaliya Seth Ji, Sanwaliya Bihari Ji, and Sanwaliya Lala Ji. Beyond the main temple, the Sanwariaji area includes Govardhan Ghat along a seasonal stream, bazaars selling traditional prasad, silver ornaments, and devotional items, and several smaller Vaishnava shrines. The temple town draws pilgrims from across Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat, especially during the Kartik Purnima Mela, Ekadashi festivals, and Janmashtami celebrations. Sanwariaji is well-connected by road to Chittorgarh city, Udaipur, and Kota.
