Veeramakaliamman Temple, Arantangi

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Veeramakaliamman Temple, Arantangi is a sacred temple located in Tamil Nadu, India. Visitors come here for temples, pilgrimage and sacred theertham. Nearby places worth exploring include Aranthangi, Aranthangi taluk and Aliyanilai. The best time to visit is Nov – Feb.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Aranthangi — Aranthangi is a town in Pudukkottai district in the state of Tamil Nadu, India. As of 2011, it had a population of 40,814.
  • Aranthangi taluk — Ara(m)nthangi taluk is a taluk of Pudukkottai district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.
  • Aliyanilai — Azhiyanilai is a village in the Aranthangi revenue block of Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Anjaneya Temple, Azhiyanilai — Anjaneya Temple at Azhiyanilai, near Arantangi, Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India, is a Hindu temple dedicated to the god Hanuman.
  • Maramadakki — Maramadakki is a village in the Aranthangi revenue block of Pudukkottai district, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Vanniyan Viduthy — Vanniyan Viduthy is a village located in the Pudukkottai District of South Tamil Nadu, India and Vanniyan Viduthy village is part of Arayappatti Panchayat (Village Panchayat) . The village is located 07–10 km from Alangudi, Vadakadu, Silattur and Avanathankottai.
