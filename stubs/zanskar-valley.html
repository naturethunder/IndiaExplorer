Zanskar Valley

State: Ladakh | Category: adventure | Best Time: Jun – Sep, Jan – Feb

Zanskar Valley is a semi-arid high altitude desert valley in Ladakh surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Renowned for the honeycomb-like Phugtal Gompa built into a cliff cave entrance and the winter Chadar trek over the frozen Zanskar river.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Phugtal Cave Monastery (Phuktal Gompa) — Cliffside honeycomb cave monastery built into a natural limestone cliff face.
  • Drang Drung Glacier & Pensi La Pass — Massive winding ice river glacier viewed from high altitude Pensi La pass (4,400m).
  • Karsha Gompa Padum — Largest Gelugpa monastery in Zanskar overlooking the entire Padum valley floor.
  • Sani Monastery & Ancient Stupa — Ancient monastery associated with Emperor Kanishka housing Kanishka Chorten.
  • Zangla Fort & Palace Ruins — Hilltop fort citadel where Csoma de Koros compiled the first Tibetan-English dictionary.
  • Stongdey Monastery Cliff — Perched cliff monastery housing 11 temples with ancient Buddhist wall murals.
