Bandhavgarh

State: Madhya Pradesh | Category: wildlife | Best Time: Oct – Jun

Bandhavgarh, in Umaria, Madhya Pradesh, is one of India’s favourite wildlife reserve. Visitors come for its highest tiger density, fort, safari, with the best months being Oct – Jun.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Tala Zone Safari — Core zone with India’s highest density of Bengal tigers.
  • Bandhavgarh Fort — 2,000-year-old hill fort with ancient Vishnu statues.
  • Three Cave Point — Prime spot to glimpse tigers resting in rock shelters.
