Badami

State: Karnataka | Category: heritage | Best Time: Oct – Mar

Badami, in Bagalkot, Karnataka, is one of India’s favourite heritage city. Visitors come for its cave temples, chalukya, sandstone, with the best months being Oct – Mar.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Badami Cave Temples — 6th-century rock-cut Hindu and Jain caves above Agastya lake.
  • Bhutanatha Temples — Sandstone temple group on the edge of the lake.
  • Pattadakal — UNESCO complex of Chalukyan temples, a cradle of Indian architecture.
