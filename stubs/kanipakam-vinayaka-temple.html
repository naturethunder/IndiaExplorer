Kanipakam Vinayaka Temple

State: Andhra Pradesh | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Sri Varasidhi Vinayaka Swamy Temple is a Hindu temple of Ganesha. It is located at Kanipakam in Chittoor district of Andhra Pradesh, India. The temple is about 11 km from Chittoor and 68 km from Tirupati.

Top Attractions & Places to Visit

  • Aragonda Sanjeevi Hills — Aragonda is an ancient village nestled among the Sanjeevi granite hills in Chittoor district. According to local folklore, a portion of the mythical Sanjeevani hill dropped here during the Ramayana era.
  • Koundinya Wildlife Sanctuary — Koundinya Wildlife Sanctuary is the only elephant reserve in Andhra Pradesh, featuring dry deciduous forests, rugged granite valleys, and winding streams in the Eastern Ghats.
  • Mogili Mogileeswara Temple — Mogili Mogileeswara Temple is an ancient 10th-century shrine dedicated to Lord Shiva, famous for its perennial natural spring water flowing beneath the stone Nandi idol.
  • Horsley Hills (Yenugu Mallamma Konda) — Horsley Hills is Andhra Pradesh's premier hill station, rising to 4,150 feet in Chittoor district with cool mountain breezes, fragrant eucalyptus plantations, and panoramic valley viewpoints.
