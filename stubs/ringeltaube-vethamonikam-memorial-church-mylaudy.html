Ringeltaube Vethamonikam Memorial Church, Mylaudy

State: Tamil Nadu | Category: spiritual | Best Time: Nov – Feb

Ringeltaube Vethamonikam Memorial Church, (Tamil: றிங்கல் தௌபே வேதமாணிக்கம் நினைவு தலைமை தேவாலயம், மயிலாடி) is a church located in Mylaudy, in the Kanyakumari district of Tamil Nadu, India. It is one of the oldest and largest churches in the region and serves as the cathedral of the Kanyakumari Diocese of the Church of South India (CSI). The church has a history of over 200 years. On 1 May 1996, the church was designated as a District Church.
